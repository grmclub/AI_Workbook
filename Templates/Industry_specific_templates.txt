Project Management Templates by Industry

##Construction

Tracking crews, site locations, permits, and project timelines is key in construction. A clear, structured template helps organize tasks by phase and location. Use the example below to assign work, monitor progress, and keep everything on schedule.

Site Name
Task Description
Assigned Crew
Start Date
End Date
Permit Status
Notes

##Manufacturing

Manufacturing projects revolve around timing, machine capacity, and production flow. A template for project management in this space should highlight key stages, shift schedules, and areas where delays might happen. The example below helps manage operations and prevent bottlenecks.

Product Line
Stage
Machine
Shift
Start Time
End Time
Status
Notes

##Field Services

For field teams, timing and location are everything. Coordinators need a clear view of who’s going where, when, and with what equipment. This template helps schedule technicians, assign jobs, and tighten service windows.

Technician
Service Call
Location
Time Window
Equipment Needed
Status
Notes


##Human Resources

HR teams handle a range of processes, from welcoming new hires to tracking time off and managing performance. A good template simplifies this work and keeps everything in one place. Here’s an example that covers the essentials.

Employee Name	|Process Step	|Assigned HR Lead	|Due Date	|Status	 |Notes
