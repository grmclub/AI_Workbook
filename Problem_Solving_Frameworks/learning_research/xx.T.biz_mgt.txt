30 Years Of Brutally Honest Advice For Consultants In 18 Minutes | Russell Raath

In this video, Russell Raath distills three decades of consulting experience into key, unfiltered operational principles for independent consultants and advisors.
Key Insights & Lessons Covered

    Why Likability Trumps Raw Intelligence

        The Reality: Technical expertise and raw IQ get you into the room, but rapport, warmth, and relational alignment determine whether proposals get signed and engagements get renewed. Clients buy outcomes from people they trust and enjoy working with.

    The Dangers of Winging an Answer

        The Trap: When a client asks a question you don't know the answer to, guessing or faking certainty destroys executive credibility instantly.

        The Principle: Professional rigor means having the discipline to say, "I don't know, but I will find out and follow up." C-suite stakeholders respect clarity and honesty over improvised answers.

    Understanding Mutual Buyer Psychology

        Re-framing sales interactions from "pitching" to a joint discovery process. Understanding the underlying emotional, organizational, and political risks a buyer faces allows you to structure engagements that address their real concerns, not just technical specs.

    Stamina as a Competitive Skill

        High-level consulting requires exceptional mental resilience and operational stamina. Navigating project friction, complex stakeholder alignment, and business development demands consistency over long cycles.

Key Frameworks & Lessons Covered

    What Consulting Truly Is

        The Reality: Consulting isn't about bragging about credentials, academic degrees, or corporate titles—it's strictly about solving tangible, high-value problems for paying clients.

    The Offer Formula & Demand Validation

        Positioning: Package your existing skills into a focused, outcome-driven offer rather than selling broad, generic advisory services.

    Niching Down & Ideal Client Profiles

        Avoid targeting "everyone." Clearly define your ideal client persona and niche down to target businesses that genuinely have the budget and urgency to pay for your expertise.

    Service Models & Pricing Strategies

        Delivery Models: Choose between Done-With-You (DWY), Done-For-You (DFY), or advisory (DIY) formats depending on operational capacity.

        Pricing Structure: Move away from low-margin hourly billing toward value-based or tiered retainers to capture full profit margin.

    Direct Outreach & Sales Pipeline

        Leverage your existing warm network first to land your initial 1–3 anchor clients using targeted outreach worksheets and simple CRM tracking, avoiding expensive paid ads early on.

    Operations, Back Office & Tech Stack

        Implement lightweight operational templates, master process checklists, and AI tools to streamline client onboarding, delivery, and ongoing project management.
