##6 Frameworks every Researcher should Master to write High-Quality Papers in 2026
##Great acronym methods of research framework

1.Abstract- IMRaD Framework
(Introduction – Method – Results – and Discussion)
🔹 Purpose
Summarize the entire research paper briefly — what was done, how, key results, and what it means.
🔹 Structure
I – Introduction: What problem or question is being addressed?
M – Method: How was the study conducted?
R – Results: What were the key findings?
D – Discussion: What do these findings imply?

2. Introduction — C.A.R.D. Framework
(Context – Aim – Rationale – Direction)
🔹 Purpose
Introduce your research logically — from background to your study’s direction.
🔹 Structure
C – Context: What is the broader issue?
A – Aim: What is the purpose of the study?
R – Rationale: Why is this study important?
D – Direction: What will the paper cover or argue?

3. Literature Review — C.L.A.I.M. Framework
(Categorize – Link – Analyze – Identify gap – Map forward)
🔹 Purpose
Critically review what’s already known and what remains unexplored.
🔹 Structure
C – Categorize: Group existing studies by themes or focus areas.
L – Link: Show how studies connect or differ.
A – Analyze: Evaluate strengths and weaknesses.
I – Identify Gap: Point out what’s missing.
M – Map Forward: Show how your study addresses the gap.

⚙️ 4. Methodology — P.A.S.T.E. Framework
(Population – Approach – Steps – Tools – Ethical considerations)
🔹 Purpose
Explain how the study was conducted so others can replicate it.
🔹 Structure
P – Population: Who or what was studied?
A – Approach: Research design (qualitative, quantitative, mixed).
S – Steps: Data collection procedures.
T – Tools: Instruments or technologies used.
E – Ethics: How ethical approval and participant rights were ensured.

5. Results & Discussion — S.I.R.F. Framework
(Summary – Interpretation – Relevance – Future directions)
🔹 Purpose
Present your findings and explain their meaning.
🔹 Structure
S – Summary: Highlight main results.
I – Interpretation: What do they mean?
R – Relevance: Why do they matter?
F – Future Directions: What should come next?
6. Conclusion — R.I.S.C. Framework
(Restate – Implications – Strengths/Limitations – Call to action)
🔹 Purpose
Wrap up the study with key insights and recommendations.
🔹 Structure
R – Restate: Summarize main findings briefly.
I – Implications: Show theoretical or practical value.
S – Strengths/Limitations: Acknowledge boundaries.
C – Call to Action: Suggest future research or policy change.
