Core Strategies to Build Confidence

    • Action over Outcome: Remind yourself: "My actions succeeded or failed—I didn't succeed or fail." Detaching your identity from specific outcomes allows you to take more risks [05:23].
    • Self-Compassion: Research shows that self-compassion is a stronger source of durable confidence than self-esteem. When you fail, treat yourself with the same kindness you would offer a best friend [11:06].

Actionable Takeaways
    • Outer Ring Action: Before a hard conversation, stand tall, take up space, and breathe deeply [04:19].
    • Middle Ring Action: Identify a skill gap in your domain and actively learn it to build self-efficacy [04:37].

    Academy of Ideas: Excellent for long-form, deep-dive content on philosophy, psychology, and how to navigate modern society. It resonates with the "uncarved block" and deep-thinking themes found in theMITmonk’s content.

    Big Think: A massive library featuring experts who provide actionable, educational content. It is a great place to find the "recombination of ideas" that Swadia often discusses.

    Naval Ravikant (via various podcast/clip channels): While not a traditional "teacher," Naval’s philosophy on wealth, happiness, and "leveraged" intelligence shares a deep DNA with the "AI-native" and "high-income habits" concepts promoted by Swadia.

3. Practical AI for Personal Growth

If your interest is specifically in how to use AI as a "sidekick" for learning:

    Krish Naik: Focuses on the technical side of AI and Data Science. If you want to move from being a user of AI to understanding the "trunk" of how these tools actually function, this is a more technical but highly relevant path.

    General Search for "AI for Learning": Many newer creators are focusing on "AI-assisted note-taking" and "second brains." Channels exploring Obsidian, Notion, or Logseq workflows often attract the same audience because they focus on the "system" of managing knowledge rather than just consuming it.

Why these align with your interests:

    Intentionality: Like Swadia, these creators reject passive scrolling in favor of active, "cathedral-building" work.

    Human-Centric Tech: They share the view that AI is a tool to be "directed" to enhance human agency, not a replacement for human critical thinking.

    Range over Specialization: They frequently encourage the "M-shaped" career path—combining multiple disparate interests to create unique value.

Recommendation: If you want to start with the most similar "vibe," I recommend Ali Abdaal for productivity systems or Academy of Ideas for the philosophical underpinning of modern self-development.
