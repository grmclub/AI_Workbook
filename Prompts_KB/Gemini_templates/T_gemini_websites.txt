## 7 Prompts That Can Build a Website From Scratch Using Gemini 3.5 Pro 

📌 1. Website Architecture Intelligence Prompt
(Build the skeleton before you decorate the house.)
You're a senior conversion-focused website strategist.
Based on the business information below, design a complete website architecture optimized for clarity, authority, and conversions.
Include:
– Required core pages
– Section breakdown for each page
– Logical content flow
– Primary and secondary CTAs
– Conversion objective per page
– Trust-building elements needed
Business Details:
– Industry: [Insert]
– Target Audience: [Insert]
– Core Offer: [Insert]
– Price Point: [Insert]
– Primary Goal (Leads / Sales / Authority / Bookings): [Insert]
– Unique Advantage: [Insert]
👉This ensures your site is built with strategy, not vibes.

📌 2. Brand Positioning & Messaging Engine
(If your positioning is weak, your website won’t convert.)
Analyze the business details below and define:
– Brand voice (tone, personality, communication style)
– Market positioning statement
– Unique value proposition
– Core emotional driver
– Brand promise
– Messaging pillars
Ensure the positioning feels premium, differentiated, and aligned with my audience psychology.
Business Details:
[Insert full business summary]
👉This creates consistency across your entire website.

📌 3. Homepage Conversion Framework Prompt
(Your homepage is not decoration. It’s persuasion.)
You're a CRO (conversion rate optimization) specialist.
Build a high-converting homepage structure tailored to my business.
Include:
– Headline formula with emotional trigger
– Subheadline logic
– Authority positioning block
– Problem agitation section
– Solution breakdown
– Benefits hierarchy
– Objection-handling elements
– Proof placement strategy
– CTA placement strategy
Business Details:
[Insert]
👉This transforms your homepage into a strategic funnel.

📌 5. Full Website Copy Generator (Aligned With Strategy)
Using the defined brand voice and structure above, generate complete persuasive copy for:
– Home
– About
– Services / Products
– Contact
Requirements:
– Clear and concise
– Emotionally engaging
– Objection-aware
– Action-oriented
– Consistent with positioning
Maintain this tone: [Insert tone style]
Avoid: [Insert words or tone to avoid]
👉This prevents robotic or generic website copy.


📌 6. Premium Visual Direction Blueprint
(Design influences trust before words do.)
Provide a detailed visual direction strategy for this website.
Include:
– Recommended layout style (minimal, bold, editorial, etc.)
– Color palette suggestions (with psychology explanation)
– Typography pairings
– Spacing and layout principles
– Visual hierarchy strategy
– Image style guidance
– UI inspiration direction
Brand Personality: [Insert]
Target Audience Type: [Insert]
👉This gives you a designer’s thinking, not random Canva colors.

📌 7. SEO Infrastructure Optimization Prompt
(Traffic doesn’t happen by accident.)
You're an SEO strategist.
Optimize my website content for organic search visibility.
Provide:
– Title tag recommendations per page
– Meta descriptions (CTR optimized)
– Primary and secondary keyword suggestions
– Internal linking structure
– Schema markup suggestions
– On-page optimization checklist
Industry: [Insert]
Target Region: [Insert]
