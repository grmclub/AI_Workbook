## Claude Can Help You Organize Almost Every Part Of Your Life!

1. BUILD YOUR LIFE OS
Prompt:
"Act as my Life OS planner.
My main areas:
[work/business]
[health]
[money]
[relationships]
[learning]
[personal goals]
My biggest challenges:
[overwhelm / procrastination / lack of routine / etc.]
Build me a simple system that includes:
• Life categories
• Daily routine
• Weekly planning
• Task tracker
• Habit tracker
• Sunday review
• Simple dashboard
Format it so I can easily copy it into Notion or my notes app. Keep everything practical and beginner-friendly."

2. ORGANIZE YOUR BRAIN DUMP
Prompt:
"Act as my productivity assistant.
Here’s everything on my mind:
[paste brain dump]
Organize it into:
• Urgent
• Important
• Schedule
• Delegate
• Delete
• Personal
• Work
• Ideas
Then create:
• Priority list
• 3-day action plan
• 7-day action plan
• Quick wins under 15 minutes
• The 3 things I should focus on first"

3. BUILD A DAILY ROUTINE
Prompt:
"Create a realistic daily routine around my actual schedule.
Wake-up: [time]
Bedtime: [time]
Work/school: [hours]
Peak energy: [morning / afternoon / night]
Main goals: [goals]
Include:
• Morning routine
• Focus blocks
• Breaks
• Exercise
• Meals
• Evening reset
• Night routine
Also create a minimum version for days when I'm busy."

4. PLAN YOUR ENTIRE WEEK
Prompt:
"Act as my weekly planning assistant.
Deadlines: [deadlines]
Meetings/events: [events]
Goals: [goals]
Unfinished tasks: [tasks]
Available time: [time]
Create a realistic weekly plan with:
• Top 3 priorities
• Day-by-day schedule
• Focus blocks
• Personal time
• Buffer time
• Backup plan
• Sunday review checklist
Don't overload my schedule. Leave room for unexpected tasks."

5. TURN GOALS INTO ACTION
Prompt:
"Help me turn these goals into an actionable plan:
Goal 1: [goal]
Goal 2: [goal]
Goal 3: [goal]
For each one, create:
• 90-day target
• Monthly milestones
• Weekly actions
• Daily habits
• Likely obstacles
• Progress tracker
Then rank the goals based on the priorities and constraints I provide, explain your reasoning, and give me one practical action I can take today."

6. BUILD BETTER HABITS
Prompt:
"Act as my habit-planning assistant.
Habits I want to build:
[habits]
Habits I want to reduce:
[habits]
My normal routine:
[routine]
Create a practical habit system with:
• Best time for each habit
• 2-minute starter version
• Habit stacking ideas
• Reminder ideas
• Missed-day recovery plan
• Ways to reduce common triggers
• Simple 30-day tracker
Make the plan realistic enough to maintain."

7. ORGANIZE YOUR DIGITAL LIFE
Prompt:
"Help me create a simple digital organization system.
Email: [tool]
Calendar: [tool]
Notes: [tool]
Tasks: [tool]
Storage: [tool]
Social media: [platforms]
Create a system for organizing:
• Email
• Calendar
• Notes
• Tasks
• Files
• Password-related records
• Content
• Documents
Include a folder structure, naming conventions, and weekly cleanup checklist.
Don't ask me to paste passwords, recovery codes, or other sensitive credentials."

8. THINK THROUGH A BIG DECISION
Prompt:
"Help me evaluate this decision:
Decision: [decision]
Options:
[option]
[option]
[option]
My priorities:
[time / cost / family / career / health / etc.]
Compare each option based on:
• Pros
• Cons
• Risks
• Costs
• Short-term impact
• Long-term impact
• Opportunity cost
• Fit with my priorities
Then identify which option appears to best match the priorities I've given you and explain why.
Flag any assumptions or missing information that could change the conclusion."

9. BUILD A PRODUCTIVITY SYSTEM
Prompt:
"Help me build a productivity system around how I actually work.
Work style:
[morning / night / flexible]
Biggest distractions:
[distractions]
Responsibilities:
[responsibilities]
Main productivity problem:
[problem]
Create a simple system for:
• Capturing tasks
• Prioritizing
• Daily planning
• Focused work
• Managing distractions
• Unfinished tasks
• Weekly reviews
• Sustainable workload
Keep it simple enough that I can actually use it every day."

10. CREATE A 30-DAY RESET
Prompt:
"Act as a planning and accountability assistant.
Here's my current situation:
[describe your situation]
Areas I want to improve:
Health: [details]
Finances: [details]
Career: [details]
Relationships: [details]
Personal development: [details]
First, ask me 10 useful questions to understand my situation.
After I answer, create a realistic 30-day plan with:
• Top priorities
• Weekly goals
• Daily actions
• Habits to build
• Behaviors to reduce
• Weekly check-ins
• Simple progress tracker
For medical, financial, legal, or other high-stakes issues, identify where professional guidance may be appropriate."
