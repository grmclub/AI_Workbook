Use them before your next project gets messy. Here’s the full list of 50 prompts 👇

1/ Plan, Organize & Execute Faster
These prompts help Claude organize your work and create clear action plans.
➔ "Write a prioritized daily plan from these tasks: [paste tasks]. Group them into High, Medium, and Low priority, estimate the time for each, and identify the top 3 tasks that will create the biggest impact."
➔ "Turn these messy notes into a structured action plan with objectives, action items, owners, deadlines, and risks."
➔ "Summarize these meeting notes into key decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, and unanswered questions."
➔ "Create a step-by-step execution plan for this project. Break it into milestones, dependencies, risks, and success metrics."
➔ "Analyze this task list and identify the 20% of tasks that will generate 80% of the results."
➔ "Rewrite this email to be concise, professional, and persuasive while preserving the original intent."
➔ "Turn this business idea into a professional proposal with an executive summary, objectives, timeline, budget, and expected outcomes."
➔ "Create a detailed SOP for this workflow, including prerequisites, steps, edge cases, and quality checks."
➔ "Review my workflow, identify bottlenecks, explain why they occur, and recommend improvements."
➔ "Break this large project into weekly milestones with deliverables and deadlines."

2/ Build Better Systems
Use Claude to build repeatable systems instead of making one-off plans.
➔ "Create a 30-day execution roadmap for this goal with weekly milestones and daily actions."
➔ "Prioritize these tasks using the 80/20 Principle and explain your reasoning."
➔ "Generate a weekly schedule based on my priorities, meetings, and available working hours."
➔ "Create a weighted decision matrix to compare these options."
➔ "Turn this brainstorming session into organized action items grouped by priority."
➔ "Write a professional project brief from these notes."
➔ "Create a meeting agenda with objectives, discussion topics, and expected outcomes."
➔ "Write a follow-up email summarizing this meeting and clearly assigning action items."
➔ "Organize these research notes into logical categories with summaries."
➔ "Convert this transcript into an executive report with recommendations."

3/ Create Better Content
Claude excels when you give it audience, context, and output requirements.
➔ "Generate 20 content ideas for my niche based on these audience pain points."
➔ "Rewrite this article as a LinkedIn post that maintains the key message."
➔ "Convert this blog post into a Twitter/X thread with engaging hooks."
➔ "Repurpose this content into a Facebook post optimized for engagement."
➔ "Build a 30-day content calendar with publishing dates and content goals."
➔ "Generate 10 viral hooks for this topic aimed at [target audience]."
➔ "Rewrite this article for complete beginners without losing accuracy."
➔ "Turn this tutorial into a step-by-step checklist."
➔ "Generate frequently asked questions from this document."
➔ "Write five compelling CTAs for this offer, each with a different style."

4/ Grow Your Business
Use Claude as a strategist, not just a copywriter.
➔ "Analyze my competitor's marketing strategy and identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats."
➔ "Audit this landing page for messaging, UX, conversion optimization, and trust signals."
➔ "Generate SEO keyword opportunities based on this topic and search intent."
➔ "Write product descriptions that emphasize customer benefits and unique selling points."
➔ "Create customer personas from this research."
➔ "Generate 20 email subject lines optimized for high open rates."
➔ "Write a sales script that handles common objections."
➔ "Create responses to these customer objections using empathy and evidence."
➔ "Build a complete marketing campaign for this product launch."
➔ "Generate Facebook, Google, and LinkedIn ad copy for this offer."

5/ Learn, Analyze & Improve
Claude works best when you ask it to think critically before answering.
➔ "Analyze this spreadsheet and identify trends, anomalies, and recommendations."
➔ "Explain this topic as if teaching a complete beginner."
➔ "Turn this PDF into structured study notes."
➔ "Create a quiz with answers based on this document."
➔ "Build a four-week learning roadmap for this skill."
➔ "Compare these two strategies objectively using a decision matrix."
➔ "Act as my executive assistant and organize today's priorities."
➔ "Review this business plan and identify the biggest risks."
➔ "Review my work against industry best practices and recommend improvements."
➔ "Before we begin, ask me the same questions an experienced consultant would ask to fully understand this project."


The biggest productivity gains don't come from using more prompts.
They come from writing clearer prompts with more context, specific constraints, and a defined output format.
