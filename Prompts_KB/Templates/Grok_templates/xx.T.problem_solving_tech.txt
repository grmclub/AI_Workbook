║  💎 GOLDEN RULES FOR TECH CRITICAL THINKING                                                                  ║
║  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  ║
║                                                                                                              ║
║  1.  Simple is not simplistic. Complexity is not sophistication                                              ║
║  2.  The map is not the territory. Your diagram is not the system                                            ║
║  3.  Working software beats elegant architecture every time                                                  ║
║  4.  Optimize for the next engineer, not the next benchmark                                                  ║
║  5.  Premature optimization is the root of all evil — Knuth                                                  ║
║  6.  Name the bias before it ships to production                                                             ║
║  7.  Understand why the fence exists before tearing it down                                                  ║
║  8.  The best code is code you don't have to write                                                           ║
║  9.  Scale problems are good problems. Solve today's problems today                                          ║
║  10. Every technical decision is also a team and organizational decision                                     ║
