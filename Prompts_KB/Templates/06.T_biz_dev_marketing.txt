Prompt 1: Niche Picker

"Act as a local business analyst. Identify the top 3 niches in [my city] paying $5,000+/month for a repetitive role AI can replace. Rank by payroll budget, urgency, and ease of install. Pick the winner. Use real data, not guesses."
This kills "I don't know what niche to pick" in 30 seconds.

Prompt 2: Cold Outreach Generator

"Write 5 cold DMs to [niche] business owners. Under 75 words each. Open with a specific dollar amount AI can save them on payroll. No jargon. No 'AI consultant' language. End each with: 'Want me to send a 2-minute demo?'"
This is the message that books your first 5 discovery calls.

Prompt 3: Discovery Call Closer

"Build me a 15-minute discovery call script for a [niche] owner. Open with: 'What's the most expensive role in your business right now?' Map 5 pain-uncovering questions, 2 objection handlers for 'I don't think AI can do that,' and a soft pitch to a 30-day pilot."
Now nothing on a call catches you off guard.

Prompt 4: Installation Playbook

"Design a complete client onboarding SOP for installing Intercom AI, Helios AI, and n8n. Cover the kickoff call, tool access setup, a 14-day install timeline, success metrics, and monthly reporting. Simple enough to hand to a $300/month VA so I never touch a dashboard myself."

Now you're hands-off the delivery — and free to close more.
