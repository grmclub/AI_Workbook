Step 1: Pick a Niche That Has Buyers (Not Just Attention)
Most people pick niches based on what they like…
That’s why they stay broke.
You need:
👉 problems
👉 urgency
👉 people already spending money
No buyers = no income.
✅ Action plan:
1️.Pick a niche where people want results
2.Look for pain (money, health, relationships)
3.Avoid “hobby” niches with no urgency
‼️ Prompt:
“Act as a market research expert.
Give me 10 profitable niches where people are already spending money online.
For each niche include:
• main problem people want solved
• what they are currently buying
• content ideas that attract buyers (not just views)”

Step 2: Create Content That Attracts Buyers (Not Just Likes)
Likes don’t pay bills.
Buyer content does.
Your content should:
👉 solve problems
👉 expose mistakes
👉 show outcomes
That’s what pulls in people ready to act.
✅ Action plan:
1.Teach something simple
2.Call out a painful problem
3.Show a clear result
‼️ Prompt:
“Act as a content strategist.
Generate 20 Facebook post ideas in the [INSERT NICHE].
Each idea must:
• solve a real problem
• attract buyers (not just engagement)
• be simple enough for beginners to create”

Step 3: Write Hooks That Stop the Scroll
If your first line is weak…
Nobody reads the rest.
Your hook is everything.
It should:
👉 trigger curiosity
👉 challenge beliefs
👉 make people feel something
✅ Action plan:
1.Keep it short and bold
2.Speak directly to pain
3.Create curiosity gaps
‼️ Prompt:
“Act as a viral Facebook copywriter.
Write 25 scroll-stopping hooks for the [INSERT NICHE].
Each hook should:
• be under 12 words
• create curiosity or tension
• make people want to read more”

Step 4: Turn Posts Into Conversations (This Is Where Money Starts)
Most people post…
Then disappear.
Smart creators:
👉 start conversations
👉 reply to comments
👉 move people into DMs
That’s where sales happen.
✅ Action plan:
1.End every post with a simple CTA
2.Reply to every comment
3.Start natural conversations
‼️ Prompt:
“Act as a social media engagement expert.
Create 10 simple CTAs that:
• encourage comments
• feel natural (not salesy)
• lead into conversations in DMs”

Step 5: Use DMs to Build Trust (Not Push Sales)
People don’t buy from strangers.
They buy from people they trust.
Your DMs should feel like:
👉 helping
👉 guiding
👉 understanding
Not selling.
✅ Action plan:
1.Ask simple questions
2.Understand their situation
3.guide them step by step
‼️ Prompt:
“Act as a DM conversation expert.
Give me a step-by-step DM conversation flow that:
• builds trust
• uncovers the person’s problem
• naturally leads to a solution
Use simple language for beginners.”

Step 6: Offer Something That Already Works
This is where most people get stuck…
They think they need to create their own product.
You don’t.
You can start by selling:
👉 proven digital products
👉 existing offers
👉 systems that already convert
That’s how you move faster.
✅ Action plan:
1.Find a proven offer
2.focus on traffic + conversations
3.improve your messaging
‼️ Prompt:
“Act as a business coach for beginners.
Explain how someone can start making money online without creating their own product.
Include:
• examples of what to sell
• how to find proven offers
• how to start fast”

Step 7: Consistency Creates Momentum (This Is the Real Secret)
Nobody talks about this…
But this is what changes everything.
The algorithm rewards:
👉 consistency
👉 engagement
👉 activity
Most people quit too early.
That’s why they never win.
✅ Action plan:
1.Post daily (or consistently)
2.Engage with people every day
3.improve based on feedback
‼️ Prompt:
“Act as a growth strategist.
Create a simple 30-day Facebook growth plan for a beginner.
Include:
• what to post daily
• how to engage
• how to improve results over time”
