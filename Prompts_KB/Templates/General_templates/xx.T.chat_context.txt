1. Type this in any chat:
"What do you remember about me? List everything in your saved memory, exactly as it's stored, not summarized."
This pulls the actual entries, not a vague overview.

2. Find what you didn't mean to share
"Of everything you remember about me, what's the most personal or sensitive detail in there? Be honest, even if I might not like the answer."

3. Catch outdated info
"Do any of your memories about me sound like they could be outdated, like an old job, a finished project, or a preference I might have changed?"
This one catches the stuff most people forget is even in there.

4. Delete something specific
"Forget that I mentioned [whatever it is]. Confirm once it's removed."
Works conversationally, no settings menu needed.

5. Understand what it's using right now
"Without listing facts, tell me how you're currently adjusting your tone or answers based on what you know about me."
This shows you the effect of memory, not just the data behind it.

6. The full reset
If you want a clean slate: go to Settings → Personalization → Manage Memory, and you'll see every entry listed individually with a delete option, plus a "Clear all" at the bottom.
It takes less than a minute. Most people never open that screen once.
