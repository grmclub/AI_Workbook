List sample prompts to analyze APAC equities market trends

Prompt 1: Track Regional Macro Drivers
text
Act as a senior APAC equity strategist. Analyze how recent macroeconomic shifts—specifically regional central bank interest rate decisions, currency fluctuations against the USD, and regional inflation data—are impacting major APAC equity indices (e.g., Nikkei 225, ASX 200, Hang Seng, Nifty 50). Provide a structured summary highlighting the top three macro headwinds and top three tailwinds for the current quarter.


Prompt 2: Evaluate Regulatory Shifts & Policy Impacts
text
Act as a financial regulatory analyst specializing in Asian markets. Review recent policy updates, security regulations, and trade restrictions originating from major APAC economies. Detail how these regulatory changes are influencing foreign institutional capital inflows and outflows across emerging versus developed APAC equity markets.


Prompt 3: Assess Cross-Border Supply Chain Vulnerabilities
text
Act as an equity research analyst covering the APAC technology and manufacturing sectors. Evaluate how ongoing regional supply chain reconfigurations and geopolitical tensions are impacting the earnings outlooks of top-tier semiconductor and hardware equities. Identify which specific geographic sub-regions are benefiting as alternative manufacturing hubs.


Prompt 4: Scan for Valuation Anomalies
text
Act as a quantitative equity analyst. Create a framework to identify sector-wide valuation anomalies across APAC markets. Compare current Forward P/E, P/B, and EV/EBITDA ratios against their 5-year historical averages for the Financials, Consumer Discretionary, and Technology sectors. Highlight which countries appear historically undervalued despite strong fundamentals.


Prompt 5: Monitor Sector-Specific Capital Flows
text
Act as a portfolio manager tracking APAC institutional data. Analyze recent sector-level capital flows, focusing heavily on Clean Energy, Electric Vehicles, and Banking sectors across the region. Synthesize market sentiment data to predict which sectors are positioned for the highest institutional momentum over the next 30 to 60 days.

Master Prompt Template for Deep Market Analysis

Use this multi-step prompt framework to guide an AI through a technical and fundamental market analysis. Copy, paste, and fill in the bracketed variables:

"Act as a Quantitative Market Strategist and Financial Analyst. Conduct a thorough market and sentiment analysis for [Ticker Symbol / Asset Name] using the following data-driven structure:
    1. Price Action & Trend Analysis: Evaluate the current price trends relative to key moving averages (50-day and 200-day EMA/SMA). Identify immediate support and resistance levels.
    2. Technical Indicators: Analyze momentum and volatility metrics (RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands) to determine if the asset is overbought, oversold, or consolidating.
    3. Fundamental Catalysts: Assess recent earnings reports, revenue growth trajectories, P/E ratios relative to sector peers, and upcoming corporate events (e.g., product launches, earnings calls).
    4. Market Sentiment: Synthesize broader market sentiment, options flow patterns (put/call ratios), and recent institutional trading activity.
    5. Risk-Reward Matrix: Detail the primary bearish and bullish scenarios over a 30-day horizon, outlining specific stop-loss areas and upside targets based on current volatility.
Base your conclusions strictly on quantitative trends rather than speculative sentiment, and clearly outline the core risks."
