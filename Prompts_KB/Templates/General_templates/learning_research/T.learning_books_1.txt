##7 Claude prompts help you understand nonfiction books faster and remember more.

1/ The One Big Idea
Prompt:
Act as an expert teacher.
After reading [Book Title] by [Author], tell me the single most important idea in the book.
Explain it in simple language and give me one real-world example that makes it easy to
remember.

2/ The 80/20 Summary
Prompt:
Apply the 80/20 principle to [Book Title] by [Author].
Identify the 20% of ideas that provide roughly 80% of the practical value.
Ignore minor details and focus on what matters most.

3/ The Action Blueprint
Prompt:
Turn the main ideas from [Book Title] by [Author] into a practical action plan.
Give me five specific actions I can start using immediately.

Each action should take less than 15 minutes to begin.

4/ Challenge the Author
Prompt:
Play devil's advocate.
What are the strongest arguments against the ideas in [Book Title] by [Author]?
Where might the author's advice fail, and in what situations would a different approach work
better?

5/ Connect the Dots
Prompt:
Compare the ideas in [Book Title] by [Author] with three other well-known nonfiction books.
Show where the ideas overlap, where they disagree, and what unique perspective this book
contributes.

6/ Make It Stick
Prompt:
Help me remember the key lessons from [Book Title] by [Author].
Create:
● five memorable analogies,
● three mental models,
● and ten flashcards I can review in under five minutes.

7/ Teach It Like an Expert

Prompt:
Pretend I have to teach the ideas from [Book Title] by [Author] tomorrow.
Create a simple 10-minute lesson with:
● the core concepts,
● relatable examples,
● common misunderstandings,
● and three questions that test whether someone truly understands the material.
End with the single lesson I should never forget.

5. Smart Knowledge Summary
"Summarize [insert topic] into clear frameworks, mental models, and simplified structures so the most important information becomes easier to remember and apply."

7. Weakness Assessment

##Claude Has An Infinite Personal Tutor Mode That Can Teach Any Skill From Zero In 4 Hours 👇

1. THE LEARNING CURVE DESTROYER
PROMPT:
"You are a teacher who only has 4 hours with me and will never see me again. Your only objective is to make me functional in [SKILL] before the time runs out.
Do not give me theory without practical use.
Do not give me a list.
Tell me: what to learn first, what to completely ignore, and what exercise, if I do it once, will already put me ahead of 70% of people who have been studying this for months."

2. THE REAL ERROR SIMULATOR
PROMPT:
"Do not explain [CONCEPT] to me.
Put me directly into a real situation where I would have to use it and would probably make a mistake.
When I make a mistake, do not give me the answer. Ask me a question that forces me to discover where my reasoning is broken.
Only give me the answer after I have tried at least two times.
Repeat this cycle until I can get it right without hesitation."

3. THE IMPOSSIBLE LANGUAGE
PROMPT:
"This content below is confusing to me.
Before explaining it, tell me: what is the one sentence that, if I understand it, will make the rest make sense on its own?
Explain only that sentence first.
Use an everyday analogy, with no technical terms.
Then ask me 3 questions that only someone who truly understood it would be able to answer.
Do not continue until I pass all three.
[PASTE THE CONTENT HERE]"

4. THE PERSONAL LEARNING PATH
PROMPT:
"My real goal is [GOAL].
It is not to learn [SKILL] in general. It is to achieve [SPECIFIC RESULT] within [DEADLINE].
I already know [WHAT YOU ALREADY MASTER].
Based on that, build me a 7-day learning path.
Each day must include: one single task that fits within 45 minutes, a clear criterion so I know whether I did it correctly, and what not to do that day so I do not waste time.
If the entire path does not lead me to the goal, rebuild it until it does."

5. THE HIDDEN GAP DETECTOR
PROMPT:
"I think I already master [SKILL].
I want you to prove me wrong.
Ask me 5 questions that seem simple but expose the gaps of someone who has never truly gone deep.
For every answer I give, tell me: what my answer reveals about what is still missing in my foundation.
Do not go easy on me.
If I am being shallow, tell me directly."

6. THE FORCED FEYNMAN METHOD
PROMPT:
"I just studied [TOPIC].
I am going to explain what I understood as if you were 10-year-old child.
While I explain, stop me every time I use jargon without knowing what it means, skip a step in the reasoning, or simplify so much that it becomes wrong.
At the end, tell me exactly what these mistakes reveal about what is still not solid in my mind."

##ChatGPT Became One Of The Best Tools For Students!
Here Are 7 Prompts EVERY Student Should Try

1. LEARN ANY TOPIC LIKE A PRO
Prompt:
Act as an expert teacher and explain [TOPIC] from beginner to advanced level.
Break the topic into small sections and explain each concept in simple language. Use real-world examples, analogies, and practical applications to make learning easier.
At the end, provide:
- Key concepts summary
- Important formulas/definitions
- Common mistakes students make
- 10 practice questions with answers

2. TURN ANY CHAPTER INTO EXAM NOTES
Prompt:
Convert the following chapter into concise, high-scoring exam notes.
[PASTE CONTENT]
Requirements:
- Use bullet points
- Highlight important definitions
- Extract key formulas and concepts
- Include frequently asked exam questions
- Create memory tricks where possible
- Provide a 5-minute revision sheet at the end

3. CREATE A PERSONALIZED STUDY PLAN
Prompt:
I have an exam in [X] days for [SUBJECT].
Create a detailed day-wise study plan based on my available time. Focus on high-weightage topics first and include revision sessions, practice tests, and time management strategies.
My current preparation level:
[BEGINNER/INTERMEDIATE/ADVANCED]
My target score: [TARGET MARKS OR PERCENTAGE]

4. SOLVE QUESTIONS AND EXPLAIN THE LOGIC
Prompt:
Solve the following question step by step.
[PASTE QUESTION]
For each step:
- Explain the reasoning
- Mention common mistakes
- Provide a shortcut method if available
- Explain which concept is being used
At the end, generate 3 similar practice questions with solutions.

5. PERSONAL AI TUTOR MODE
Prompt:
Act as my personal tutor for [SUBJECT].
First assess my current knowledge level by asking a few questions. Then identify my weak areas and create a personalized learning path.
Teach concepts interactively, ask follow-up questions, provide practice exercises, and help me improve step by step until I fully understand the topic.

6. PLACEMENT & INTERVIEW PREPARATION
Prompt:
Based on my resume and target role, generate the most likely interview questions I may face.
[PASTE RESUME]
Include:
- Technical questions
- HR questions
- Situational questions
- Detailed model answers
- Common mistakes candidates make
- Important topics I should revise before the interview

7. BUILD A COMPLETE CAREER ROADMAP
Prompt:
I am a [YEAR] student and I want to become a [ROLE].
Create a complete roadmap covering:
- Skills I need to learn
- Best free resources and courses
- Projects to build
- Certifications worth pursuing
- Internship strategy
- Resume and LinkedIn tips
- Interview preparation plan
Create a month-by-month action plan that can help me become job-ready as quickly as possible.
