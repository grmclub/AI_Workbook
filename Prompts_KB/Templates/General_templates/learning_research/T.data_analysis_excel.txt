🚨 8 prompts using Claude to simplify working with spreadsheets
Analyze spreadsheets.
Generate formulas.
Build dashboards.
Find hidden insights.
Create executive reports.


1.UNDERSTAND ANY SPREADSHEET
Upload your Excel file and use:
PROMPT:
Analyze this spreadsheet and develop a comprehensive understanding of its structure, purpose, and contents. Identify the types of data included, key metrics being tracked, relationships between columns, reporting objectives, and the overall business context. Explain how the dataset is organized, assess its completeness and quality, and provide an overview of the most important information it contains. Present the output as a structured briefing that helps a new analyst quickly understand the dataset before performing deeper analysis.

2.ANALYZE THE DATA LIKE A SENIOR ANALYST
PROMPT:
Analyze this spreadsheet as a senior business analyst. Explain what the dataset represents, identify key trends, patterns, anomalies, missing values, inconsistencies, and potential errors. Evaluate the overall quality and reliability of the data while highlighting the most important findings and business insights. Support your conclusions with evidence from the dataset and explain the practical implications of each observation. Present the analysis in a clear, structured format prioritized by significance and potential business impact.

3.GENERATE EXCEL FORMULAS AUTOMATICALLY
PROMPT:
Review this spreadsheet and determine the most valuable calculations, metrics, and formulas needed to improve analysis, reporting, forecasting, and decision-making. Generate the exact Excel formulas required, including lookups, growth calculations, profitability metrics, conditional logic, trend analysis, and forecasting models. Explain the purpose of each formula, how it should be implemented, what business problem it solves, and provide examples of expected outputs. Prioritize recommendations based on analytical value and business usefulness.

4.CLEAN AND FIX THE DATA
PROMPT:
Act as a data quality specialist and perform a comprehensive audit of this spreadsheet. Identify duplicate records, missing values, formatting inconsistencies, outliers, broken formulas, invalid entries, and any issues that could affect accuracy or analysis. Explain the impact of each issue, assess the overall health of the dataset, and recommend specific corrective actions. Create a step-by-step data-cleaning plan that improves reliability, consistency, and analytical readiness.

5.FIND HIGH-VALUE BUSINESS INSIGHTS
PROMPT:
Act as a senior strategy consultant and analyze this spreadsheet to uncover meaningful business insights. Identify growth opportunities, emerging trends, performance drivers, customer behavior patterns, operational inefficiencies, potential risks, and hidden opportunities that may not be immediately obvious. Support each insight with evidence from the data, explain its significance, and provide actionable recommendations. Prioritize findings based on strategic importance, potential impact, and decision-making value.

6.BUILD CHARTS & DASHBOARDS
PROMPT:
Act as a business intelligence and data visualization expert. Analyze this spreadsheet and recommend the most effective charts, visualizations, dashboards, and reporting structures. For each recommendation, explain why it should be used, what insight it communicates, who the intended audience is, and how it supports decision-making. Design an executive-level dashboard framework that highlights the most important KPIs, trends, opportunities, and risks while maximizing clarity, usability, and business impact.

7.CREATE AUTOMATED REPORTING SYSTEMS
PROMPT:
Design a comprehensive reporting and automation system based on this spreadsheet. Recommend workflows for recurring reports, KPI monitoring, automated alerts, executive summaries, dashboard updates, performance tracking, and operational reporting. Explain how each automation improves efficiency, reduces manual effort, increases accuracy, and supports better decision-making. Create a scalable framework that transforms this spreadsheet into an automated business intelligence and reporting system.

8.EXECUTIVE SUMMARY MODE
PROMPT:
Analyze the spreadsheet and create a comprehensive executive summary. Include the most important findings, key trends, critical risks, growth opportunities, supporting evidence, business implications, and strategic recommendations. Prioritize information based on significance and potential impact. Present the output in a concise, professional, data-driven format suitable for executives, decision-makers, investors, and stakeholders, with a clear focus on actions, priorities, and next steps.

🚨 GEMINI CAN CREATE INTERACTIVE DASHBOARDS FROM RAW DATA.

Step 1: Upload Your Data
Open Gemini and upload your Excel file, CSV, Google Sheet, or PDF report.
Gemini can understand your data, identify trends, calculate key metrics, and prepare everything needed for a professional dashboard.

Step 2: The Canvas Setup & Build
▪︎ Open Gemini and click Canvas.
▪︎ Ensure you're using the latest Gemini Pro model (the Thinking model works best for complex data analysis).
▪︎ Upload your Excel file, CSV, Google Sheet, or PDF if you haven't already.
▪︎ Wait for Gemini to process your data.
💡 Tip: Canvas lets you build and preview the dashboard side by side with the chat, making it easy to iterate in real time.
Now use this prompt:
«Analyze the attached dataset and create a modern interactive dashboard for [metric] covering [time period]. Include KPI cards, trend charts, category comparisons, filters, and a searchable data table. Make the layout clean, responsive, and easy to navigate.»

Step 3: Customize the Design
Once the dashboard is generated, tailor it to match your brand.
You can change colors, typography, spacing, icons, and the overall layout.
Prompt:
"Redesign this dashboard using [Company Name] branding. Use [Color 1] and [Color 2], apply a modern design, improve spacing and typography, and use professional icons throughout."

Step 4: Edit Individual Components
One of Canvas's best features is targeted editing.
Instead of rebuilding the entire dashboard, click a specific chart, table, button, or section and tell Gemini exactly what to change.
Examples:
"Replace this bar chart with a stacked column chart."
"Change this icon to a shopping cart."
"Move the KPI cards above the charts."

Step 5: Add Advanced Features
Go beyond static charts by adding functionality.
Prompt:
Add filters for date, region, and product category. Include year-over-year comparisons, growth percentages, drill-down charts, dark mode, and Export as PDF and Export as Image buttons.

Step 6: Export and Share
When you're happy with the result, ask Gemini to export the project.
You can:
▪︎ Export the React code.
▪︎ Continue refining it in Canvas.
▪︎ Share it with your team.
▪︎ Deploy it as a web application.
