Step 1: Identify and Define the Research Problem
Objective: Clearly identify a meaningful research problem and refine it into a focused, relevant, and researchable topic that addresses a genuine gap in existing knowledge.
Upload to NotebookLM:
- Research idea or concept note
- Preliminary literature
- Key journal articles
- Reports and policy documents (if applicable)
- Notes on the research context or field

Prompt:
"Using the uploaded research materials, help me identify and define a clear, significant, and researchable problem for my research proposal. Critically evaluate the existing literature to identify unresolved issues, inconsistencies, emerging trends, practical challenges, and gaps in current knowledge. Assess the originality, relevance, and feasibility of the proposed research topic and explain why it is important to investigate. Refine the research problem into a concise and compelling problem statement that is suitable for a research proposal. Provide recommendations for strengthening the significance, clarity, focus, and scholarly contribution of the proposed study."


Step 2: Conduct a Preliminary Literature Review
Objective: Explore and critically evaluate the existing literature to understand the current state of knowledge, identify key theories and debates, and refine the direction of the proposed research.
Upload to NotebookLM:
- Preliminary journal articles
- Conference papers
- Book chapters
- Reports and policy documents (if applicable)
- Research problem and concept note
- Existing literature review notes (if available)
Prompt:
"Using the uploaded literature and research problem, conduct a preliminary literature review to evaluate the current state of knowledge on my proposed research topic. Identify the major theories, concepts, methodological approaches, key findings, debates, and recent developments within the literature. Critically assess the strengths, limitations, inconsistencies, and unresolved issues across existing studies. Identify potential research gaps and explain how they relate to my proposed study. Provide recommendations for refining the research focus and strengthening the theoretical and scholarly foundation of my research proposal."

Step 3: Develop the Research Aim, Objectives, and Questions
Objective: Develop a clear research aim, specific objectives, and focused research questions that align with the identified research problem and guide the entire study.

Assess whether the research questions are clear, feasible, sufficiently focused, and capable of generating meaningful findings. Recommend improvements to strengthen their clarity, coherence, originality, and scholarly significance. Ensure that the final research aim, objectives, and questions provide a strong foundation for the proposed study and are suitable for a high-quality research proposal."

Step 4: Establish the Research Gap and Justification
Objective: Clearly identify the research gap and develop a compelling justification that demonstrates the significance, originality, and potential contribution of the proposed study.
Upload to NotebookLM:
- Preliminary literature review
- Key journal articles
- Research problem statement
- Research aim, objectives, and questions
- Notes on existing knowledge gaps

Provide recommendations for strengthening the research gap, justification, and scholarly contribution to ensure that the proposal presents a compelling rationale for conducting the study."
