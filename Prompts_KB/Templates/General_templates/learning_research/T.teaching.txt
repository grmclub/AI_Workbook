Use these 10 powerful NotebookLM prompts to create engaging, classroom-ready English presentations in minutes


1. Complete English Lesson
"Act as an experienced English teacher and create a complete, slide-by-slide presentation on [insert topic]. Include a title slide, lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, clear explanations, examples, guided practice, independent practice, review activities, and a final recap slide. Structure the lesson according to communicative language teaching (CLT) principles to maximize student engagement and learning."

2. Grammar Lesson Presentation
"Act as an expert English teacher and create a complete presentation on [insert grammar point] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, clear explanations of the target grammar, examples in context, guided practice, communicative activities, common learner mistakes, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and maximize student interaction throughout the lesson."

3. Vocabulary Lesson Presentation
"Act as an expert English teacher and create a complete vocabulary lesson presentation on [insert topic] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, target vocabulary with definitions and pronunciation support, example sentences, collocations, guided practice, communicative activities, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and ensure students actively use the target vocabulary throughout the lesson."

4. Reading Lesson Presentation
"Act as an expert English teacher and create a complete reading lesson presentation on [insert text/topic] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, pre-reading tasks, prediction activities, vocabulary preparation, gist and detailed comprehension questions, critical thinking tasks, discussion activities, follow-up tasks, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and ensure the lesson is interactive and learner-centered."

5. Listening Lesson Presentation
"Act as an expert English teacher and create a complete listening lesson presentation on [insert topic] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, pre-listening tasks, prediction activities, key vocabulary, gist and detailed listening questions, discussion activities, follow-up speaking tasks, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and ensure students actively engage with the listening material throughout the lesson."

6. Writing Lesson Presentation
"Act as an expert English teacher and create a complete writing lesson presentation on [insert writing genre/topic] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, model texts, text analysis, useful language, writing structure, guided writing tasks, peer-review activities, independent writing practice, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and ensure students develop both accuracy and fluency in writing."

7. Speaking Lesson Presentation
"Act as an expert English teacher and create a complete speaking lesson presentation on [insert topic] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, useful vocabulary and expressions, model dialogues, guided speaking tasks, pair-work and group activities, role plays, discussion questions, fluency-building activities, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and maximize student talking time throughout the lesson."

8. Pronunciation Lesson Presentation
"Act as an expert English teacher and create a complete pronunciation lesson presentation on [insert pronunciation feature] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, clear explanations of the target pronunciation feature, mouth position guidance, listening discrimination tasks, minimal pairs, drilling activities, controlled practice, communicative speaking tasks, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and ensure students have multiple opportunities to practice and produce the target sounds."

9. Business English Lesson
"Act as an expert Business English teacher and create a complete lesson presentation on [insert topic] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, key business vocabulary, authentic workplace scenarios, model dialogues, role plays, discussion tasks, case studies, communication strategies, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and ensure learners can confidently apply the target language in real workplace situations."

10. IELTS Preparation Lesson
"Act as an expert IELTS teacher and create a complete IELTS lesson presentation on [insert IELTS skill/task] for [insert CEFR level] learners. Include lesson objectives, a warm-up activity, exam strategies, model answers, useful language, common mistakes, guided practice activities, exam-style tasks, feedback opportunities, review exercises, and a final recap slide. Follow communicative language teaching (CLT) principles and ensure students develop the skills and confidence needed to succeed in the IELTS exam."
