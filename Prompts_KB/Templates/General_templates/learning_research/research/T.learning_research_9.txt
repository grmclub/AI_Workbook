1. The Interactive Expert Tutor Framework
    Prompt: Act as an expert tutor that helps me master any topic through an interactive, interview-style course. The process must be recursive and personalized.
    This is what I want you to do:
        Ask me which topic I want to learn.
        Divide that topic into a structured program of progressive lessons, starting with the fundamentals and advancing to more advanced concepts.
        For each lesson:
            Explain the concept clearly and concisely, using analogies and real-world examples.
            Ask me Socratic-style questions to assess and deepen my understanding.
            Give me a short exercise or thought experiment to apply what I've learned.
            Ask me if I am ready to continue or if I need more clarification.
                If I say yes, move on to the next concept.
                If I say no, reformulate the explanation, provide additional examples, and guide me with clues until I understand it.
        After each important section, offer a mini-quiz or a structured summary.
        When the entire topic has been covered, assess my understanding with a final challenge that combines multiple concepts.
        Encourage me to reflect on what I have learned and suggest how I could apply it to a real project or situation.

    FORMAT:

        Bonus: Analogies or examples to make it super easy to understand
    STYLE:
        Use simple words and short sentences
        No jargon (or explain it simply)
        Write like you're teaching a friend
        Use analogies, stories, or everyday examples
        Add emojis where helpful 😊

        Do not copy large blocks of text
        Do not use technical jargon without explanation
        Do not make it too long or academic
        Do not leave out the key findings

##15 ChatGPT Prompts For Better Research

These templates focus on research strategies, literature mapping, and analysis frameworks.

1. Topic Brainstorm
    Prompt: Give 10 research topics on [field], why they matter in 2025, one challenge, one key question.

2. Literature Review
    Prompt: Summarize top 5 studies (2023-2025) on [topic]: methods, findings, gaps, links.

3. Question Builder
    Prompt: Turn this idea into 5 research questions tied to 2025 trends, each with a hypothesis.

4. Timeline
    Prompt: List key events in [topic], 2010-2025: breakthroughs, people, implications.

5. Data Helper
    Prompt: Given this dataset [paste], suggest 3 tests, insights, visuals for a 2025 paper.

6. Gap Finder
    Prompt: From these sources [links], list 4 gaps and propose an experiment for each.

7. Methodology
    Prompt: Draft step-by-step method for [topic]: tools, ethics, outcomes for 2025.

8. Credibility Check
    Prompt: Evaluate this article [link]: score bias, evidence, relevance (1-10). Suggest better sources.

9. Trend Forecast
    Prompt: Predict 5 trends in [field] for 2026-2030 using 2025 data and research angles.

10. Ethics Review
    Prompt: List ethical issues in [topic]: privacy, bias, 2025 regulations.

11. Summary Maker
    Prompt: Condense this abstract [paste] into 5 bullets: innovations, limits, 2025 uses.

12. Hypothesis Generator
    Prompt: Give 3 testable hypotheses for [question], with variables, results, validation.

13. Interdisciplinary Linker
    Prompt: Connect [topic1] + [topic2] in 4 ways and suggest hybrid 2025 projects.

14. Quiz Builder
    Prompt: Create 10 Q&A on [topic] with brief explanations.

15. Research Plan
    Prompt: Draft a plan for [topic]: objectives, timeline, budget, 2025 impact/partners.
