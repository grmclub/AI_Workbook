Research Design Prompt (ChatGPT)

"Act as an expert academic methodologist. I am developing a research study with the following core parameters:
    • Research Topic: [Insert your specific topic, e.g., the impact of remote work on employee retention in the tech sector]
    • Research Question: [Insert your primary research question]
    • Objective: [Insert what you want to achieve, e.g., to identify causal factors of turnover]
    • Constraints: [Insert any constraints, e.g., qualitative or quantitative, timeframe, sample population]
Based on these details, please generate a complete research design draft. Include the following sections:
    1. Research Design: Recommend a design (e.g., cross-sectional, phenomenological, RCT) and justify why it fits my objective.
    2. Population & Sampling: Suggest an appropriate sampling technique (e.g., purposive, stratified random) and target sample size.
    3. Data Collection: Propose valid and reliable data collection instruments.
    4. Data Analysis: Recommend specific statistical or thematic analysis methods.
    5. Limitations & Validity: Identify potential biases or limitations and suggest mitigation strategies.
Please format this as an organized outline and ask me up to 3 clarifying questions if any of my parameters are unclear."

To narrow down the results, refine search with:
Is your research primarily qualitative, quantitative, or mixed methods?
What is your target population or geographic location?
