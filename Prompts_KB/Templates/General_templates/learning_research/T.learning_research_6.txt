Here are 10 powerful prompts to help you summarize any video, uncover hidden insights, and turn information into action.

1. Executive Summary Generator
"Analyze this video and provide a concise executive summary. Identify the main topic, the speaker's core arguments, and the five most important takeaways. Present the information in clear, easy-to-understand language suitable for someone who has not watched the video."

2. Timestamped Insight Extractor
"Analyze this video and extract the 10 most valuable insights. For each insight, include the timestamp, a brief explanation of why it matters, and one practical action I can take based on that insight. Present the results in a table for easy reference."

3. Action Item Finder
"Review this video and identify every practical recommendation, strategy, framework, and action item mentioned. Organize them into a prioritized checklist categorized as quick wins, medium-term actions, and long-term initiatives."

4. Study Guide Creator
"Transform this video into a comprehensive study guide. Include key concepts and definitions, important examples and analogies, memorable quotes, discussion questions, and a 10-question quiz with answers to assess understanding."

5. Expert Analysis Prompt
"Act as an expert in [FIELD]. Analyze this video and evaluate the accuracy of the information presented, the strengths and weaknesses of the speaker's arguments, any assumptions, biases, or unsupported claims, and areas requiring additional evidence or clarification. Conclude with your overall assessment of the video's credibility and usefulness."

6. Content Repurposing Assistant
"Analyze this video and repurpose its content into the following formats: a LinkedIn post, an X (Twitter) thread, a Facebook post, a blog post outline, and five memorable quotes from the speaker. Adapt each piece of content to suit its intended audience and platform while preserving the original message and key insights."

7. Meeting Notes Generator
"Review this meeting recording and generate professional meeting notes. Include the meeting objective, key discussion points, decisions made, unresolved issues, assigned responsibilities, and recommended next steps. Present the information in a clear, structured format suitable for sharing with stakeholders."

8. Learning Accelerator
"Analyze this educational video and identify the 20% of concepts that deliver 80% of the practical value. Explain these concepts in simple terms, provide real-world examples to illustrate their application, and recommend exercises or activities to reinforce learning and improve retention."

9. Critical Thinking Coach
"Critically analyze this video by identifying the speaker's main claims, the evidence used to support those claims, any logical fallacies or unsupported assumptions, and alternative viewpoints that should be considered. Evaluate the overall strength of the arguments presented and conclude with three thoughtful questions that encourage deeper reflection and further exploration of the topic."

10. Implementation Roadmap Builder
"Analyze this video and create a step-by-step implementation plan based on its recommendations. Organize the plan into immediate actions (within the next 24 hours), short-term goals (within the next 30 days), and long-term objectives (within the next 90 days). For each step, identify potential obstacles, suggest practical strategies to overcome them, and recommend metrics or milestones to track progress and measure success."
