2. Strict Questioning Tutor
​
Prompt:
“Act as a strict tutor for the material in these uploads. Do not give me direct answers. Instead, ask me one thought-provoking question at a time to test my comprehension of the sources. Wait for my response. If my logic contradicts the documents, point out the citation I missed and ask another question to guide me.”

3. Core Truths Builder
​Prompt:
“Synthesize all my uploaded materials. Strip away the complex terms. Break the arguments down into their fundamental, undeniable truths. Once we have those basics, show me step-by-step how the authors build their complex theories back up from them.”

4. Feynman Simplifier
​
Prompt:
“Explain the hardest concepts in these sources using the Feynman Technique.
→ Step 1: Explain it to a 12-year-old using an everyday analogy.
→ Step 2: Introduce the technical terms from the text, linking them back to the analogy.
→ Step 3: Ask me to explain it back to you, and score my accuracy based strictly on the source material.”

​
Prompt:
“Scan the uploaded case studies and books. Identify the top 3 decision-making frameworks used by the experts mentioned. For each framework, provide:
→ A brief definition.
→ A scenario where a beginner fails by ignoring this mindset.

6. Skill Transfer Bridge
​
Prompt:
“I understand [Concept A from Upload 1]. I am trying to grasp [Concept B from Upload 2]. Act as a learning strategist and map the overlapping concepts between these specific documents. 7. Real-World Simulator
​
Prompt:
“Act as a real-world simulator based entirely on the rules and data in my uploaded documents. Feed me difficult scenarios one at a time. I will tell you my action. Then:

​Prompt:
“I am going to explain my understanding of the uploaded research below. Act as a ruthless academic critic. Tear apart my explanation, point out edge cases from the documents I completely missed, expose my logical flaws, and challenge my assumptions. Do not hold back. My explanation: [Insert Explanation]”

Prompt:

Prompt:
