##10 Ready-to-Use Prompts for Teachers
"All 10 prompts + the best AI tools for teachers:👉 https://aitoolhd.com/ai-tools-for-teachers.html

01.Lesson Plan
You are an experienced [GRADE LEVEL] teacher. Create a complete lesson plan for [TOPIC]. Include: learning objectives (aligned to [STANDARD]), hook/warm-up (5 min), direct instruction (15 min), guided practice (10 min), independent practice (10 min), exit ticket, materials needed, and differentiation for advanced and struggling students.

02.Differentiated Text
You are a literacy specialist. Take the following text and rewrite it at 3 reading levels: grade level, 2 grades below, and 2 grades above. Maintain the same key information and vocabulary. Flag key terms in each version. [PASTE TEXT]

03.Quiz Questions (20)
You are a curriculum developer. Create a 20-question quiz on [TOPIC] for [GRADE LEVEL]. Mix: 10 multiple choice, 5 true/false, 3 short answer, 2 application questions. Include an answer key with explanations. Aligned to [STANDARD].

04.Parent Communication Email
You are a teacher communicating with parents. Write an email to parents about [TOPIC — behavior concern / academic progress / upcoming event]. Tone: professional but warm. Be specific, solution-oriented, and invite response. Under 200 words.

05.Rubric Creation
You are an assessment specialist. Create a detailed rubric for [ASSIGNMENT TYPE] for [GRADE LEVEL]. Criteria: [LIST 4-5 CRITERIA]. Performance levels: 4 (Exceeds), 3 (Meets), 2 (Approaching), 1 (Below). Each cell: specific, observable behaviors, not vague adjectives.

06.Student Feedback (5 Students)
You are an English teacher. Write constructive written feedback for 5 different student essays on [TOPIC]. Each student profile: [DESCRIBE BRIEFLY — struggling writer / average / strong / ESL / disengaged]. Each feedback: 1 specific strength, 1 specific improvement, 1 actionable next step. Under 100 words each.

07.IEP Accommodation Strategies
You are a special education specialist. For a student with [DISABILITY/NEED], suggest 10 classroom accommodation strategies for [SUBJECT]. For each: what it is, how to implement it, why it works, and any materials needed. Practical, not theoretical.

08.Class Discussion Questions
You are a Socratic seminar facilitator. Create 15 discussion questions for [TEXT/TOPIC] for [GRADE LEVEL]. Include: 5 recall questions (warm up), 5 analysis questions (building), 5 synthesis/evaluation questions (deeper thinking). Sequence them for a 45-minute seminar.

09.Project-Based Learning Design
You are a PBL curriculum designer. Design a 2-week project-based learning unit on [TOPIC] for [GRADE LEVEL]. Include: essential question, final product, daily structure, checkpoints, standards addressed, group roles, and assessment strategy.

010.Professional Development Plan
You are an instructional coach. Create a personal professional development plan for a teacher who wants to improve in [AREA]. Include: current state assessment, 3-month goals, specific strategies, resources (books, courses, communities), and how to measure progress.
