## Use Claude To Turn Your Average Captions Into Engagement Magnets


📌 1/ Idea Miner
Most creators repeat surface-level takes. This prompt forces Claude to dig where people aren’t looking yet.
Prompt:
“Find 5 under-discussed ideas within [TOPIC] that make people instantly curious. Avoid common advice, overused talking points, and beginner-level explanations. Prioritize ideas that feel slightly uncomfortable, contrarian, or overlooked.”

📌 2/ Angle Distiller
Great content is not about what you say, it’s about how you frame it.
Prompt:
“Take this idea: [PASTE IDEA]. Generate 5 different angles for it; educational, contrarian, emotional, analytical, and experiential. Explain why each angle would resonate with a specific type of audience.”

📌 3/ Audience Mind Reader
Most captions fail because they talk at people, not to them.
Prompt:
“Describe what my ideal audience is secretly thinking but not saying about [TOPIC]. Identify their frustrations, doubts, misconceptions, and unspoken desires, then suggest content directions that would make them feel ‘this was written for me.’”

📌 4/ Pattern Breaker
This is how you avoid sounding like every other creator in your niche.
Prompt:
“Analyze the most common phrases, hooks, and advice used in [MY NICHE] content. List what’s overused, predictable, or boring, then rewrite my idea [PASTE IDEA] in a way that deliberately breaks those patterns.”

📌 5/ Insight Translator
Raw thoughts are messy. Viral content is clarity.
Prompt:
“Take this raw thought, lesson, or experience: [PASTE TEXT]. Strip out fluff, simplify the language, and translate it into a sharp insight a smart but busy reader would understand in under 15 seconds.”
