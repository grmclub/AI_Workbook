The Claude Prompt Playbook

20 Claude Prompts I Use Every Week
1. The CEO Daily Planner
You are a world-class productivity strategist.
Help me create the most effective plan for today.
My goals:
[Goals]
My tasks:
[Tasks]
My meetings:
[Meetings]
My deadlines:
[Deadlines]
Create:
• My top 3 priorities
• An optimized schedule
• Tasks that can be delegated or automated
• Potential bottlenecks
• The highest-leverage actions
• A realistic definition of a successful day
Present everything in priority order.

2. The 10-Minute Research Brief

You are a professional research analyst.
Research the following topic:
[Topic]
Provide:
• Executive summary
• Key trends
• Important statistics
• Major companies or players
• Opportunities
• Risks and challenges
• What most people misunderstand
• Sources to explore further
Make the report concise and practical.

3. Untangle Any Idea
I am going to share an unstructured idea.
Your job is to:
• Identify the core idea
• Organize it logically
• Find missing pieces
• Point out weaknesses
• Suggest improvements
• Rewrite it into a clear framework
Idea:
[Paste idea]

4. Learn Any Topic Faster
You are an expert teacher.

Teach me:
[Topic]
Structure your response using:
• Simple explanation
• Key principles
• Real-world examples
• Common mistakes
• Practice exercises
• Memory shortcuts
• Summary
Assume I am intelligent but completely new to this topic.

5. The Decision Framework
Help me evaluate this decision:
[Situation]
Analyze:
• Benefits
• Risks
• Second-order consequences
• Best-case scenario
• Worst-case scenario
• Alternative options
Then recommend the most rational path and explain why.

6. Instant Writing Upgrade
Improve the following text.
Goals:

• More clarity
• More persuasion
• More brevity
• Better flow
• Stronger credibility
Text:
[Paste text]
Then explain the top 5 improvements you made.

7. Break Down Any Problem
Help me solve this problem:
[Problem]
Provide:
• Root causes
• Contributing factors
• Key constraints
• Potential solutions
• Pros and cons of each solution
• Recommended next steps
Think step-by-step.

8. Should I Pursue This Idea?
You are an experienced startup advisor.
Evaluate this idea:
[Idea]
Analyze:

• Market demand
• Target customer
• Competition
• Revenue potential
• Key risks
• Barriers to entry
• Likelihood of success
Give an honest recommendation.

[Paste information]

• Plain-English explanation
• Why it matters
• Practical applications
• What to remember
Use simple language.

[Niche]
