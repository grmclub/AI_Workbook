1. Durable
Durable is built for entrepreneurs who want to get online fast. You tell it your business name and what you do, and in ~30 seconds it generates a multi-page website (with copy, images, layout) ready for publishing. It also includes built-in tools like CRM, invoicing, analytics, and marketing features, so it's not just for showing your business — it helps you run it.
🔗 durable.co / durable.ai

2. TeleportHQ (AI Website Builder)
TeleportHQ’s AI Website Builder lets you create a responsive site purely from a text prompt (often via ChatGPT style input). It then gives you editable pages, components, and even lets you export production-ready clean code (HTML, CSS, JS). It’s great if you want a mix of AI automation + developer flexibility.
🔗 teleporthq.io

3. 10Web AI Website Builder
10Web offers an AI-powered WordPress solution, where you can spin up an AI-generated WP site (layout + content) and then fine-tune with Elementor. It also includes automatic hosting, backups, and performance optimizations (like speed boosting).
🔗 10web.io

4. Wix AI / Wix ADI (Artificial Design Intelligence)
Wix’s AI tools ask you a few questions (what your business is, desired style, features you want), then builds a full site (layout + content) in minutes. You still retain full visual control to edit, drag things around, change styles, etc. It’s ideal for non-designers who want a polished site fast.
🔗 wix.com

5. Hostinger AI Website Builder
Hostinger’s tool (which includes many of Zyro’s AI features) builds websites via AI by prompting you for your site type, aesthetic preferences, etc. It also offers AI content generation (AI Writer), AI heatmap for optimizing conversions, and is tightly integrated with its hosting + domain services.
🔗 hostinger.com

6. Framer AI
Framer’s AI approach is “text to website.” You describe (in sentences) what kind of site you want (“a landing page for my cafe”, etc.), and Framer generates a starter site which you can then fully customize visually. It's particularly good for designers or creatives who want both speed and control.
🔗 framer.com

## 7 Prompts That Can Build a Website From Scratch Using Gemini 3.5 Pro

📌 1. Website Architecture Intelligence Prompt
(Build the skeleton before you decorate the house.)
You're a senior conversion-focused website strategist.
Based on the business information below, design a complete website architecture optimized for clarity, authority, and conversions.
Include:
– Required core pages
– Section breakdown for each page
– Logical content flow
– Primary and secondary CTAs
– Conversion objective per page
– Trust-building elements needed
Business Details:
– Industry: [Insert]
– Target Audience: [Insert]
– Core Offer: [Insert]
– Price Point: [Insert]
– Primary Goal (Leads / Sales / Authority / Bookings): [Insert]
– Unique Advantage: [Insert]
👉This ensures your site is built with strategy, not vibes.

📌 2. Brand Positioning & Messaging Engine
(If your positioning is weak, your website won’t convert.)
Analyze the business details below and define:
– Brand voice (tone, personality, communication style)
– Market positioning statement
– Unique value proposition
– Core emotional driver
– Brand promise
– Messaging pillars
Ensure the positioning feels premium, differentiated, and aligned with my audience psychology.
Business Details:
[Insert full business summary]
👉This creates consistency across your entire website.

📌 3. Homepage Conversion Framework Prompt
(Your homepage is not decoration. It’s persuasion.)
You're a CRO (conversion rate optimization) specialist.
Build a high-converting homepage structure tailored to my business.
Include:
– Headline formula with emotional trigger
– Subheadline logic
– Authority positioning block
– Problem agitation section
– Solution breakdown
– Benefits hierarchy
– Objection-handling elements
– Proof placement strategy
– CTA placement strategy
Business Details:
[Insert]

📌 5. Full Website Copy Generator (Aligned With Strategy)
Using the defined brand voice and structure above, generate complete persuasive copy for:
– Home
– About
– Services / Products
– Contact
Requirements:
– Clear and concise
– Emotionally engaging
– Objection-aware
– Action-oriented
– Consistent with positioning
Maintain this tone: [Insert tone style]
Avoid: [Insert words or tone to avoid]
👉This prevents robotic or generic website copy.

📌 6. Premium Visual Direction Blueprint
(Design influences trust before words do.)
Provide a detailed visual direction strategy for this website.
Include:
– Recommended layout style (minimal, bold, editorial, etc.)
– Color palette suggestions (with psychology explanation)
– Typography pairings
– Spacing and layout principles
– Visual hierarchy strategy
– Image style guidance
– UI inspiration direction
Brand Personality: [Insert]
Target Audience Type: [Insert]
👉This gives you a designer’s thinking, not random Canva colors.

📌 7. SEO Infrastructure Optimization Prompt
(Traffic doesn’t happen by accident.)
You're an SEO strategist.
Optimize my website content for organic search visibility.
Provide:
– Title tag recommendations per page
– Meta descriptions (CTR optimized)
– Primary and secondary keyword suggestions
– Internal linking structure
– Schema markup suggestions
– On-page optimization checklist
Industry: [Insert]
Target Region: [Insert]
👉This ensures your website is discoverable, not invisible.

## 8 Claude Prompts to help you Create Premium-Looking Websites like Professional Web Design Agencies 👇

1. The Premium Website Builder
Act as a premium website specialist. I want a stunning, interactive website for [business type]. Give me the ideal page structure, the design elements that create a premium feel, and copy that encourages visitors to take action.

2. The First Impression Engineer
Act as a website first-impression specialist. Design a hero section for my website that captures attention quickly. Create a headline, subheadline, and call-to-action that encourages visitors to keep exploring.

3. The Conversion Page Copywriter
Act as a conversion optimization specialist. Write my complete homepage for [offer] aimed at [audience]. Include a compelling hook, problem section, solution, trust-building elements, and a call-to-action that encourages visitors to learn more or get started.

4. The Portfolio Creator
Act as a portfolio website specialist. Design a portfolio for [profession] that highlights my best work. Create a project showcase, an About section that builds credibility, and a contact section that makes it easy for potential clients to reach out.

5. The Service Page Copywriter
Act as a service page copywriting specialist. Write my complete service page for [service] priced at [$X]. Include a headline that speaks to my ideal clients, a clear explanation of the value I provide, and a booking CTA that encourages inquiries.

6. The About Page Storyteller
Act as an About page storytelling specialist. Write my complete About page for [business]. Create an introduction that builds trust, tell my story in a relatable way, and highlight what makes my business different.

7. The Mobile Optimizer
Act as a mobile website optimization specialist. Review my website and recommend improvements for mobile devices. Suggest layout changes, better button sizing, faster-loading design choices, and ways to improve the overall user experience.

8. The Website Copy System
Act as a complete website copywriting specialist. Write all the copy for my [business type] website, including the homepage, About page, Services section, testimonial placeholders, FAQ section, and footer CTA. Make the writing clear, persuasive, and consistent throughout the site.
