🚨ChatGPT can now help you create, illustrate, and publish a children's book without hiring a writer or illustrator.
These 9 prompts can take you from idea to Amazon-ready book in just a few days.

1. Book Idea Generator
Prompt:
Act as a bestselling children's book author, child psychologist, and publishing expert. Generate 20 original children's book ideas for children aged [AGE RANGE] around the theme of [TOPIC]. Each idea should include a memorable title, a relatable main character, the key lesson or skill being taught, a short plot summary, and a clear explanation of why parents would buy the book. Prioritize concepts that are emotionally engaging, highly marketable, and capable of becoming successful picture books or series.

2. Story Outline Creator
Prompt:
Create a complete [NUMBER OF PAGES]-page children's picture book outline for children aged [AGE RANGE]. The story should focus on [TOPIC] and teach [LESSON]. The main character is [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION]. Develop a compelling beginning, middle, and ending that keeps young readers engaged while naturally reinforcing the lesson. For each page, describe the story event and provide a detailed illustration brief that captures the setting, actions, emotions, and visual storytelling elements.

3. Manuscript Writer
Prompt:
Using the outline above, write the complete children's book manuscript for readers aged [AGE RANGE]. Keep the language appropriate for the target audience, using approximately [WORD COUNT RANGE] words per page. The tone should be [TONE], the reading experience should be enjoyable aloud, and the main character's voice should remain consistent throughout. Build emotional connection, curiosity, and momentum across the story, ending with a memorable conclusion that reinforces [LESSON] in a natural and satisfying way.

4. Character Sheet Creator
Prompt:
Act as a professional children's book illustrator and character designer. Create a detailed character sheet for [CHARACTER NAME]. The character is a [SPECIES/TYPE], aged [AGE], with the personality traits [TRAITS]. Include physical appearance, clothing, accessories, color palette, facial expressions, body language, distinctive features, and artistic style. The design should suit a [ART STYLE] children's picture book and remain visually consistent across every illustration in the series.

5. Illustration Generator
Prompt:
Create a professional children's book illustration using the character description below and the scene details provided. Character: [CHARACTER SHEET]. Scene: [SCENE DESCRIPTION]. Illustration Style: [ART STYLE]. Color Palette: [COLOR STYLE]. The artwork should be visually consistent with previous illustrations, emotionally expressive, age-appropriate for [AGE RANGE], and designed to feel like a professionally published picture book.

6. Cover Creator
Prompt:
Design a professional children's book cover for the title "[TITLE]" with the subtitle "[SUBTITLE]" (if applicable). The target audience is [AGE RANGE]. The story focuses on [TOPIC] and teaches [LESSON]. Feature [MAIN CHARACTER] prominently on the cover and use a [ART STYLE] illustration style. The cover should be visually striking, easy to understand at thumbnail size, emotionally appealing to parents, and compelling enough to encourage clicks and purchases.

7. Amazon KDP Description Writer
Prompt:
Write a high-converting Amazon KDP description for a children's book titled "[TITLE]". The book is intended for children aged [AGE RANGE] and focuses on [TOPIC]. It helps children learn [LESSON] through the story of [MAIN CHARACTER]. Create a description that highlights the emotional and educational benefits, addresses what parents care about most, and motivates them to purchase the book. Include a strong opening hook, benefit-driven copy, and naturally integrated keywords related to the topic.

8. Series Expansion Prompt
Prompt:
Based on the book titled "[TITLE]", create a series plan containing [NUMBER] additional books. Each book should feature the same core character while teaching a different lesson related to [OVERALL THEME]. For each title, provide the lesson, a short story concept, and an explanation of how it strengthens the overall brand. The goal is to create a cohesive children's book series that encourages repeat purchases and long-term audience growth.

9. Design your book layout
Use Canva or Book Brush to:
• Combine your text with illustrations
• Create consistent formatting
• Design a eye-catching cover
• Export as print-ready PDF
Most people skip this step & wonder why their books look unprofessional.
