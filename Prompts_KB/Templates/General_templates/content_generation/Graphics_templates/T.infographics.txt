Prompt:
Create a high-value hand-drawn sketchnote infographic about: [TOPIC]
The infographic should visually explain the topic in a way that is educational, engaging, and instantly understandable. Structure the content like a professional visual thinking session or graphic recording.
Style & Design Requirements:
- Use a pristine white paper background (no lines or textures).
- Use a hand-drawn “graphic recording” / “visual thinking” style.
- Draw using black fine-liner ink for outlines, text, arrows, and illustrations.
- Add simple accent shading using only teal, orange, and muted red markers.
- Make the layout feel clean, premium, and highly organized.
Composition:
- Place the main title in the center inside a bold 3D-style rectangular box.
- Arrange key ideas radially around the title.
- Include:
- Simple doodles
- Business and technology icons
- Stick figures
- Charts and graphs
- Symbols and metaphors
- Containers, banners, and callout boxes
- Connect related ideas using arrows, flow lines, and visual hierarchy.
Content Structure:
- Automatically break the topic into:
- Core concepts
- Key insights
- Frameworks
- Processes
- Examples
- Actionable takeaways
- Statistics or trends (if relevant)
- Prioritize clarity, depth, and practical value over decoration.
- Make the infographic feel like a high-end workshop summary or consulting whiteboard.
Typography:
- Use clear handwritten ALL-CAPS lettering.
- Ensure all text is legible, distinct, and neatly spaced.
- Use varied text sizes for hierarchy and emphasis.
Output Requirements:
- A4 portrait layout
- Dense with valuable information but never cluttered
- Balanced negative space
- Visually dynamic and highly shareable
- The final result should look like a premium viral sketchnote infographic created by an expert visual strategist.
