Pitch Deck
A pitch deck is an investor-facing sales tool. It answers why this business will make money and why investors should write a check. 

    Format: Visual PDF, Canva, or PowerPoint presentation (usually 10–20 slides).
    Core Elements:
        Problem: What is fundamentally broken in the market?
        Solution: How your company uniquely fixes it.
        Market Size: The total addressable market (TAM).
        Traction & Validation: Proof that people want this (e.g., revenue, user growth).
        Business Model: How you make money.
        The Ask: How much funding you are raising and what you will do with it.

##Best Practices & Templates

--What is the 10/20/30 rule for pitch deck?
The idea of the 10/20/30 rule is easy to understand, which is summed up in three points. Your presentation should consist of no more than 10 slides. Your presentation should last no longer than 20 minutes. The text on each slide should be no lower than 30 points in size

--What are the 3 C's of pitching?
The 3 C's of Business Pitch: Clear, Concise and Compelling.

--What is the 7 7 7 rule for presentations?
It suggests limiting each slide to a maximum of 7 lines of text, with no more than 7 words per line, and using a font size no smaller than 7 points.

--What are 5 key elements of a pitch?
5 key elements of the perfect sales P-I-T-C-H

    P- Problem solver (Show how you have a solution that works) ...
    I- Inspire an action (Have an end goal in mind that you're leading them to) ...
    T- Tailor it to the audience. ...
    C- Concise (be quick and to the point) ...
    H- Heart (Be passionate!)

    Title – Includes the business name, the presenter’s name, contacts, etc.
    Problem/Opportunity – Highlights pain points or unmet needs of customers you aim to solve.
    Value Proposition – Articulates the value or benefits of your product or service.
    Underlying Magic – Explains the key technology that goes into your product or service offers.
    Business Model – Describes how you plan to generate revenue.
    Go-to-Market Plan – Outlines your strategy for bringing your product or service to market, e.g., marketing and sales plan.
    Competitive Analysis – Explains how your business is positioned to compete and capture market share.
    Management Team – Highlights your management team’s skills, experience, and expertise that will drive the success of your business.
    Financial Projections and Key Metrics – Highlights your business’s financial viability and potential profitability.
    Current Status, Accomplishments to Date, Timeline, and Use of Funds – Provides an overview of your current business status, any accomplishments or milestones achieved to date, the timeline for future milestones, and how you plan to use the funds you seek.
