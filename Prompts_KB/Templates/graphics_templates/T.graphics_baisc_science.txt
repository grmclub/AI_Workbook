/list prompts to generate graphics to teach and explain high school science concepts 

Here is a list of prompt concepts designed to generate educational graphics for high school science, ranging from biology and chemistry to physics and environmental science.
The prompts are structured to define the specific subject, the required explanatory style (e.g., cutaway, flow diagram, isometric), and the aesthetic (e.g., clean digital illustration, vector design, photorealistic macro).

##Biology
• Cellular Structure (The Cutaway):
"A highly detailed, 3D cutaway diagram of a eukaryotic animal cell. The graphic clearly labels and visualizes all major organelles, including the nucleus, mitochondria (shown with inner folds), ribosomes, endoplasmic reticulum, and Golgi apparatus. Soft, diffused lighting, clean digital render, vibrant color coding for each organelle against a subtle background."
• DNA Replication (The Step-by-Step Flow):
"An infographic illustrating the DNA replication fork. The diagram uses an isometric perspective to show the double helix unzipping, with distinct visualization of the leading strand, lagging strand (showing Okazaki fragments), DNA polymerase, helicase, and primase enzymes. Simplified, friendly color palette with clear, bold text labels."
• Mitosis vs. Meiosis (Side-by-Side Comparison):
"A split-panel comparison chart illustrating Mitosis and Meiosis. The left side shows the four stages of Mitosis (Prophase to Telophase), and the right side shows Meiosis I and II. The graphic highlights key differences like homologous chromosome pairing and crossing over. Clean vector illustration, educational layout."

##Chemistry
• Atomic Structure & Electron Configuration (The Bohr Model):
"A graphic illustrating the Bohr model for a Neon atom (Ne-20). It shows a central nucleus with protons and neutrons, surrounded by concentric electron shells (2 in the first shell, 8 in the second). The diagram uses glowing points for electrons and contrasting colors for protons and neutrons. Clean, schematic design."

• Chemical Bonding (Covalent vs. Ionic):
"A dynamic 3D graphic demonstrating the difference between Ionic and Covalent bonds. On one side, Sodium (Na) transfers an electron to Chlorine (Cl), forming ions. On the other side, two Oxygen atoms share electrons. Use stylized 3D spheres with soft shadows and clear directional arrows. Laboratory aesthetic."

• The Periodic Table (Interactive Layered Design):
"An isometric, layered infographic of the Periodic Table of Elements. The elements are color-coded into families (Alkali Metals, Noble Gases, etc.). When highlighted, individual groups are subtly elevated in 3D to show trends (like Electronegativity increasing up and to the right). Modern UI/UX design, clean interface look."

##Physics
• Newton's Laws of Motion (The Force Diagram):
"An isometric diagram illustrating Newton's Three Laws of Motion using everyday examples. (1) A ball resting on the ground (Inertia). (2) A person pushing a car vs. a truck (F=ma). (3) A rocket launching (Action/Reaction). The graphic includes vectors, force arrows, and simple mathematical formulas integrated into the scene. Bright, clear vector illustration."

• Electromagnetic Spectrum (The Continuum):
"A continuous flow diagram visualizing the entire Electromagnetic Spectrum, ordered by wavelength and frequency. From left (Radio) to right (Gamma), with a central zoomed-in 'Visible Light' rainbow. The graphic uses illustrative icons above each section (e.g., a radio, a microwave, an X-ray machine). Clean digital art, glowing accents."

• Circuits (Schematic to Real-World):
"A comparison graphic showing a simple electrical circuit. On top, a standard schematic diagram using technical symbols (battery, resistor, switch). Below, a photorealistic 3D rendering of the physical setup with wires, a 9V battery, and a glowing LED. Arrows show the flow of conventional current. Industrial blueprint style."

##Earth & Environmental Science

• The Carbon Cycle (Global Systems):
"A comprehensive, global isometric diagram illustrating the Carbon Cycle. The visualization connects spheres: the Atmosphere, Hydrosphere (ocean), Biosphere (forests/animals), and Geosphere (fossil fuels). It details pathways like photosynthesis, respiration, decomposition, combustion, and ocean absorption. Blue and green color palette, clean digital map style."

• Plate Tectonics (Cross-Section):
"A 3D cross-section diagram showing the layers of the Earth (Crust, Mantle, Core) and the mechanism of Plate Tectonics. The visualization highlights three boundary types: Divergent (mid-ocean ridge), Convergent (subduction zone with volcano), and Transform (fault line). Arrows indicate magma convection currents. Geological texture render."

• The Greenhouse Effect (Atmospheric Science):
"An educational infographic explaining the Greenhouse Effect. Sunlight (UV radiation) arrives and warms the Earth's surface. Infrared radiation is emitted back but becomes trapped by a visualized layer of greenhouse gases, heating the atmosphere. Simple, clean geometry and clear directional lighting to emphasize temperature differences."
