##5 Secret Codes For Claude

#1: /ghost
what it does:
Makes any AI-written text sound like a real human wrote it — strips out robotic phrasing and AI patterns completely. Use it before sending a cold email or any copy that needs to sound like you.

#2: /redteam
what it does:
Claude attacks your idea from every angle a critic, competitor, or skeptic would use. It's a military term for "find the holes before the enemy does." Use it before any public launch or pitch.

#3: /premortem
what it does:
Imagines your project already failed 6 months from now, then works backwards to figure out why. Forces you to find the cracks before you're in too deep.

#4: /tripwire
what it does:
Identifies the single thing most likely to blow up your entire plan. Not a list of risks — just the one that would kill everything. Huge for decisions with a lot of moving pieces.

#5: /ultrathink
what it does:
Type this word in any prompt and Claude shifts into its deepest reasoning mode — slower, more thorough, and more deliberate than a normal response. It's the difference between Claude giving you an answer and Claude actually thinking through the problem.
