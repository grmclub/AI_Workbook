2/ Deep Research Deck

3/ Complete Presentation With Script
"Create a fully ready presentation on [insert topic], AND write a complete spoken script I can read while presenting. Ensure the script matches the tone, pacing, and emotional flow of the slides. Include pauses, emphasis, storytelling cues, and delivery instructions."

4/ Simplified Explanation Builder
"Build a presentation on [insert topic] designed for beginners. Break down complex concepts into simple explanations, analogies, and step-by-step logic. Make the content so clear that even a 10-year-old can grasp the full topic."

5/ Storytelling Master Deck

📌 1 | Lay the Foundation: Strategic Slide Blueprint

>> Prompt Template:
"I'm preparing a presentation on [insert topic] for [insert audience type - e.g., clients, students, execs]. Create a slide-by-slide breakdown (max 10 slides) with a logical flow: opener, main ideas, conclusion, and CTA. Ensure it persuades or educates clearly."

🔥Why this works: It builds structure before design, saving hours.

📌 2 | Visual Direction Without Designing

>> Prompt Template:
"Based on this slide outline → [insert or paste your outline], recommend suitable visuals, charts, icons, or simple illustrations for each slide. Focus on clarity and professionalism. No busy designs, just elements that support the message."

🔥Pro Tip: Ask Claude to reference real-world examples or common analogies to make concepts visual.

📌 3 | Transform Messy Notes Into Polished Slides

>> Prompt Template:
"Here are my rough notes for a talk on [insert subject] → [paste notes]. Turn them into concise, impactful slide content with strong headings and supporting bullets. Avoid paragraph text, make it presentation-ready."

🔥Extra Angle: Ask for two versions; one casual, one formal, based on your audience type.


📌 4 | Add Influence: Data, Quotes & Openers

>> Prompt Template:
"Enrich this presentation outline → [paste outline] with powerful hooks, surprising statistics, or relevant quotes per slide. Tailor them to a tone of [choose one: expert, thought-leader, motivational, direct]."

🔥Why this matters: Hooks and data boost credibility and engagement instantly.
