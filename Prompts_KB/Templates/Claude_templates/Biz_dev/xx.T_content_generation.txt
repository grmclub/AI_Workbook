Prompt 3: "Edit this until it's better than 90% of books on Amazon"
"Act as a professional book editor who has edited 200+ published books for traditional publishing houses. I'm going to give you my completed manuscript chapter by chapter. For each chapter perform a three-round edit: Round 1 — Structural Edit: Flag any sections that are out of order, repetitive, or don't serve the reader. Remove anything that slows the pace. Ensure every chapter has a clear purpose and payoff. Round 2 — Line Edit: Tighten every sentence. Replace weak verbs with strong ones. Cut filler words like 'very,' 'really,' 'just,' and 'basically.' Eliminate every AI-sounding phrase. Make sure it reads like a human expert wrote it on their best day. Round 3 — Reader Experience Edit: Read it from the buyer's perspective. Flag any section where a reader might get bored, confused, or tempted to put the book down. Add hooks, transitions, or examples wherever engagement dips. After all 3 rounds give me the polished version plus a summary of every major change you made. Here is Chapter 1: [PASTE CHAPTER]."
