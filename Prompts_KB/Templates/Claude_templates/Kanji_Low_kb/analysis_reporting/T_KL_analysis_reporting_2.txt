##Thinking partner_1

1. Stop summarising. Start deciding.
"I need to make a decision about [situation]. Don't give me a pros and cons list. Act as a senior advisor who has seen this exact decision go wrong before. Tell me the one thing most people overlook when they're in this position, the question I should be asking but aren't, and what you would actually do if this were your business."


2. Turn a vague goal into a real plan
"My goal is [X]. I've said this before and not followed through. Don't build me a motivation plan. Instead, identify the 3 most likely reasons someone with my background fails at this specific goal — then redesign the goal so that failure becomes structurally harder than success."

3. Use Claude as a thinking partner, not a content machine
"I'm working through a problem I can't seem to crack: [describe it]. Don't solve it for me yet. Ask me the 5 questions a world-class consultant would ask before offering a single recommendation. Then after I answer, tell me what my answers reveal about where the real problem actually is."

4. Get brutal honest feedback on your work
"Here is [my email / proposal / landing page / content piece]. I don't want encouragement. I want you to read this as a cynical, highly experienced person in this space who has seen a hundred versions of this before. Tell me the moment you would have stopped reading and exactly why. Then tell me what the top 1% version of this looks like."

5. Extract a system from your own brain
"I want to document how I do [task or process you're good at] so I can delegate it, automate it, or teach it. Interview me about it. Ask me one question at a time. Don't move to the next question until I've answered. When we're done, turn my answers into a repeatable system with clear steps, decision points, and quality standards."

6. Find the leak in your business
"Here is a snapshot of my business right now: [revenue, offers, how you get clients, how you deliver, your team]. Don't tell me what's working. Tell me where businesses at this exact stage typically leak the most money, time, and opportunity — and which of those leaks you can already see in what I've described."

7. Pressure test your idea before you build it
"I'm planning to [launch / build / invest in / pitch] this: [describe your idea]. Before I go any further, act as the smartest person in the room who wants this to succeed but won't let me deceive myself. Give me the 3 most likely reasons this fails within 90 days — not generic risks, but the specific failure modes for this exact idea given what I've told you."

8. Build your unfair advantage into everything you create
"Here is my background, experience, and what I'm building: [paste context]. I want every piece of content, every offer, and every conversation I have to carry the weight of what makes me genuinely different. Identify my actual unfair advantage — not the one I'd put on a LinkedIn bio, but the one that makes competitors quietly uncomfortable. Then show me how to embed it into [my next post / proposal / pitch] without it sounding like I'm bragging."

Prompt 2 — "Write 5 cold DMs to [Your Target region][niche] owners. Under 75 words each. Open with the specific U.S. dollar amount AI saves them on payroll. Frame me as a [Your role] with Fortune 500 experience. End with: 'Want me to send a 2-minute demo?'"

Gets your first discovery calls booked without sounding desperate.

Prompt 3 — "Design a 15-minute discovery call script for [niche] owner. Open with: 'What's the most expensive role in your business right now?' Map 5 pain-uncovering questions, 2 objection handlers for 'I'm not sure AI can do that,' and a soft pitch to a 30-day pilot at setup fee plus monthly recurring." 

Handles the call without freezing up. Biggest confidence killer for new service business owners — solved.

Prompt 4 — "Write a 1-page proposal for a [niche] business owner. Their pain: a costly role they want to automate. My solution: pre-built AI tools relevant to their workflow. Pricing: setup fee plus monthly recurring. 30-day pilot, cancel anytime. Make the value obvious in the first 3 lines."

Makes the offer feel real and easy to say yes to. Nobody signs a 10-page proposal from someone they just met.


Prompt 5 — Build me a 90-day plan to land my first clients for an AI implementation service while managing my current commitments. 

Include: business registration steps, payment setup, client onboarding workflow, and key milestones. Goal: reach sustainable monthly recurring revenue by day 90.


The bridge from idea to actual execution, with the boring but important stuff already mapped out.

I've been in business for 25 years. I've never seen the cost of starting a service business this low. Not even close. Five prompts, a laptop, and the willingness to actually reach out to people — that's the whole barrier now.
