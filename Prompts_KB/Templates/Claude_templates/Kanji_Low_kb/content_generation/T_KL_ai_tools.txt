30 Days Guide to Build & Launch Your First App (With Zero Coding):

The gap between you and a launched app is 30 days, not 3 years.

Week 1 — Pick the idea & build the MVP with Claude.
This week is about picking one tiny idea and shipping a working version with Claude Code.
Your checklist for week 1:
🌶️ Pick one tiny app idea & write a one-page PRD (what it does, who it helps, what you charge)
🌶️ Paste the PRD into Claude Code. Let it scaffold Next js + Supabase auth + Stripe checkout
🌶️ Push to GitHub and deploy on Vercel. Ugly is fine, working is the goal
By the end of this week: You have a LIVE app on a real domain.

Week 2 — Market it before it's perfect.
Start posting while the app is rough. People follow the JOURNEY, not the polish. Every bug becomes a Reel.
Your checklist for week 2:
🌺 Post short build-in-public videos on IG, TikTok & YT Shorts
🌺 Write LinkedIn posts about what you're learning (one lesson per post)
🌺 Share carousels breaking down your build on IG

Week 3 — Get your first paying users.
DM the people engaging with your content and offer a FREE TRIAL in exchange for feedback.
Your checklist for week 3:
🌐 DM 30 people from your followers for review (no pitch, just ask)
🌐 Share a 5-7 min YouTube demo — the asset every ad and post points back to
🌐 Set up Stripe payment links so people can pay you in one click
By the end of this week you have REAL users, REAL feedback, and your first revenue hitting Stripe.

Week 4 — Make it properly scalable.
Now that paying users are using it, lock down the infra so nothing breaks at 2am. This is where MOST solo founders skip steps and pay for it later. Don't.
Your checklist for week 4:
🌐 Add Sentry for error tracking & PostHog for analytics — so you actually see what users do and where things break
🌐 Lock down Supabase RLS, env vars, and Stripe webhooks (the boring stuff that saves you from getting hacked)
🌐 Set up Resend for transactional emails & Trigger dev for background jobs — so the app runs itself while you sleep
