Here are 8 Gemini prompts that can create 10 viral videos in 10 minutes:

1/ Hook Opener

Prompt: “You are a YouTube editor who has worked on videos with 10M+ views. Your job is to write the first 15 seconds of a video about [TOPIC — e.g. "I gave 100 strangers $1,000 each"].

The hook must do THREE things simultaneously:
1. Create an open loop the viewer cannot mentally close without watching
2. Show — don't tell — the stakes (use a visual or number, never the word "incredible" or "amazing")
3. End on an incomplete sentence or unanswered question

Output format:
— SPOKEN WORDS (with delivery note in brackets)
— ON-SCREEN TEXT (font size: large/medium/small)
— VISUAL CUE (what the camera shows)
— SOUND EFFECT (describe the audio bed)
— WHY THIS HOOK WORKS (2 sentences max)

Do not use the words: crazy, insane, unbelievable, epic, amazing.”


2/ B-roll director

Prompt: “You are a senior video editor at a top-tier YouTube production studio. I'm going to give you a script. Your job is to build a shot-by-shot B-roll breakdown.

Rules:
— Maximum 2.5 seconds per shot before a cut or motion change
— Every shot must have an EMOTIONAL INTENT (curiosity / tension / relief / excitement)
— Alternate between wide, medium, and close-up shots — never two wides in a row
— Flag any moment that needs a "pattern interrupt" (sound design shift, zoom punch, freeze frame)

For each shot output:
SHOT # | TIMECODE | SHOT TYPE | CAMERA MOTION | ON-SCREEN TEXT | EMOTIONAL INTENT | EDITOR NOTE

[PASTE YOUR SCRIPT HERE]”

3/ Thumbnail designer

Prompt: “You are a YouTube thumbnail strategist. Your thumbnails average 8–12% CTR. Design a thumbnail for this video: [TITLE — e.g. "I Ate Only McDonald's For 30 Days"].

Apply these rules:
— The human face (if present) must show ONE clear emotion — no neutral expressions
— Max 4 words of text, minimum 80pt font, high contrast against background
— Use the "curiosity gap" principle: the thumbnail should raise a question the title doesn't answer
— Colours must create contrast against YouTube's white and dark backgrounds

Output:
1. THUMBNAIL CONCEPT A — describe scene, face, text, colours, focal point
2. THUMBNAIL CONCEPT B — an alternative angle or emotion
3. WHY A WILL OUTPERFORM B (be specific about psychology)
4. GEMINI IMAGE GEN PROMPT — ready to paste, photorealistic style, under 100 words.”

4/ Full video script

Prompt: “You are a YouTube scriptwriter. Your videos consistently hold 60%+ audience retention. Write a full script for: [TITLE — e.g. "I Survived On $1 A Day For 30 Days"].

Script architecture:
— 0:00–0:15 — Hook: open a loop, show the stakes visually, end incomplete
— 0:15–1:00 — Setup: establish the rules, introduce the person, seed 3 future payoffs
— 1:00–3:30 — Rising action: escalate difficulty every 90 seconds, drop a "mini-reveal" at each escalation
— 3:30–4:30 — Crisis or twist: the moment everything nearly falls apart
— 4:30–4:50 — Resolution: pay off all 3 seeded payoffs from the setup
— 4:50–5:00 — CTA: one ask only, tied emotionally to the story

For every 60 seconds of script, include:
[RETENTION NOTE] — what keeps viewers watching through this section

Banned phrases: "smash that like button", "without further ado", "in today's video", "let's get started"

5/ Retention surgeon

Prompt: “You are a YouTube retention analyst. You've reviewed thousands of videos using analytics data. Audit this script and find every point where a viewer's brain will decide "I've seen enough."

For each problem, categorise it as one of:
— PREDICTABILITY DROP (viewer knows what's coming next)
— PACING DRAG (too long on one idea)
— STAKES COLLAPSE (stakes feel lowered or resolved too early)
— RELEVANCE BREAK (viewer loses the "why should I care")

For each issue, rewrite the section using ONE of these fixes:
— Open loop injection
— Unexpected number or stat drop
— Perspective flip
— Micro-cliffhanger

Show BEFORE / AFTER for each. End with a PREDICTED RETENTION SCORE improvement estimate (e.g. "+8–12% average view duration").

[PASTE SCRIPT HERE]”

6/ Title & SEO engine

Prompt “You are a YouTube SEO strategist and title copywriter. Generate 10 titles for a video about [TOPIC — e.g. "extreme budget meal prep"].

Each title must use ONE of these proven CTR frameworks (label which framework you used):
— THE CHALLENGE: "I [did extreme thing] for [time period]"
— THE CONTRAST: "[Big number] [thing] vs [opposite thing]"
— THE REVELATION: "The truth about [topic] nobody tells you"
— THE STAKES: "I almost [lost/failed/quit] because of [specific thing]"
— THE RESULT: "[Specific outcome] in [specific timeframe]"

For each title also output:
— SEARCH INTENT (what is the viewer actually looking for?)
— EMOTIONAL TRIGGER (the psychological pull)
— WEAKNESS (what could make this underperform)

Then rank all 10 by CTR potential and name your top pick with a one-paragraph defence.”

7/ Short-form repurpose machine

Prompt: You are a short-form content strategist who repurposes long YouTube videos into viral clips. Extract 5 short-form videos from this script. Each clip must be 45–60 seconds.

For each clip:

PLATFORM FIT — which performs best on: Reels / TikTok / Shorts (and why)
HOOK (first 3 words spoken — must create pattern interrupt without context)
SCRIPT — the full spoken words, tightened for short-form pacing
CUT LIST — on-screen text at each visual cut, max 5 words per line
CAPTION — 2 punchy lines + 1 question to drive comments + 5 hashtags
THUMBNAIL FRAME — describe the exact still frame to use as cover

Rule: no clip can start with "In this video" or reference the long-form video. Each clip must be completely self-contained.

[PASTE LONG-FORM SCRIPT HERE]”

8/ 10-video content calendar

Prompt: “You are a YouTube channel strategist. Build a 10-video content calendar for a channel in this niche: [NICHE — e.g. "personal finance for Australian millennials"].

The 10 videos must include this mix:
— 3 x Search-driven videos (people are already looking for this)
— 3 x Trend-driven videos (timely, shareable, newsworthy angle)
— 2 x Authority videos (positions the creator as the go-to expert)
— 2 x Viral-potential videos (high shareability, emotional or shocking angle)

For each video output:
TITLE | CONTENT TYPE | ONE-LINE PREMISE | EMOTIONAL HOOK | STORY ARC (3 beats) | THUMBNAIL CONCEPT | MONETISATION SIGNAL (brand deal category, affiliate angle, or product tie-in)

End with: which 3 videos to film FIRST and why — based on effort-to-impact ratio.”
