##content generation prompts

1. Content prompts that build your strategy for you.
Act as a senior content strategist and create a 90-day Instagram content strategy for a creator in [NICHE] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Include weekly themes, content formats, and authority-building post ideas.
Generate 30 high-performing content ideas for a creator in [NICHE]. For each idea include a hook and explain why the idea would attract attention.
Turn the topic [TOPIC] into 10 scroll-stopping reel hooks that maximise curiosity and watch time.


2. Understand your audience better than they understand themselves.
Identify 10 frustrations experienced by [TARGET AUDIENCE] and explain the emotional triggers behind each problem.
Suggest three unique positioning strategies that would help a creator in [NICHE] stand out from competitors.
Create a detailed audience persona for [TARGET AUDIENCE], including motivations, fears, desires, and spending behaviour.

3. Build a brand that actually means something.
Write a brand positioning statement using this format: "I help [AUDIENCE] achieve [RESULT] without [COMMON PROBLEM]."
Rewrite this Instagram bio so it sounds clearer, more confident, and authority-driven: [BIO].
Generate 10 tagline ideas for a brand operating in the [NICHE] industry.

4. Prompts that move people to buy.
Generate 10 lead magnet ideas that creators in [NICHE] could offer to attract potential clients.
Turn the service [SERVICE] into three irresistible offer packages with clear outcomes.
Generate 10 marketing hooks that could be used to promote [PRODUCT].

5. Stop being busy. Start getting things done.
Design a weekly workflow system for a creator managing content creation, marketing, and client work.
Turn this task list into a clear weekly execution plan: [TASK LIST].
Create a content batching system that allows a creator to produce one month of content in a single day.

6. Turn what you know into income.
Generate 10 ways a creator in [NICHE] can monetise their audience.
Suggest five digital product ideas based on this expertise: [SKILL].
Generate 10 services creators could offer to brands in this industry.

7. Build something that sells while you sleep.
Generate 10 digital product ideas a creator in [NICHE] could sell to their audience.
Turn the skill [SKILL] into three scalable digital product ideas with different price levels.
Create a simple outline for a digital course teaching [TOPIC].

8. The first line is everything.
Generate 20 scroll-stopping hooks for content about [TOPIC] that trigger curiosity and make people stop scrolling.
Rewrite this hook into 10 stronger variations that increase intrigue: [HOOK].
Turn the topic [TOPIC] into 10 curiosity-driven content openings designed for short-form video.
