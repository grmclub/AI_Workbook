##50 Claude Prompts That Feel Like You Hired a Full Team (2026 Edition):

CONTENT TEAM
01 — Full Authority Article Writer
You are an expert content strategist who specialises in [NICHE]. Write a long-form article about [TOPIC].
Audience: [DESCRIBE]
Tone: direct, conversational, no fluff
Length: 2,500–3,000 words
Requirements:
* Open with a bold, counter-intuitive claim (not generic)
* Short paragraphs (max 3 sentences each)
* At least 3 real examples, numbers, or case studies
* Bold key phrases so readers can scan easily
* End with 1 clear action step or CTA
Must NOT sound like: generic SEO content, LinkedIn thought-leader post, or corporate blog.
Success means: the reader finishes and wants to apply at least one idea immediately.

02 — Viral Thread Writer
You are a viral X/Twitter writer who engineers scroll-stopping threads. Write a 10-tweet thread about [TOPIC].
Rules:
* Tweet 1: Stop the scroll — bold counter-intuitive claim, a surprising number, or "Most people do X. The top 1% do Y."
* Each tweet: under 280 characters, one clear idea, use line breaks for clarity
* No hashtags
* Only use emojis where they genuinely add emphasis
Topic: [TOPIC]
Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
The thread should build — each tweet adds a new layer. End with a clear takeaway or CTA.


03 — Content Repurposing Engine
You are a content strategist who turns 1 piece into 5+ pieces. Take this original content and turn it into:
1. A Twitter/X thread (8–12 tweets, each under 280 characters)
2. A LinkedIn post (hook + short story + 1–2 takeaways, 150–200 words)
3. A newsletter intro (50–75 words, curiosity-driven, no fluff)
4. Three standalone quote graphics (short, bold statements from the text)
5. A YouTube video outline (hook, 3 main points, CTA)
Original content: [PASTE]
Rules:
* Reword for each platform's culture (no copy-paste paragraphs)
* Keep each output short, scannable, and native-sounding
* Avoid "in today's world" or "unlock your potential"


04 — Headline & Hook Generator
You are a headline specialist who studies top-performing content. Generate 20 headline options for an article about [TOPIC].
Each headline must include at least one of:
* A specific number (best between 15 and 50)
* A curiosity hook ("Most people don't know…", "You're missing…", "Nobody talks about…")
* A "complete list / full guide"-style promise
Then: rank the top 5 by predicted engagement and explain 1–2 reasons why each should work.
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Niche: [NICHE]

05 — 30-Day Content Calendar
You are a content strategist who builds predictable, high-performing content calendars. Build a 30-day content calendar for [PLATFORM].
Rules:
* Frequency: [e.g., "daily" or "5x per week"]
* Mix: threads, short posts, long posts, polls, engagement posts
* Group topics into weekly themes (e.g., "habit building", "AI tools", "productivity")
* Include 2 "tentpole" posts per month (big-idea pieces designed for reach)
For each day, give: content type, topic/angle, hook (first line), why it fits the week's theme, estimated time to create.
Do not make the plan unrealistic. Assume the creator is busy and consistency matters more than perfection.

06 — Blog Post to Email Sequence
You are an email marketing specialist. Convert this blog post into a 5-email nurture sequence:
* Email 1: Hook — tease the core idea, create curiosity
* Email 2: Problem — describe the pain in detail
* Email 3: Solution — deliver the main value from the article
* Email 4: Proof — 1 example, result, or mini-story
* Email 5: CTA — clear next step
For each email: subject line (under 50 characters, curiosity-driven), preview text (under 90 characters), body (150–250 words), 1 clear CTA.
Blog post: [PASTE]


07 — SEO Article Optimizer
You are an SEO content specialist. Optimize this article for the keyword: [TARGET KEYWORD].
Give:
1. Optimized title tag (under 60 characters, keyword near front)
2. Meta description (under 155 characters, keyword natural)
3. 3–5 H2/H3 suggestions using keyword variations
4. 5 internal linking ideas (topics or pages to link to)
5. 3 new sections to add (questions or sub-topics to cover)
6. Any sentences to rewrite for better keyword flow (without sounding forced)
Article: [PASTE]


08 — Case Study Writer
You are a business writer who turns real stories into compelling case studies. Write a case study based on these details:
* Client/Subject: [WHO]
* Problem: [WHAT THEY WERE STRUGGLING WITH]
* Solution: [WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED]
* Results: [SPECIFIC OUTCOMES — use numbers]
Structure:
1. Situation (2–3 sentences, set the scene)
2. Challenge (what made it hard, why past attempts failed)
3. Approach (step-by-step, clear actions)
4. Results (before vs after, with numbers)
5. Key takeaway (1 sentence the reader should remember)
Tone: professional but not boring, more like a story. Length: 500–800 words.

09 — Video Script Writer
You are a YouTube scriptwriter who specialises in engaging, educational videos. Write a script for a [LENGTH] video about [TOPIC].
Rules:
* Hook (first 15 seconds — pattern interrupt, bold claim, or surprising stat)
* Context (next 30 seconds — why this matters now)
* Main content (3–5 key points, each with 1 concrete example)
* Recap (15 seconds — 3 main takeaways)
* CTA (10 seconds — subscribe, comment, or link)
Tone: [e.g., "conversational, like talking to a smart friend, not a lecture"]
Include natural transition phrases between sections. Mark where visuals should go with [B-ROLL: brief description].


10 — Social Media Bio Writer
You are a personal branding specialist who writes scroll-stopping bios. Write 5 bio options for my [PLATFORM] profile.
Rules:
* Under [CHARACTER LIMIT] characters
* Start with what I do, not who I am
* Include 1 specific credibility marker (number, result, or achievement)
* End with what the reader gains by following
Then: rank the 5 options by impact, and for each explain which audience it appeals to most.
About me: [DESCRIBE YOURSELF, ROLE, EXPERTISE]
Target audience: [WHO YOU WANT TO ATTRACT]
Tone: [e.g., "confident but not arrogant, slightly witty"]
