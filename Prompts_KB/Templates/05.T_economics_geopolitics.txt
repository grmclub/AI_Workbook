Prompts for Trend Identification & Data Extraction

    • Extract the top 5 macroeconomic indicators from this [text/report] and format them into a markdown table with columns for Country, Indicator, Value, and YoY Change.
    • Analyze this dataset of global trade flows and list all anomalies where trade volume between two nations dropped by more than 15% quarter-over-quarter.
    • Identify the main geopolitical risks mentioned in this central bank report and categorize them by Likelihood (High/Medium/Low) and Economic Impact.
    • Scan this government policy briefing and isolate all newly announced tariffs, export controls, or sanctions, specifying the Target Country and Affected Sector.

Prompts for Cross-Impact & Scenario Planning

    • Assume a 20% escalation in maritime transit fees through the Suez Canal. Analyze the cascading economic effects on European manufacturing costs and Global inflation.
    • Evaluate how the implementation of [Specific Trade Policy] will impact supply chain dependencies for critical minerals between North America and Southeast Asia.
    • Create three distinct geopolitical scenarios for [Region/Country] over the next 12 months (Optimistic, Status Quo, Pessimistic) and outline the specific currency volatility triggers for each.
    • Compare the economic resilience of [Country A] and [Country B] against a sudden 30% spike in global crude oil prices based on their energy import ratios.

Prompts for Strategic Reporting & Synthesis
    • Draft a 300-word executive brief for an institutional investor detailing how the latest regional trade agreement alters the risk profile of infrastructure bonds in [Region].
    • Synthesize these three conflicting economic commentary articles into a neutral summary that highlights the core areas of disagreement regarding global debt sustainability.
    • Act as a sovereign risk analyst. Evaluate the structural vulnerabilities in [Country's] fiscal policy following their recent regulatory changes in the tech sector.
    • Generate a list of 5 counter-arguments to the prevailing market consensus that [Specific Geopolitical Event] will cause a prolonged recession.
