Best Prompts for Critical Thinking and Problem Solving

Purpose
Use these prompts to improve analysis, decision-making, troubleshooting, strategic planning, and creative problem-solving. They help uncover assumptions, evaluate evidence, identify risks, and generate stronger solutions.

1. Define the Problem Clearly
prompt:
Act as a critical thinking expert. Help me define the real problem behind this situation. Separate symptoms from root causes, identify constraints, and rewrite the problem statement clearly.

2. Root Cause Analysis
prompt:
Analyze this issue using the 5 Whys method and identify the most likely root causes. Distinguish between direct causes, contributing factors, and underlying systemic causes.

3. Challenge Assumptions
prompt:
Review my reasoning and identify hidden assumptions, biases, unsupported conclusions, and areas where evidence is weak or missing.

4. First-Principles Thinking
prompt:
Break this problem down into its fundamental truths and assumptions. Rebuild possible solutions from first principles rather than relying on conventional approaches.

5. Decision Analysis
prompt:
Evaluate the available options using criteria such as cost, risk, impact, feasibility, and long-term consequences. Rank the options and explain the reasoning.

6. Devil’s Advocate Review
prompt:
Act as a skeptical expert. Critique my proposal, identify weaknesses, challenge assumptions, and explain what could cause failure.

7. Scenario Planning
prompt:
Create best-case, worst-case, and most-likely scenarios for this situation. Identify triggers, risks, opportunities, and recommended actions for each.

8. Systems Thinking
prompt:
Analyze this problem as a system. Identify stakeholders, feedback loops, dependencies, bottlenecks, unintended consequences, and leverage points for improvement.

9. Structured Problem Solving
prompt:
Use a structured framework to solve this problem:
1. Define the problem
2. Gather evidence
3. Identify causes
4. Generate options
5. Evaluate trade-offs
6. Recommend actions
7. Define success metrics

10. Risk Assessment
prompt:
Identify all significant risks associated with this decision. Estimate likelihood and impact, then propose mitigation strategies and contingency plans.

11. Comparative Analysis
prompt:
Compare these alternatives objectively. Highlight strengths, weaknesses, risks, opportunities, and situations where each option performs best.

12. Red Team Review
prompt:
Act as a red team. Attempt to find flaws, vulnerabilities, overlooked risks, and failure points in this plan. Suggest improvements to strengthen it.

13. Generate Creative Solutions
prompt:
Provide 10 unconventional but practical solutions to this problem. Explain the reasoning, benefits, drawbacks, and implementation considerations for each.

14. Evidence-Based Evaluation
prompt:
Evaluate this claim using available evidence. Separate facts, assumptions, opinions, and uncertainties. Estimate confidence levels for each conclusion.

15. Action Plan Development
prompt:
Convert the analysis into a practical action plan with priorities, milestones, timelines, required resources, risks, and measurable success indicators.
