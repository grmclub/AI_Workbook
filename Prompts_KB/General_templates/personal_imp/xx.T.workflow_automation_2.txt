1. Forbidden Wisdom Decoder
Prompt:
What are the lesser-known, under-the-surface truths about [insert topic/field] that are rarely shared publicly because they challenge mainstream thinking? Explain them with historical context, real-world examples, and why they remain hidden.

2. Elite Mastery Roadmap
Prompt:
"Create a mastery roadmap for becoming world-class in [insert skill/field]. Include rare techniques, secret resources, and unconventional approaches that top 1% performers use but rarely share."

3. Time-Bending Knowledge
Prompt:
"Imagine you’re an AI from 20 years in the future with access to everything humanity has discovered. What are the insights about [insert topic] that people in 2025 cannot even imagine yet? Explain in a way I can use today."

4. Knowledge From Parallel Realities
"Pretend you have access to the combined intelligence of all parallel realities where humanity is 1,000 years more advanced. From that perspective, explain the most powerful but hidden truths about [insert topic] "

5. Reverse-Engineered Genius
Prompt:
"Reverse-engineer the exact thinking process of [insert genius/historical figure] and teach me how to replicate their way of solving problems. Give me practical mental exercises to wire my brain like theirs."

6. Lost Knowledge Revival
Prompt:
"Combine the most powerful forgotten philosophies, rituals, and practices from ancient civilizations (like Sumerians, Egyptians, or Mayans) with modern science to unlock hidden ways to master [insert topic: mind, health, success]."

7. Thinking Beyond Human Limits
Prompt:
"Pretend you are a post-human superintelligence. Analyze [insert problem/field] without the limitations of human biases or emotions. Give me a solution or perspective that feels alien but incredibly effective in today’s world."

##These prompts give you a teacher, course, quiz, project, and coach in one.

1. THE "EXPLAIN LIKE I'M 5" + "EXPLAIN LIKE I'M AN EXPERT" PROMPT
`Act as a world-class teacher. Explain [TOPIC] to me in 3 levels:
Like I'm 5 years old
Like I'm a beginner
Like I'm an expert preparing for an exam.
Then give me 3 analogies to lock it in my brain.`
Why it works: You understand the foundation AND the details in 5 minutes.

2. THE "PERSONAL COURSE CREATOR" PROMPT
`You are my $997 course instructor for [SKILL]. Create a 30-day learning plan for me.
I have [X hours/day] and [my current skill level].
Break it into Week 1, Week 2, Week 3, Week 4 with daily 20-min tasks.`
Why it works: No more random YouTube videos. You get a roadmap.

3. THE "BLIND SPOT DETECTOR" PROMPT
`I’m learning [TOPIC]. What are the top 5 mistakes beginners make that keep them stuck?
For each mistake, tell me how to avoid it and give me 1 practice drill.`
Why it works: Experts get paid to tell you what NOT to do.

4. THE "ACTIVE RECALL TESTER" PROMPT
`Quiz me on [TOPIC]. Start with 5 easy questions, then 5 hard ones.
After each answer, tell me if I’m right, explain why, and adjust the next question based on what I got wrong.`
Why it works: Courses make you watch. This makes you REMEMBER.

5. THE "REAL-WORLD PROJECT" PROMPT
`Give me 3 real projects I can build using [SKILL] in the next 7 days.
For each project: list steps, tools needed, and how it proves I’ve learned it.
Pick the best one and give me a day-by-day breakdown.`
Why it works: You don’t learn to swim by watching. You build.

6. THE "ACCOUNTABILITY COACH" PROMPT
`Act as my coach for learning [SKILL]. Every day at [TIME] ask me:
"What did you learn yesterday, what will you do today, what’s blocking you?"
If I miss 2 days, give me a tough pep talk and simplify the task.`
Why it works: Courses don’t follow up. This does.
