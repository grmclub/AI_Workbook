1/ "Explore unconventional approaches"
Use it when:
You want ideas beyond the obvious.
What it does:
Pushes AI to consider less conventional angles instead of defaulting to the most predictable response.

2/ "Read between the lines"
Use it when:
Context matters as much as the words.
What it does:
Asks AI to look for implied meaning, unstated assumptions, tone, and context.

3/ "Extract the core variables"
Use it when:
Something feels complicated.
What it does:
Identifies the few factors that have the biggest influence on the outcome.

4/ "Let 3 experts debate this"
Use it when:
You want multiple perspectives.
What it does:
Simulates different expert viewpoints, compares their reasoning, and summarizes where they agree or disagree.

5/ "Think backwards from the outcome"
Use it when:
You know the result you want.
What it does:
Starts with the desired outcome and works backward to identify the steps, requirements, and potential obstacles.

6/ "Evaluate your own reasoning"
Use it when:
You want AI to critique its first answer.
What it does:
Prompts the model to inspect its reasoning, identify weaknesses, and suggest improvements.
Important: This can improve an answer, but it doesn't guarantee accuracy.

7/ "Question the assumptions"
Use it when:
You might be solving the wrong problem.
What it does:
Identifies assumptions behind the question and challenges the ones that may not hold up.

8/ "Increase the emotional precision"
Use it when:
Your writing feels generic.
What it does:
Helps identify more specific emotional cues, motivations, and language that may better fit the context.

9/ "Point out my blind spots"
Use it when:
You want AI to challenge you.
What it does:
Looks for missing information, overlooked risks, weak assumptions, and alternative explanations.

10/ "Raise the abstraction level by two layers"

You want the bigger principle.
What it does:
Moves from a specific example → broader pattern → general principle you may be able to apply elsewhere.
