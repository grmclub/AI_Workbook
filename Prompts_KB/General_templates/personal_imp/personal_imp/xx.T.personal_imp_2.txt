🚨 CLAUDE HACK: Turn a vague goal into a clear, realistic action plan step by step.
Here are 6 powerful prompts to turn "I want to…" into "Here’s exactly what I need to do." 👇

1. 🎯 DEFINE THE REAL GOAL
I want to achieve: [GOAL].
Help me turn this vague goal into a specific, measurable outcome. Define what success looks like, what needs to change, what assumptions I’m making, and what I may be overlooking. Ask only the most important questions before finalizing the goal.

2. 🚧 IDENTIFY THE REAL OBSTACLES
Analyze my goal: [GOAL].
Identify the biggest obstacles that could realistically prevent me from succeeding. Separate external constraints from weaknesses in my strategy, skills, habits, resources, or assumptions. Rank each obstacle by how seriously it could affect the outcome.

3. ⚡ FIND THE HIGHEST-LEVERAGE ACTIONS
Analyze my goal and current situation: [GOAL + CONTEXT].
Identify the few actions that could create the biggest measurable progress. Rank them by potential impact, effort, urgency, and dependencies. Focus on actions that actually move the goal forward—not tasks that simply keep me busy.

4. 🗺️ BUILD THE ROADMAP
Turn this goal into a realistic step-by-step roadmap: [GOAL + CONTEXT].
Break it into clear phases and milestones. For each milestone, define the objective, key actions, expected outcome, dependencies, and most important deliverable. Make the sequence logical, practical, and easy to execute.

5. 🔍 STRESS-TEST THE PLAN
Review this plan: [PLAN].
Act as a brutally honest strategist. Identify anything unrealistic, unnecessary, inefficient, overly complicated, or unlikely to produce meaningful results. Remove low-value work and simplify the plan without removing the actions that actually matter.

6. 💨 START TODAY
Based on my goal, context, and plan above, identify the 3 highest-impact actions I should take today.
Prioritize them by expected progress—not convenience or ease. Make each action specific enough to start immediately, and define exactly what "done" looks like for each one.

1. 🔥 Brutal Truth Mode
"Don’t reassure me, flatter me, or automatically agree with me. Act like a brutally honest strategic advisor. Analyze [MY PLAN] objectively and tell me what’s wrong with it. 2. ⚔️ Devil’s Advocate
"Act as a highly skeptical devil’s advocate. Challenge your previous answer instead of defending it. Give me the 3 strongest arguments against your recommendation and explain the logic or evidence behind each one. Look for weaknesses, contradictions, missing information, and alternative explanations. Then tell me which counterargument is hardest to dismiss and what it means for my original decision."

3. 🔮 Second-Order Effects
"Don’t just analyze the immediate result of [MY DECISION]. Think several steps ahead. Map the likely first-, second-, and third-order effects. What happens immediately? What happens next? What could change over 6–12 months? Consider how customers, competitors, employees, partners, or other stakeholders might respond. Identify both positive and negative consequences I may not be considering."

4. 🧠 Expert Panel
"Simulate a panel of 5 world-class experts in [FIELD], each with a different perspective, experience, and area of expertise. Have them independently analyze [MY PROBLEM], challenge each other’s assumptions, disagree where appropriate, and defend their positions with clear reasoning. After the debate, synthesize their strongest insights and give me the conclusion they would most likely agree is the most defensible. Highlight where they still disagree and what I should consider before making a decision."

5. 🕵️ Hidden Assumption Finder
"Analyze everything I’ve written about [MY IDEA/PLAN]. Identify every important assumption I’m making—especially the ones I’m treating as facts without evidence. Separate proven facts from assumptions, beliefs, and guesses. Rank each assumption by importance, uncertainty, and potential impact. Then identify the ONE assumption that, if proven wrong, could undermine or completely break the entire plan. Finally, tell me how I can test that assumption quickly and cheaply."

6. ✂️ 80/20 Reconstructor
"Act as an elite strategic operator. Take [MY PLAN] and apply the 80/20 principle aggressively. Remove anything unnecessary, redundant, low-impact, or distracting. Keep only the actions most likely to produce meaningful results. Rank the remaining actions by potential impact and effort required. Tell me what to eliminate first, what to simplify, what to delegate or automate, and what I absolutely need to keep. Then rebuild the entire plan into the simplest, highest-impact version possible."
