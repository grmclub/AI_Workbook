TURN CLAUDE INTO "ADHD FOCUS MODE"
7 prompts for when your brain refuses to cooperate 👇

1. CAN’T GET STARTED?
Try this prompt:
"I’m stuck on [Task] and can’t get started. Break it into ridiculously small steps that each take less than 1 minute.
Give me ONLY the first step and make it so easy I can start right now."

2. BORED AFTER 5 MINUTES?
Try this prompt:
"I’m struggling to stay engaged with [Task].
Turn it into a ‘Dopamine Menu’ with quick 5-minute tasks, 20-minute focus sessions, and 10-minute creative breaks.
Make it feel varied enough that I actually want to keep going."

3. NEED SOMEONE TO KEEP YOU ON TRACK?
Try this prompt:
"Act as my virtual body double while I work on [Task].
Help me choose one thing to work on for the next 30 minutes. When I come back with an update, help me decide the next step and keep me focused."

4. CAN’T SWITCH TASKS?
Try this prompt:
"I just finished [Task A], but I need to start [Task B] and my brain is still stuck on the first task.
Create a simple 3-minute reset routine that helps me mentally switch gears and start Task B."

5. MAKE A BORING TASK A GAME
Try this prompt:
"I need to finish this boring task: [Task].
Turn it into a mini mission with checkpoints, a progress system, and a small reward at the end.
Keep it simple enough that setting up the game doesn’t become another task."

6. ALWAYS UNDERESTIMATE TIME?
Try this prompt:
"I think [Project] will take about [Time].
Break it into every step I’m likely forgetting, estimate how long each part could take, and give me a more realistic time range with some buffer."

7. TOO MUCH IN YOUR HEAD?
Try this prompt:
"My brain feels overloaded. I’m going to dump everything I’m thinking about below.
Organize it into:
• Do Now
• Schedule
• Delegate/Ask
• Ignore
Then give me ONE clear next action to start with."
