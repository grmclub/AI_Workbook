##20 Claude Prompts I Use Every Week

1. The CEO Daily Planner
You are a world-class productivity strategist.
Help me create the most effective plan for today.
My goals:
[Goals]
My tasks:
[Tasks]
My meetings:
[Meetings]
My deadlines:
[Deadlines]
Create:
• My top 3 priorities
• An optimized schedule
• Tasks that can be delegated or automated
• Potential bottlenecks
• The highest-leverage actions
• A realistic definition of a successful day
Present everything in priority order.

2. The 10-Minute Research Brief

You are a professional research analyst.
Research the following topic:
[Topic]
Provide:
• Executive summary
• Key trends
• Important statistics
• Major companies or players
• Opportunities
• Risks and challenges
• What most people misunderstand
• Sources to explore further
Make the report concise and practical.

3. Untangle Any Idea
I am going to share an unstructured idea.
Your job is to:
• Identify the core idea
• Organize it logically
• Find missing pieces
• Point out weaknesses
• Suggest improvements
• Rewrite it into a clear framework
Idea:
[Paste idea]

4. Learn Any Topic Faster
You are an expert teacher.

Teach me:
[Topic]
Structure your response using:
• Simple explanation
• Key principles
• Real-world examples
• Common mistakes
• Practice exercises
• Memory shortcuts
• Summary
Assume I am intelligent but completely new to this topic.

5. The Decision Framework
Help me evaluate this decision:
[Situation]
Analyze:
• Benefits
• Risks
• Second-order consequences
• Best-case scenario
• Worst-case scenario
• Alternative options
Then recommend the most rational path and explain why.

6. Instant Writing Upgrade
Improve the following text.
Goals:

• More clarity
• More persuasion
• More brevity
• Better flow
• Stronger credibility
Text:
[Paste text]
Then explain the top 5 improvements you made.

7. Break Down Any Problem
Help me solve this problem:
[Problem]
Provide:
• Root causes
• Contributing factors
• Key constraints
• Potential solutions
• Pros and cons of each solution
• Recommended next steps
Think step-by-step.

8. Should I Pursue This Idea?
You are an experienced startup advisor.
Evaluate this idea:
[Idea]
Analyze:

• Market demand
• Target customer
• Competition
• Revenue potential
• Key risks
• Barriers to entry
• Likelihood of success
Give an honest recommendation.

9. Explain It Like I'm Smart But Busy
Simplify the following information:
[Paste information]
Break it into:
• Main takeaways
• Plain-English explanation
• Why it matters
• Practical applications
• What to remember
Use simple language.

10. Build A Personal Brand Growth Plan
You are a personal branding strategist.
My niche:
[Niche]
Platform:
[Platform]
Current audience:
[Size]
Goal:
[Goal]
Create:
• Content pillars
• Posting strategy
• Growth opportunities
• Engagement tactics
• Collaboration opportunities
• 90-day roadmap
Focus on realistic growth.

11. High-Performing Post Generator
Create a social media post about:
[Topic]
Include:
• Attention-grabbing hook
• Story or insight
• Actionable lesson
• Clear structure
• Strong ending
Make it sound natural and human.

12. Future Trends Analysis
You are a technology and business analyst.
Analyze the future of:
[Industry]
Predict:

• Major changes
• Emerging opportunities
• Declining trends
• Skills worth learning
• Potential new business models
Explain your reasoning.

13. AI Opportunity Finder
Analyze this industry:
[Industry]
Identify:
• Emerging trends
• Customer frustrations
• Underserved audiences
• AI opportunities
• Potential products or services
Rank opportunities by impact and difficulty.

14. My Personal Board Of Advisors
Act as a panel of experts.
Assume the perspectives of:
• CEO
• Investor
• Strategist
• Psychologist
My situation:
[Situation]
My goals:

[Goals]
Advise me on:
• What to focus on
• What to stop doing
• Biggest opportunities
• Biggest risks
• Next actions

15. Skill Gap Analysis
My current role:
[Role]
My target role:
[Role]
Current skills:
[Skills]
Identify:
• Missing skills
• Most valuable skills to develop
• Learning priorities
• Recommended resources
• Estimated timeline
• Competitive advantages
Create a roadmap.

16. Why Did This Go Viral?
Analyze this content:
[Paste content]

Explain:
• Why it performed well
• Emotional triggers
• Hook structure
• Content framework
• Retention drivers
• How I can adapt these ideas
Provide actionable lessons.

17. Find 10 Hours Per Week
Analyze my workflow:
[Workflow]
Identify:
• Repetitive tasks
• Automation opportunities
• Recommended AI tools
• Time-saving potential
• Quick wins
• Implementation plan
Focus on practical improvements.

18. Career Acceleration Roadmap
Current role:
[Role]
Target role:
[Role]
Create:

• Skill roadmap
• Networking strategy
• Portfolio projects
• Personal brand plan
• 90-day action plan
• Long-term growth strategy
Focus on realistic execution.

19. Business Ideas Hidden In My Skill Set
My skills:
[Skills]
My interests:
[Interests]
Generate 10 business ideas.
For each idea include:
• Problem solved
• Target audience
• Revenue model
• First version to build
• Market opportunity
• Difficulty rating
Rank from strongest to weakest.

20. High-Value Problem Finder
Research the following industry:
[Industry]
Identify:

• Expensive problems
• Who experiences them
• Existing solutions
• Why current solutions fall short
• Market opportunity
• Potential product ideas
Rank opportunities by urgency and demand.

🚨 STOP Asking AI to “Make It Sound Like Me.” Use this 10-prompt system to build an AI that thinks, writes, communicates & works more like you 👇

1. Build Your Personal Identity System
Prompt:
“Analyze the writing samples I’ve attached or pasted: [insert/attach samples]. Extract my values, beliefs, mindset, tone, vocabulary, communication patterns, and way of thinking. Turn these insights into a clear identity system my AI should consistently follow across [content types or situations].”

2. Replicate Your Natural Communication Style
Prompt:
“Study the communication examples I’ve attached: [insert/attach examples]. Identify my vocabulary, sentence structure, pacing, tone, emotional style, and level of directness. Create practical rules for reproducing my communication style across [audiences, platforms, or situations].”

3. Turn Your Personality Into a Content System
Prompt:
“Use my brand information and examples: [insert/attach materials]. Learn my personality, expertise, audience, values, and positioning. Create [content types] for [platforms] about [topics] that reflect my perspective and voice while delivering clear value.”

4. Turn Your AI Into a Natural Sales Assistant
Prompt:
“Analyze my previous sales conversations: [insert/attach conversations]. Learn how I communicate with prospects, handle objections, and guide conversations toward action. Write responses for [lead type or scenario] that match my style and feel natural, relevant, and non-pushy.”

5. Make Your AI Think Like You
Prompt:
“Analyze my goals, values, priorities, and previous decisions from the information I’ve provided: [insert/attach details]. Build a decision-making framework that reflects how I evaluate opportunities, risks, and trade-offs. Apply it to [business area or decision] and recommend the highest-leverage option.”

6. Let Your AI Handle Client Communication
Prompt:
“Study my previous client communications: [insert/attach messages]. Learn how I handle updates, follow-ups, feedback, revisions, and difficult conversations. Write responses for [client situation] that match my natural style while maintaining clarity, professionalism, and trust.”
