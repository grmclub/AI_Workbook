##Astrology prompts

1. The Career Destiny Finder 
Prompt: 
"You are my future career mentor. Using my date of birth [insert DOB], analyze my natural talents, decision-making style, and hidden drives. Then reveal the 3 career or business paths where I am destined to achieve extraordinary success, along with the one field I should absolutely avoid at all costs." 

2. The Relationship Destiny Map 
Prompt: 
"I will give you my date of birth [insert DOB]. Based on it, uncover the type of people I am most compatible with, the love lessons I must learn, and the role relationships play in my life journey. Provide me with an exact description of the kind of partner who will help me grow into my highest self." 

3. The Wealth & Abundance Code 
Prompt: 
"Using my date of birth [insert DOB], decode the exact way I am meant to attract wealth, opportunities, and abundance in life. Reveal my natural money personality, the mistakes that block my financial growth, and the wealth-building strategy that fits my true self — not generic advice." 

4. The Future Timeline Guide 
Prompt: 
"I want you to use my date of birth [insert DOB] as a time map. Show me the key turning points in my life journey (past, present, and future), the phases of growth and struggle, and the exact 5-year path I am meant to follow. Write it like a roadmap so I can clearly see where I am heading." 

5. The Inner Genius Unlocker
Prompt:
"You are my inner genius mentor. Based on my date of birth [insert DOB], identify the unique talent or skill that sets me apart from 99% of people. Then give me a practical 3-step daily routine I can follow to unlock this genius and turn it into mastery and recognition in my lifetime."

1. Build my life map
Paste into ChatGPT:
"Using my birth date [DD/MM/YYYY] to understand my age and current life stage, help me build a personal life map.
Ask me 5 questions about my priorities, strengths, challenges, relationships, and goals.
Then connect my answers and identify:
• 5 strengths to build on
• 5 areas worth improving
• 3 questions I should ask myself
Don’t make assumptions from my birth date alone."

2. Find my blind spots
Paste into ChatGPT:
"Use my birth date [DD/MM/YYYY] only to understand my age and life stage.
Then ask me 5 revealing questions that could uncover blind spots in how I make decisions or handle relationships.
Based only on my answers, identify recurring patterns worth examining.
For each one, show me:
Trigger → Reaction → Underlying need → Better response
Don’t diagnose or label me."

3. Explore my next path
Paste into ChatGPT:
"Using my birth date [DD/MM/YYYY] for age and life-stage context, help me explore what I might want next.
Ask me about the problems I enjoy solving, environments where I thrive, what motivates me, and what drains me.
Then suggest 3 possible directions to explore.
For each, explain:
• What I might gain
• What I might give up
• The biggest tradeoff
• One small way to test it"

4. Build my self-care plan
Paste into ChatGPT:
"Use my birth date [DD/MM/YYYY] only for age and life-stage context.
Ask me about my schedule, stressors, energy, responsibilities, boundaries, and what helps me recharge.
Then build a realistic self-care plan around my answers.
Separate it into:
Recharge → Stabilize → Grow
Finish with a daily, weekly, and monthly routine, plus a simple plan for stressful days."
