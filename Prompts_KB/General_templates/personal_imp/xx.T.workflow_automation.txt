🚨CLAUDE CAN HELP YOU SAVE 10+ HOURS EVERY WEEK.
Copy these 9 automation prompts to eliminate repetitive work.👇👇


1. Workflow Audit
"I'm a [job title] who works roughly [X hours] per day. My main responsibilities are [list 3-5 tasks]. Audit my typical workday and give me a focused 1-hour plan that cuts the lowest-ROI work and puts the highest-leverage tasks first."
Paste your title. Get a leaner workday in 60 seconds.￼

2. Repetitive Task Killer
"Here are the tasks I repeat most often: [list 5-10 tasks]. For each one, write me a Claude prompt I can save and reuse so I stop rebuilding it from scratch every time."
Your whole week of manual work. One reusable playbook.

3. SOP Builder
"I need to document this process: [describe the messy process in plain language]. Turn it into a clean step-by-step SOP. For any step that involves writing, thinking, or summarising, include the Claude prompt I should use at that step."
Messy process in. Documented system out.

4. Email Draft Machine
"I write these types of emails regularly: [list 4-6 email types, e.g. follow-ups, cold outreach, client updates]. For each one, write me a reusable template plus a Claude prompt I can use to rewrite it, shorten it, or make it more persuasive depending on the situation."
Never draft from a blank page again.

5. Meeting Notes to Action Plan
"Here are my raw meeting notes: [paste notes]. Turn these into a clean action plan. Group items by owner if possible. Include deadlines where they were mentioned. Flag anything that is a decision versus a task."
Chaotic notes. Real action plan. 30 seconds.

6. Daily Priority Planner
"My goals this week are [list goals]. Today I have [X hours] available. My tasks are [list tasks]. I do my best thinking in the [morning / afternoon / evening]. Map out my ideal workday with time blocks, priorities ordered by impact, and low-energy slots reserved for admin."
Two minutes to fill in. No more wasted morning figuring out where to start.

7. Deep Work Sprint
"I need to finish [specific task] today. It usually takes me [estimated time] but I keep getting pulled away. Break this into 3 focused work blocks with a clear goal for each block, a short checkpoint question at the end of each block, and one rule I should follow to protect the session."
Stop circling the task. Start finishing it.

8. Research Assistant
"I need to understand [topic] well enough to [explain it to a client / write about it / make a decision]. Give me the 5 most important questions to answer first, 3 angles most people miss, a plain-language summary format I can fill in as I go, and 2-3 action points once I know enough."
The structured research brief that would take you an hour to build yourself.

9. 30 Day Content Map
"My niche is [niche]. My audience is [describe them in one sentence]. Give me a full 30-day content plan with weekly themes, one hook idea per day, the format that fits each post (carousel, short video, text post), and a CTA for each piece tied to [your offer or lead magnet]."
A full month planned. Done before lunch.

Google Gemini can now generate Docs, Sheets, Slides, and PDFs from one simple prompt.
Here's exactly how it works 👇

Setup
​Step 1: Open Google Gemini.
Step 2: Upload your raw data (receipts, text) or tell Gemini to find a file in your Google Drive.
Step 3: State your format (e.g., "create a Google Doc" or "export to PDF").
Step 4: Click the link to open it in Workspace or download it locally.

​1. Spreadsheets & Data (Sheets/Excel)
​Upload raw files or connect your data. The AI builds the spreadsheet, writes formulas, and visualizes the numbers.
​What it does:
​↳ Extracts data from uploaded images (like receipts) into clean rows.
​↳ Automatically builds formulas and reorganizes messy logs.
​↳ Features Canvas: Instantly turns raw data into an interactive visual dashboard.
​↳ Exports directly to Google Sheets, Excel (.xlsx), or CSV.
​Starter prompt: "Take these receipt images and create an Excel file with all expenses listed by date, amount, and category."

​2. Documents & Reports (Docs/Word)
​Have the AI research a topic or analyze a file, then watch it compile a polished, formatted document.
​What it does:
​↳ Researches the web in real-time and formats findings into structured layouts.
​↳ Uses an inline Gemini Bar to rewrite text, adjust tone, and tweak content on the fly.
​↳ Supports direct export to Google Docs, Word (.docx), and Markdown (.md).
​Starter prompt: "Research the latest Google IO rumors and create a Google Doc with a clean breakdown of the findings."

​3. Decks & Presentations (Slides/PowerPoint)
​Turn text, research, or data into a structured presentation deck.
​What it does:
​↳ Converts long documents or spreadsheets into complete slide outlines.
​↳ Writes slide copy, structures bullet points, and generates speaker notes.
​↳ Features an "Enhance this slide" button to instantly optimize visual layouts.
​↳ Exports to Google Slides or PowerPoint (.pptx).
​Starter prompt: "Take the research report we just generated and convert it into a structured Google Slides deck."

​4. Summaries & Document Exports (PDFs)
​Pull data from your existing Google Drive ecosystem to find trends and export them into client-ready PDF reports.
​What it does:
​↳ Accesses and analyzes files deep inside your Google Drive.
​↳ Automatically embeds visual charts (pie charts, bar graphs) into text documents.
​↳ Generates finalized PDF layouts perfect for instant sharing.
​Starter prompt: "Find my trip expenses log in Google Sheets, find where I can save money, and create a PDF summary with a breakdown pie chart."

​💡 Quick Tips for Best Results:
​↳ Name your format: Explicitly ask for a "PDF", "Markdown file", or "Google Sheet".
​↳ Expect duplicates: When editing a Drive file, the AI creates a "V2" copy to protect your original data.
​↳ Skip the upload: Just reference the file name; the system can pull it straight from your Drive.
​The people spending hours manually copy-pasting data and formatting slides are doing it the old way.
​The ones finishing in minutes are letting the AI generate the files directly.
​Save this cheatsheet. Share it with someone still building reports by hand!
