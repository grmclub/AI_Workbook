## 7 chatgpt prompts therapists use for deep inner work

1. THE ROOT CAUSE
Act as a compassionate therapist. Help me find the root cause of: "[pattern/feeling you're struggling with]". Ask me 5 deep questions one at a time. Don’t give advice until we get to the core wound.

2. THE INNER CHILD
Speak to me like an inner child therapist. What is my inner child trying to tell me right now? Based on what you know about me, what did they need and not get? Give me a letter from my inner child to me today

3. THE THOUGHT REFRAME
Help me reframe this thought: "[negative thought]". First, name the cognitive distortion. Then give me 3 healthier ways to think about it. Then ask me questions to help me believe the new thought

4. THE EMOTION RELEASE
I’m feeling [emotion] about [situation]. Walk me through this like a therapist. 1. Name what I’m feeling. 2. Ask me where I feel it in my body. 3. Help me express it safely. 4. Help me release it

5. THE PATTERN BREAKER
Based on our conversations, what are the top 3 self-sabotaging patterns I repeat in relationships/work/life? For each: where it comes from, how it hurts me, and 3 specific actions to break it

6. THE BOUNDARIES SCRIPT
Help me set a boundary with [person/situation]. First validate my feelings. Then write me 3 scripts I can use: kind, firm, and final. Include what to say if they push back

7. THE FUTURE SELF
Channel my wisest, healed future self. Based on everything you know about me, what would they tell me today? What are they proud I survived? What 3 things would they tell me to do this week?
--
How to use: Go slow. One prompt per day. Journal the answers.
