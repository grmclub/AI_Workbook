7 ChatGpt Prompts to Fix Your CV & Job Search👇👇

1. Zero-Callback Diagnostic
Prompt:
Act as a senior recruiter and career strategist. Analyze my resume, target role, and application history to identify the most likely reasons I am not receiving interview calls. Look for positioning problems, missing keywords, weak experience framing, unclear achievements, and potential role mismatches. Give me specific, actionable recommendations to improve my chances of getting interviews.
Resume & details: [Paste here]

2. Target Role Clarity
Prompt:
Based on my background, skills, experience, and career goals, identify the most realistic job titles I should target. Prioritize roles where my current profile is a strong match and eliminate roles that are too broad, unrealistic, or poorly aligned. Then define a focused job-search direction with the top roles I should prioritize.
Background: [Paste here]

3. Application Strategy Builder
Prompt:
Create a structured job-application strategy based on my target role, experience, and current job market. Recommend how many positions I should apply to each week, how to prioritize opportunities, when to customize my resume and cover letter, how to approach referrals and networking, and how to track applications efficiently. Build the strategy around quality, consistency, and maximizing interview opportunities.

4. Recruiter 10-Second Test
Prompt:
Review my resume and LinkedIn profile from the perspective of a recruiter who has only 10 seconds to scan my profile. Identify what immediately stands out, what is confusing, what feels weak, and what information is missing. Then rewrite the most important sections to make my profile clearer, more relevant, and more impactful.
Profile: [Paste here]

5. Cold Outreach Message Builder
Prompt:
Write a concise, professional outreach message I can send to a recruiter or hiring manager about the [JOB TITLE] position. Make it personalized, confident, and focused on my relevance and potential value. Keep it natural and conversational without sounding desperate, overly formal, or generic.
My background: [Paste here]

6. Interview Simulator
Prompt:
Act as a hiring manager interviewing candidates for the [JOB TITLE] position. Conduct a realistic mock interview tailored to the role. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before continuing. After each answer, evaluate my clarity, structure, relevance, confidence, and overall effectiveness. Give me specific feedback and show me how I could improve my answer.

7. Complete Job Search Audit
Prompt:
Conduct a complete audit of my job-search strategy, including my resume, target roles, applications, networking, recruiter outreach, LinkedIn presence, and interview preparation. Identify the biggest weaknesses and opportunities for improvement. Then turn everything into one cohesive, step-by-step job-search system designed to increase the quality of my applications and maximize my chances of getting interviews.
My current approach: [Paste here]

* Fix CV in ChatGPT

‎1. The CV X-Ray Analyzer
‎Prompt: "You are a senior HR professional with 15 years of recruiting experience in [industry]. Analyze my CV like you're screening a real candidate. Tell me: (1) which sections make you skip immediately, (2) achievements that are too weak and need strengthening, (3) the one thing that would get me rejected in the first 10 seconds. Be brutal. Don't be nice."

‎2. The Job Description Decoder
‎Prompt: "Here is the job description: [paste JD]. Break it down section by section and give me: (1) hidden skills not written but definitely expected, (2) red flags if a candidate is missing them, (3) the 3 must-have keywords that must appear in my CV, (4) what the hiring manager actually cares about that the JD never says directly."

‎3. The ATS Bypass Optimizer
‎Prompt: "My CV keeps failing initial screening. Act as an ATS specialist. Here is my CV: [paste]. Here is the job description: [paste]. Identify every gap between the two. Then rewrite my Experience and Skills sections using the exact keywords from the JD — naturally, not stuffed. Target score: 90+ on ATS. Show me before and after for every change."

‎4. The Achievement Transformer
‎Prompt: "Here are my current job descriptions on my CV: [paste bullet points]. Every single one describes a duty not a result. Rewrite each one using this formula: Strong action verb + what I did + measurable result + business impact. If I haven't given you numbers, suggest realistic placeholders I can verify. Make a recruiter stop scrolling."

‎5. The Recruiter Mind Reader
‎Prompt: "You are a recruiter who just received 200 CVs for this role: [paste JD]. You have 6 seconds per CV. Here is mine: [paste CV]. Tell me: (1) do you shortlist me or skip me in those 6 seconds and why, (2) what is the first thing that catches your eye, (3) what is the first thing that kills my chances, (4) what would make you call me immediately."

‎6. The Final CV Audit
‎Prompt: "Perform a complete audit of my final CV: [paste]. Check every section: ATS compatibility, keyword strength, achievement quality, formatting clarity, summary impact, skills relevance, length appropriateness. Score each section out of 10. Tell me exactly what is still weak and the precise fix for each. I want this CV to score 90+ before I send it anywhere."
