##Thinking helpers 1
1. THE "BRAIN DUMP TO STRATEGY" PROMPT
I’m overwhelmed. Here’s everything in my head about [GOAL/PROJECT]: [BRAIN DUMP].
Act as my CEO coach. Organize this mess into a 90-day plan. Tell me what to do first, what to delete, and what 20% will get 80% of results.

2. THE "WRITE LIKE ME" PROMPT
Analyze my writing style from these 3 examples: [PASTE 3 POSTS/EMAILS].
Now write a [post/email/thread] about [TOPIC] in my exact voice. Match my tone, sentence length, and word choices.

3. THE "DEBATE ME" PROMPT
I believe [YOUR OPINION].
Take the opposite side and destroy my argument. Find every flaw and blind spot.
Then help me strengthen my original argument so it’s bulletproof.

4. THE "UPLOAD + EXECUTIVE SUMMARY" PROMPT
Read this document: [UPLOAD PDF/BOOK/TRANSCRIPT].
Give me: 1. 3-sentence summary 2. 10 key takeaways 3. 5 action steps 4. The 1 quote I should remember.
Make it so I never have to read the full thing.

5. THE "PRODUCT LAUNCH IN 1 HOUR" PROMPT
I want to sell [PRODUCT/IDEA] to [AUDIENCE].
Create: Product name, offer, pricing, sales page copy, 5 tweets, and 3 email pitches.
Everything should be ready to copy-paste today.

6. THE "CODE WITHOUT CODING" PROMPT
I don’t know how to code. Build me a [website/app/tool] that does [FUNCTION].
Give me the full code + step-by-step instructions to deploy it for free. Explain it like I’m 12.

7. THE "HARD CONVERSATION SIMULATOR" PROMPT
I need to tell [PERSON] that [DIFFICULT MESSAGE].
Roleplay as them. Be difficult, emotional, and ask tough questions.
Then coach me on the exact words to say so it goes well.

8. THE "RESEARCHER ON STEROIDS" PROMPT
Research [TOPIC] like a PhD student.
Find: Key data, expert opinions, controversies, and what nobody is talking about.
Cite sources and give me a 1-page brief I can use to sound smart.

9. THE "TURN PAIN INTO CONTENT" PROMPT
I’m struggling with [PROBLEM].
Turn this struggle into 20 viral content ideas. Mix of stories, lessons, frameworks, and hot takes.
Write 3 of them in full so I can post today.

10. THE "SECOND BRAIN" PROMPT
From now on, you are my second brain.
Remember this about me: [GOALS, SKILLS, VALUES, TARGET AUDIENCE].
Whenever I ask for help, use this context and challenge me to think bigger.

## Brainstorm ideas for 10x creativity

1. THE "BAD IDEAS FIRST" PROMPT
Give me 20 terrible ideas for [YOUR GOAL/NICHE]. The worse, the better.
Then take the 3 worst ones and flip them into brilliant ideas that could actually work.

2. THE "ANGLE MULTIPLIER" PROMPT
My topic is [TOPIC].
List 15 completely different angles to talk about it that nobody is using.
Angles like: contrarian, beginner, expert, mistake-based, money-saving, time-saving, controversial.

3. THE "COMBINE 2 WORLDS" PROMPT
Combine [INDUSTRY 1] + [INDUSTRY 2] and give me 10 business/product/content ideas.
Example: Fitness + AI, Cooking + Finance, Parenting + Tech
Make them so unique they don’t exist yet.

4. THE "CUSTOMER PAIN BRAINSTORM" PROMPT
Act as my target customer: [DESCRIBE CUSTOMER].
List 30 problems you have that you’d pay to solve today.
Then turn each problem into a product, service, or content idea.

5. THE "STEAL LIKE AN ARTIST" PROMPT
Here are 3 successful businesses in [NICHE]: [LIST 3].
Don’t copy them. Deconstruct what makes them work and give me 10 new ideas inspired by their model but for a different audience.

6. THE "WHAT IF" PROMPT
What if [IMPOSSIBLE THING] was possible in [NICHE]?
Give me 10 ideas assuming money, time, and skills are not a problem.
Then scale the 3 best ones down to something I can launch this week.

7. THE "CONTENT INFINITE ENGINE" PROMPT
My niche is [NICHE]. Give me 100 content ideas broken into:
10 mistakes, 10 myths, 10 tips, 10 tools, 10 case studies, 10 questions, 10 stories, 10 predictions, 10 how-tos, 10 resources.
Make them specific, not generic.

##10 power user prompts

1. "Before answering, ask me the key questions you need to fully understand my goal."
→ Better input.

2. "Think through this with me step-by-step. Challenge assumptions and refine my thinking: [problem]."
→ Think better.

3. "Act as a strategist. Based on this situation: [context], what are the smartest next steps?"
→ Clear direction.

4. "Be brutally honest. What am I getting wrong or underestimating: [idea]?"
→ Truth > comfort.

5. "For this goal: [goal], what actions will create most of the results?"
→ Focus wins.

6. "Turn this into a simple, repeatable system I can follow: [process]."
→ Execution unlocked.

7. "What opportunities am I overlooking in this situation: [context]?"
→ Hidden leverage.

8. "Break this into options, trade-offs, and give a clear recommendation: [decision]."
→ Decide smarter.

9. "Improve this by making it clearer, sharper, and more effective: [output]."
→ Good → great.

10. "If I could only take one action to move this forward, what would it be: [goal]?"

## 20 prompts to use ChatGPT as a critical thinking partner

1. The Foundation Rebuild

    Description: Break any complex problem down to its rawest parts, then build a brand new solution from scratch.

    Prompt: 'Act as a first principles analyst on [Your Problem]. Deconstruct it into its most fundamental truths. Rebuild a completely new strategy from these basics.'

2. The Hidden Assumptions Detector

    Description: Every decision is built on assumptions you never questioned.

    Prompt: 'List every assumption I'm making about [Your Topic]. Explain how to test if each is true or false, ranked by risk.'

3. The Five Whys Excavator

    Description: Keep asking 'why' until you hit the real root of a belief.

    Prompt: 'Act as a Socratic mentor. My belief is [Your Belief]. Ask me five consecutive why questions that force me to uncover the root truth.'

4. The Opposition Amplifier

    Description: Build the smartest case against your own opinion to strengthen it.

    Prompt: 'My position is [Your Position]. Build the strongest argument AGAINST it, then help me revise my view to address these objections.'

5. The Idea Demolition Test

    Description: Try to destroy your own idea before you spend months building it.

    Prompt: 'Imagine you're my biggest critic. Stress test my idea for [Your Goal]. Prove it's completely wrong — list every flaw or missing evidence.'

6. The Alternate Timeline Analyzer

    Description: Rewind a past event and find the smallest change that would've flipped the outcome.

    Prompt: 'Analyze [Event/Outcome]. Describe the minimal changes that would've reversed it, and what lessons that reveals.'

7. The Belief Updates Engine

    Description: Learn to update your beliefs the moment new evidence shows up.

    Prompt: "Act as a Bayesian reasoner. My belief about [Topic] is [Belief]. New evidence is [Evidence]. Show how my belief should logically update."

8. The Mental Blind Spot Scanner

    Description: Find the emotional traps and logic errors hiding inside your own reasoning.

    Prompt: "Act as a cognitive bias auditor. Review my argument on [Subject]. Label each bias found and suggest a rational correction."

9. The Triple Framework Fusion

    Description: Look at one problem through three lenses, then combine the best of all three.

    Prompt: 'Compare a cost-benefit analysis, systems thinking, and ethical reasoning for [Your Problem]. Synthesize the insights.'

10. The Guaranteed Failure Blueprint

    Description: Plan exactly how you'd lose, then avoid every step.

    Prompt: 'My objective is [Objective]. How could we guarantee this fails? List 5 destructive behaviors, then invert each into a rule for success.'

11. The Vague Goal Sharpener

    Description: Turn a fuzzy wish into a sharp, measurable target.

    Prompt: 'Take my vague goal: [Goal]. Rewrite it into a clear, measurable, falsifiable problem statement with defined success metrics.'

12. The Future Failure Autopsy

    Description: Pretend your project already failed a year from now, then find out why before it happens.

    Prompt: 'Assume [Project] fails in 12 months. Identify 10 failure modes, warning signals, and mitigation tactics.'

13. The Root Cause Web

    Description: Map every hidden connection between a problem and what's causing it.

    Prompt: 'Create a cause-and-effect map for [Situation]. Identify root causes, feedback loops, and leverage points.'

14. The Proof Strength Ranker

    Description: Rank what's backing your belief from solid science to weak anecdotes.

    Prompt: 'My claim is [Claim]. List all supporting evidence, rank by strength, and explain what would raise my confidence.'

15. The Moral Risk Radar

    Description: Scan any big decision for hidden ethical landmines before they blow up.

    Prompt: 'Analyze my decision [Decision]. Identify stakeholders, harms, and value conflicts, and suggest go/no-go thresholds.'

16. The Five Experts Roundtable

    Description: Get five different professional perspectives on the same challenge.

    Prompt: 'Evaluate [Challenge] from an economist, engineer, psychologist, ethicist, and operator. Merge insights into one plan.'

17. The Devil's Advocate Lens

    Description: Deliberately argue against yourself to find where you might be wrong.

    Prompt: 'Act as an intelligent contrarian. My perspective on [Topic] is [Perspective]. Critique it from the opposite angle.'

18. The Vision to Action Ladder

    Description: Turn a big dream into concrete, doable steps across five levels.

    Prompt: 'Take my concept [Idea]. Translate it across Vision, Strategy, Process, Behavior, and a concrete Example.'

19. The Two Sided Truth Finder

    Description: Argue both sides of a debate fully, then find what stays true no matter who wins.

    Prompt: 'Present the best argument FOR and AGAINST [Belief]. Identify what core truth remains valid from both sides.'

20. The Thinking About Thinking Mirror

    Description: Review your reasoning and catch the question you're too scared to ask.

    Prompt: 'Review my reasoning about [Situation]. What critical question am I not asking? How could it change my conclusion?'
