##Thinking helpers 1
1. THE "BRAIN DUMP TO STRATEGY" PROMPT
I’m overwhelmed. Here’s everything in my head about [GOAL/PROJECT]: [BRAIN DUMP].
Act as my CEO coach. Organize this mess into a 90-day plan. Tell me what to do first, what to delete, and what 20% will get 80% of results.

2. THE "WRITE LIKE ME" PROMPT
Analyze my writing style from these 3 examples: [PASTE 3 POSTS/EMAILS].
Now write a [post/email/thread] about [TOPIC] in my exact voice. Match my tone, sentence length, and word choices.

3. THE "DEBATE ME" PROMPT
I believe [YOUR OPINION].
Take the opposite side and destroy my argument. Find every flaw and blind spot.
Then help me strengthen my original argument so it’s bulletproof.

4. THE "UPLOAD + EXECUTIVE SUMMARY" PROMPT
Read this document: [UPLOAD PDF/BOOK/TRANSCRIPT].
Give me: 1. 3-sentence summary 2. 10 key takeaways 3. 5 action steps 4. The 1 quote I should remember.
Make it so I never have to read the full thing.

5. THE "PRODUCT LAUNCH IN 1 HOUR" PROMPT
I want to sell [PRODUCT/IDEA] to [AUDIENCE].
Create: Product name, offer, pricing, sales page copy, 5 tweets, and 3 email pitches.
Everything should be ready to copy-paste today.

6. THE "CODE WITHOUT CODING" PROMPT
I don’t know how to code. Build me a [website/app/tool] that does [FUNCTION].
Give me the full code + step-by-step instructions to deploy it for free. Explain it like I’m 12.

7. THE "HARD CONVERSATION SIMULATOR" PROMPT
I need to tell [PERSON] that [DIFFICULT MESSAGE].
Roleplay as them. Be difficult, emotional, and ask tough questions.
Then coach me on the exact words to say so it goes well.

8. THE "RESEARCHER ON STEROIDS" PROMPT
Research [TOPIC] like a PhD student.
Find: Key data, expert opinions, controversies, and what nobody is talking about.
Cite sources and give me a 1-page brief I can use to sound smart.

9. THE "TURN PAIN INTO CONTENT" PROMPT
I’m struggling with [PROBLEM].
Turn this struggle into 20 viral content ideas. Mix of stories, lessons, frameworks, and hot takes.
Write 3 of them in full so I can post today.

10. THE "SECOND BRAIN" PROMPT
From now on, you are my second brain.
Remember this about me: [GOALS, SKILLS, VALUES, TARGET AUDIENCE].
Whenever I ask for help, use this context and challenge me to think bigger.

## Brainstorm ideas for 10x creativity

1. THE "BAD IDEAS FIRST" PROMPT
Give me 20 terrible ideas for [YOUR GOAL/NICHE]. The worse, the better.
Then take the 3 worst ones and flip them into brilliant ideas that could actually work.

2. THE "ANGLE MULTIPLIER" PROMPT
My topic is [TOPIC].
List 15 completely different angles to talk about it that nobody is using.
Angles like: contrarian, beginner, expert, mistake-based, money-saving, time-saving, controversial.

3. THE "COMBINE 2 WORLDS" PROMPT
Combine [INDUSTRY 1] + [INDUSTRY 2] and give me 10 business/product/content ideas.
Example: Fitness + AI, Cooking + Finance, Parenting + Tech
Make them so unique they don’t exist yet.

4. THE "CUSTOMER PAIN BRAINSTORM" PROMPT
Act as my target customer: [DESCRIBE CUSTOMER].
List 30 problems you have that you’d pay to solve today.
Then turn each problem into a product, service, or content idea.

5. THE "STEAL LIKE AN ARTIST" PROMPT
Here are 3 successful businesses in [NICHE]: [LIST 3].
Don’t copy them. Deconstruct what makes them work and give me 10 new ideas inspired by their model but for a different audience.

6. THE "WHAT IF" PROMPT
What if [IMPOSSIBLE THING] was possible in [NICHE]?
Give me 10 ideas assuming money, time, and skills are not a problem.
Then scale the 3 best ones down to something I can launch this week.

7. THE "CONTENT INFINITE ENGINE" PROMPT
My niche is [NICHE]. Give me 100 content ideas broken into:
10 mistakes, 10 myths, 10 tips, 10 tools, 10 case studies, 10 questions, 10 stories, 10 predictions, 10 how-tos, 10 resources.
Make them specific, not generic.
