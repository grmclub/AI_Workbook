## You don’t have a time problem. You have a system problem.
##Turn ChatGPT into your $100/hour productivity coach

PROMPT 1 — The Weekly Planning Session
"Build me a weekly planning template. Include how to prioritize tasks, block time, and leave room for the unexpected."
Result: A real system, not just a to-do list.

PROMPT 2 — The Procrastination Diagnosis
"I keep procrastinating on [task]. Here’s why I think it happens: [describe]. Diagnose the real reason and give me 3 fixes."
Result: You fix the cause, not the symptom.

PROMPT 3 — The Daily Priority Filter
"Here’s everything on my plate today: [list]. Help me identify the top 3 that actually matter and what can wait or be dropped."
Result: You stop confusing busy with productive.

PROMPT 4 — The Meeting/Distraction Auditor
"Here’s how my week actually goes: [describe]. Find the biggest time leaks and suggest what to cut, delegate, or shorten."
Result: You find hours you didn’t know you were losing.

PROMPT 5 — The Deep Work Block Builder
"Design a daily schedule that protects at least [X] hours of focused work around my existing commitments: [list]."
Result: Focus time that isn’t just wishful thinking.

PROMPT 6 — The Bad-Day Backup Plan
"Give me a minimum-viable version of my routine for days I’m exhausted or overwhelmed. Something realistic, not ideal."
Result: A plan that survives your worst days.

PROMPT 7 — The Weekly Reflection Ritual
"Design a 5-minute Sunday review: what to ask myself, what to track, and how to adjust next week’s plan."
Result: You improve the system instead of repeating mistakes.
