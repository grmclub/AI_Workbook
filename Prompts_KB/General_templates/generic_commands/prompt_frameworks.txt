1/ 9 Prompt Frameworks to Master AI
If ChatGPT gives weak answers, the problem is often the prompt, not the model.
A well-structured prompt helps AI understand:
➔ What role to play
➔ What problem to solve
➔ What information matters
➔ What output you expect
Instead of writing random instructions, use proven frameworks that guide the conversation.
These frameworks work for:
➔ Content creation
➔ Marketing
➔ Research
➔ Business planning
➔ Learning
➔ Problem solving
The right framework can instantly improve the quality of your results.

2/ Frameworks for Tasks and Problem Solving
➔ R-A-C-E
Role, Action, Context, Expectation
Best when you need AI to complete a specific task with clear instructions.
➔ R-I-S-E
Role, Identify, Steps, Expectation
Useful for breaking down challenges and finding solutions.
➔ S-T-A-R
Situation, Task, Action, Result
Great for analyzing projects, case studies, and performance reviews.
➔ S-O-A-P
Subject, Objective, Action, Plan
Helpful when creating structured plans and practical solutions.
Each framework gives AI a clear roadmap before it starts generating answers.

3/ Frameworks for Learning and Persuasion
➔ C-L-E-A-R
Context, Learn, Evaluate, Action, Review
Useful for learning new topics and measuring progress.
➔ P-A-S-T-O-R
Problem, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response
Works well for marketing, sales, copywriting, and persuasive communication.
➔ F-A-B
Features, Advantages, Benefits
Ideal for explaining products, services, and ideas in a way people understand.
These frameworks help AI move beyond information and focus on outcomes.

4/ Frameworks for Analysis and Decision Making
➔ 5-W-1-H
Who, What, When, Where, Why, How
Perfect for investigating problems and understanding situations from every angle.
➔ G-R-O-W
Goal, Reality, Options, Will
Excellent for coaching, planning, goal setting, and decision making.
Both frameworks force clarity before action.
When the inputs become clearer, the outputs become better.

5/ Which Framework Should You Use?
Use R-A-C-E when you know exactly what you want.
Use R-I-S-E when solving problems.
Use S-T-A-R when reviewing performance or projects.
Use S-O-A-P when building plans.
Use C-L-E-A-R when learning.
Use P-A-S-T-O-R when persuading.
Use F-A-B when explaining value.
Use 5-W-1-H when investigating.
Use G-R-O-W when setting goals.

Pick one framework today and compare the results against your usual prompts.
You'll quickly see why structure matters.
