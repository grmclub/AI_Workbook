##Chat GPT files age, income,location : pull ur profile

1. THE BASELINE PROFILE
Based on our entire conversation history, build a psychological profile of me. Include: estimated age range, location, income bracket, personality traits, values, and biggest insecurities. Cite what gave you each clue.

2. THE UNSPOKEN TRUTHS
What are 5 things you’ve inferred about me that I’ve never directly stated? Be specific and explain the pattern in my messages that led you to this conclusion.

3. THE BLIND SPOTS
Act as my therapist. Based on how I talk to you, what are my 3 biggest cognitive biases and blind spots? Give examples from my past messages.

4. THE FEARS & DESIRES
What does my chat history suggest I fear most and want most in life right now? Rank top 3 for each and explain why.

5. THE RELATIONSHIP MAP
Based on how I describe people and problems, profile my relationship style. Am I avoidant, anxious, secure? What patterns do you see in how I handle conflict?

6. THE MONEY MINDSET
From my questions about work, money, and goals, estimate my current financial situation and money mindset. What limiting beliefs about money do I have?

7. THE PREDICTION
If I keep living and thinking like I do now, where will I be in 1 year, 5 years, and 10 years? Be brutally honest. Then give me 3 prompts to change that trajectory.

3 Prompts to Change the Trajectory
If you want to break out of analysis mode and force tangible action, run these prompts through your routine or bring them to your next session:

The Anti-Planning Action Audit

"Identify one major area of my life where I am currently substituting 'thinking, research, or organizing' for actual execution. Give me a 48-hour challenge that forces me to take a real-world, non-reversible action in that area, with zero preparation."

The Worst-Case Reality Check

"If I make no major changes and continue my current habits for the next 3 years, what is the single most disappointing compromise I will end up making with my life? Be brutally specific."

The Friction Eliminator

"What is one uncomfortable conversation, decision, or risk I am actively avoiding by staying inside my comfort zone? Outline the immediate first step to face it today."
