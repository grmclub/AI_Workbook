##ChatGPT-5 Can Do 90% of Consulting Work with This Custom Workflow

1. Strategic Growth Advisor
“You’re my senior strategy consultant for a company in the [industry] field.
Perform a deep SWOT + market gap analysis to reveal our biggest untapped growth lever and the single biggest threat.
Conclude with one bold move we should take immediately to shift momentum in our favor.”

2. Marketing Strategy Architect
“You’re my marketing strategist. Our audience is [describe audience].
Develop 5 original, data-backed customer acquisition plays that align with real buyer psychology — not just ad trends.
Rank them by potential ROI and implementation difficulty.”

3. Root-Cause Problem Solver
“You’re my diagnostic consultant. The issue is [describe problem].
Use the 5 Whys method to trace the true root cause behind it, and summarize the full chain of reasoning.
End with a short action plan that directly attacks the real cause — not the symptom.”

4. Decision-Making Blueprint
“You’re my decision strategist. We’re choosing between [Option A] and [Option B].
Build a quick weighted decision matrix using 5 critical factors (impact, cost, risk, time, scalability).
Score each option, pick the best one, and defend it in one concise argument.”

5. Product Innovation Sprint
“You’re my product development partner. We’re building a [product/service] for [target audience].
Write the 5 essential user stories (use: ‘As a [user], I want to [action], so I can [benefit].’)
From those, define a clear MVP and one bold differentiator that would make it irresistible.”

6. Risk & Resilience Planner
“You’re my risk strategy consultant for [project or department].
Identify the 3 most probable risks and the 3 highest-impact risks.
For each, give a brief prevention plan and a fast recovery strategy if it happens.”

7. Operational Excellence Engineer
“You’re my process improvement consultant. Our workflow for [describe process] wastes time or resources.
Rebuild it into a 5-step lean system that increases speed, consistency, and accountability.
Add one automation or AI-driven improvement suggestion.”

8. Content & Visibility Strategist
“You’re my content strategy consultant. We want to become the go-to authority in [niche/topic].
Create a content pillar system with 3 major pillars and 5 detailed content ideas per pillar.
Make them high-authority pieces that position us as experts — not just posters.”

9. Performance & Focus Coach
“You’re my personal productivity mentor. I’m struggling to manage [task type or workload].
Design a weekly schedule separating deep work, admin, and strategy.
Ensure it’s sustainable, prevents burnout, and doubles focus time.”

10. Sales & Persuasion Strategist
“You’re my sales strategy expert. We sell [product/service] and compete with [competitor].
Create a mini battle plan showing 3 differentiators and one killer talking point that flips their main strength into our advantage.
Finish with a 2-sentence elevator pitch that wins attention instantly.”
