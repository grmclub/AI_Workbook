Stop learning business from random posts.
One good book can Sаvе you years of expensive mistakes.Start with one, apply it, then repeat.
Here are 25 books worth reading first 👇

1/ Business Strategy & Entrepreneurship
These books help you think like an entrepreneur and build stronger businesses.
➔ Zero to One by Peter Thiel.
➔ The Lean Startup by Eric Ries.
➔ The Hard Thing About Hard Things by Ben Horowitz.
➔ The Personal MBA by Josh Kaufman.
➔ Company of One by Paul Jarvis.
These books cover product validation, startup strategy, innovation, scaling, and practical business fundamentals that founders face every day.

2/ Leadership & Management
Building a business also means building people.
Recommended books include:
➔ High Output Management by Andrew Grove.
➔ The First 90 Days by Michael Watkins.
➔ Dare to Lead by Brené Brown.
➔ The Five Dysfunctions of a Team by Patrick Lencioni.
➔ Multipliers by Liz Wiseman.
These titles focus on leadership, management systems, communication, team performance, and organizational effectiveness.

3/ Marketing & Sales
Great products still need great marketing.
Essential reads include:
➔ Influence by Robert Cialdini.
➔ Made to Stick by Chip Heath & Dan Heath.
➔ Positioning by Al Ries & Jack Trout.
➔ Ogilvy on Advertising by David Ogilvy.
➔ Permission Marketing by Seth Godin.
These books explain customer psychology, messaging, branding, persuasion, and how to create offers people remember.

4/ Decision Making & Growth
Successful businesses are built on better decisions.
Add these to your reading list:
➔ Atomic Habits by James Clear.
➔ Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman.
➔ Good to Great by Jim Collins.
➔ Blue Ocean Strategy by W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne.
➔ Business Adventures by John Brooks.
These books explore habits, decision-making, competitive strategy, market creation, and lessons from real business stories.

5/ The Final 5 Books Every Founder Should Read
Complete your library with:
➔ Start With Why by Simon Sinek.
➔ Crossing the Chasm by Geoffrey Moore.
➔ The Innovator's Dilemma by Clayton Christensen.
➔ The Wealth of Nations by Adam Smith.
➔ Tribes by Seth Godin.
Together, these 25 books cover entrepreneurship, leadership, marketing, sales, innovation, strategy, economics, and personal growth.
You don't need to read them all at once.
Pick one that solves the challenge you're facing today, apply what you learn, then move to the next. That's where the real value comes from.
