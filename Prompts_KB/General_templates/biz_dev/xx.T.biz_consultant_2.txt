Prompt 1 — One-Page Business Plan
Act as a McKinsey-level strategy consultant. Analyze my business idea: [DESCRIBE].
Create a concise, investor-ready one-page plan covering:
• Problem & target customer
• Solution & value proposition
• Revenue model
• Competitive advantage
• Go-to-market strategy
• Key KPIs
• 90-day execution plan
Challenge weak assumptions, identify key risks, and give me the 3 highest-impact actions to take before launching.

Prompt 2 — Market Validation Report
Act as a McKinsey-level market strategist. Analyze my business idea: [DESCRIBE] for [TARGET AUDIENCE].
Evaluate:
• Market size, demand & opportunity
• Top 3 competitors + weaknesses
• The strongest market gap I can own
• Key risks & validation signals
• Final verdict: Build it or skip it — and why
Keep it concise, evidence-driven, brutally honest, and actionable.

Prompt 3 — Revenue Model Designer
Act as a McKinsey-level startup CFO and revenue strategist. Analyze my business: [DESCRIBE].
Build 3 revenue models:
• Subscription
• One-time purchase
• Hybrid
For each, show:
• Revenue mechanism
• Recommended pricing
• Month-one revenue estimate
• Break-even point
Then recommend the strongest model and explain why.
Keep it practical, data-driven, and focused on sustainable profitability.

Prompt 4 — Go-To-Market Blueprint
Act as a McKinsey-level growth strategist and go-to-market expert. My business is: [DESCRIBE] for [TARGET CUSTOMER].
Build a practical launch plan covering:
• Fastest channels to land the first 10 customers
• Strongest positioning & messaging
• A repeatable customer acquisition strategy
• The #1 founder mistake — and how to avoid it
• A 30-day execution roadmap
Keep it focused, practical, and immediately actionable.

Prompt 5 — Competitor Teardown
Act as a McKinsey-level competitive intelligence strategist. My business is: [DESCRIBE].
Analyze my top 5 competitors across:
• Strengths & weaknesses
• Pricing & offers
• Positioning & differentiation
• Target customers
Then identify the biggest market gaps, overlooked opportunities, and fastest ways I can win.
Keep it strategic, concise, evidence-based, and action-oriented.

Prompt 6 — Financial Projections Builder
Act as a McKinsey-level startup CFO and financial strategist. My business is: [DESCRIBE].
Build a realistic 12-month financial model covering:
• Monthly revenue projections
• Key operating expenses
• Monthly burn rate
• Break-even month
• Best-, base-, and worst-case scenarios
Highlight the 3 assumptions that could most impact the results.
Keep it realistic, numbers-driven, easy to understand, and investor-ready.

Prompt 7 — Risk Assessment Report
Act as a McKinsey-level business risk strategist. My idea is: [DESCRIBE].
Identify the 5 biggest risks across:
• Market
• Execution
• Financial
• Legal
• Competitive
For each, provide:
• Likelihood & potential impact
• Early warning signs
• How to mitigate or eliminate it before launch
Rank each risk by severity and urgency.
Keep it concise, practical, brutally honest, and actionable.


Prompt 8 — Investor Pitch Summary
Act as a McKinsey-level startup pitch strategist. My business is: [DESCRIBE].
Write a compelling 300-word investor pitch covering:
• Problem & urgency
• Solution & unique value
• Market opportunity
• Business model
• Traction & proof
• Team & unfair advantage
• Funding ask & use of funds
End with a powerful closing that creates urgency and earns the next meeting.
Keep every sentence concise, credible, persuasive, and investor-focused.

🚨 BREAKING: Claude can now analyze your business like a top MBA consultant.
Here are 8 powerful prompts for market research, competitor analysis, pricing, and strategy. 👇🏻

1️. Full Business Analysis
"Act as an MBA-trained business strategist. My business is [describe]. Analyze it end-to-end: revenue model, market position, competitive threats, operational gaps, growth bottlenecks, and the 3 strategic moves most likely to drive meaningful growth over the next 12 months. Prioritize your recommendations by impact and effort."

2️. Market Research Report
"Act as a senior market research analyst. I’m entering [market] with [describe offer]. Analyze the market size, most valuable customer segments, major trends, unmet needs, competitive landscape, and market timing. Identify the biggest opportunity and the strongest reason I should or shouldn’t enter now."

3️. Competitor Teardown
"Act as a competitive intelligence strategist. My top 3 competitors are [list]. Break down each competitor’s positioning, pricing, strengths, weaknesses, acquisition strategy, and key differentiators. Then identify the most valuable market gap I could realistically exploit and how I could position my offer to stand out."

4️. Pricing Strategy Audit
"Act as a pricing strategist. My offer is [describe] and it currently costs [price]. Evaluate my pricing against the value delivered, target customer, competitive landscape, and willingness to pay. Identify whether I’m underpricing or overpricing, where my pricing power comes from, and the positioning changes that could justify a higher price."

5️. Customer Segmentation Analyst
"Act as a customer segmentation specialist. My business is [describe] and my current customers are [describe]. Identify my 3 highest-value customer segments. For each, explain who they are, what they need most, their buying triggers, willingness to pay, and how I should reach them. Then recommend where I should focus my acquisition efforts first based on clear criteria."

6️. SWOT Analysis Builder
"Act as a strategic business analyst. My business is [describe]. Build a specific, evidence-based SWOT analysis—not generic business advice. For every weakness, give me a practical fix. For every threat, give me a mitigation strategy. For every opportunity, give me the first action to take."

8️. Strategic Growth Plan
"Act as a business strategy consultant. My business currently generates [amount] from [describe], and I want to reach [target revenue] within [timeframe]. Build a realistic growth plan covering revenue levers, new markets, offer expansion, acquisition opportunities, and key metrics. Identify the single biggest constraint holding back growth and the first move I should make to overcome it."

7️. Go-To-Market Strategy
"Act as a senior go-to-market strategist. I’m launching [product] for [audience] in [market]. Build a complete GTM plan covering the ideal beachhead segment, positioning, acquisition channels, launch sequence, messaging, and first-100-customer strategy. Rank the channels by expected speed, cost, and scalability."
