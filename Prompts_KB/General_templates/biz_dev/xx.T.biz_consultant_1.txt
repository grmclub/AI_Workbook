##ChatGPT-5 Can Do 90% of Consulting Work with This Custom Workflow

1. Strategic Growth Advisor
“You’re my senior strategy consultant for a company in the [industry] field.
Perform a deep SWOT + market gap analysis to reveal our biggest untapped growth lever and the single biggest threat.
Conclude with one bold move we should take immediately to shift momentum in our favor.”

2. Marketing Strategy Architect
“You’re my marketing strategist. Our audience is [describe audience].
Develop 5 original, data-backed customer acquisition plays that align with real buyer psychology — not just ad trends.
Rank them by potential ROI and implementation difficulty.”

3. Root-Cause Problem Solver
“You’re my diagnostic consultant. The issue is [describe problem].
Use the 5 Whys method to trace the true root cause behind it, and summarize the full chain of reasoning.
End with a short action plan that directly attacks the real cause — not the symptom.”

4. Decision-Making Blueprint
“You’re my decision strategist. We’re choosing between [Option A] and [Option B].
Build a quick weighted decision matrix using 5 critical factors (impact, cost, risk, time, scalability).
Score each option, pick the best one, and defend it in one concise argument.”

5. Product Innovation Sprint
“You’re my product development partner. We’re building a [product/service] for [target audience].
Write the 5 essential user stories (use: ‘As a [user], I want to [action], so I can [benefit].’)
From those, define a clear MVP and one bold differentiator that would make it irresistible.”

6. Risk & Resilience Planner
“You’re my risk strategy consultant for [project or department].
Identify the 3 most probable risks and the 3 highest-impact risks.
For each, give a brief prevention plan and a fast recovery strategy if it happens.”

7. Operational Excellence Engineer
“You’re my process improvement consultant. Our workflow for [describe process] wastes time or resources.
Rebuild it into a 5-step lean system that increases speed, consistency, and accountability.
Add one automation or AI-driven improvement suggestion.”

8. Content & Visibility Strategist
“You’re my content strategy consultant. We want to become the go-to authority in [niche/topic].
Create a content pillar system with 3 major pillars and 5 detailed content ideas per pillar.
Make them high-authority pieces that position us as experts — not just posters.”

9. Performance & Focus Coach
“You’re my personal productivity mentor. I’m struggling to manage [task type or workload].
Design a weekly schedule separating deep work, admin, and strategy.
Ensure it’s sustainable, prevents burnout, and doubles focus time.”

10. Sales & Persuasion Strategist
“You’re my sales strategy expert. We sell [product/service] and compete with [competitor].
Create a mini battle plan showing 3 differentiators and one killer talking point that flips their main strength into our advantage.
Finish with a 2-sentence elevator pitch that wins attention instantly.”

🚨 BREAKING: Claude can now build a business plan that looks like it came straight from McKinsey.
Here are 8 essential prompts every founder needs before launching. 👇


Prompt 1 — One-Page Business Plan
“Act as a top-tier strategy consultant. My business idea is [DESCRIBE IDEA]. Create a complete one-page business plan covering the problem, target customer, solution, revenue model, competitive advantage, and 90-day action plan. Make it concise enough to pitch, yet practical enough to start executing tomorrow.”

Prompt 2 — Market Validation Report
“Act as a senior market analyst. My idea is [DESCRIBE IDEA] for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Analyze the market size, identify the top 3 competitors and their key weaknesses, uncover the most promising market gap I could own, and give me an honest verdict: worth building, needs repositioning, or too crowded?”

Prompt 3 — Revenue Model Designer
“Act as a startup CFO. My business is [DESCRIBE BUSINESS]. Develop 3 revenue model options: subscription, one-time purchase, and hybrid. For each, show how I make money, recommended pricing, realistic month-one revenue, and estimated break-even point. Then recommend the strongest option and explain why.”

Prompt 4 — Go-To-Market Blueprint
“Act as a senior growth strategist. My business is [DESCRIBE BUSINESS] for [TARGET CUSTOMER]. Build a practical plan to land my first 10 paying customers. Identify the fastest acquisition channels, craft the core message I should use, and reveal the one critical step most founders overlook that can cost them months of wasted effort.”

Prompt 6 — Financial Projections Builder
“Act as a startup CFO. My business is [DESCRIBE BUSINESS]. Build a clear 12-month financial model covering monthly revenue projections, key expenses, cash burn, and break-even month. Identify the 3 critical assumptions that could make or break these projections, and explain how sensitive the model is to each.”

Prompt 5 — Competitor Teardown
“Act as a senior competitive strategist. My business is [DESCRIBE BUSINESS]. Analyze my top 5 competitors across their strengths, weaknesses, pricing, positioning, and key advantages. Identify the most valuable gaps they’re leaving open and show me exactly where I can differentiate and win quickly.”

Prompt 7 — Risk Assessment Report
“Act as a senior business risk analyst. My idea is [DESCRIBE IDEA]. Identify the 5 biggest risks across market, execution, financial, legal, and competitive factors. For each risk, assess its likelihood and potential impact, then provide a practical mitigation strategy I can implement before investing any money.”

Prompt 8 — Investor Pitch Summary
“Act as an elite pitch coach. My business is [DESCRIBE BUSINESS]. Write a compelling 300-word investor summary covering the problem, solution, market opportunity, business model, traction, team, and funding ask. Make every sentence purposeful, concise, and persuasive. End with a powerful closing line that makes investors want the meeting.”
