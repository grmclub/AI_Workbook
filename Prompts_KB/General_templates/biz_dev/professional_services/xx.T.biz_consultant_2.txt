McKinsey-style decks in hours, not weeks with 6 Claude prompts.
Use them in sequence to turn raw ideas into a polished, client-ready presentation.

1️. The Ghost Deck Architect
"I’m presenting [TOPIC] to [AUDIENCE]. Build a 10-slide ghost deck with an insight-driven headline for every slide, the ideal chart or visual, and the key information required. Each headline must communicate a clear conclusion—not simply describe the topic. Make every slide understandable in 15 seconds or less."

2️. The MECE Argument Blueprint
"My proposed recommendation is [X]. Build a MECE argument around 3 mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive supporting pillars. For each pillar, identify 2 data points needed to validate it. Flag any pillar that lacks sufficient evidence."

3️. The Insight Generator
"Here’s my data: [PASTE DATA]. For every finding, write a one-sentence ‘why it matters’ takeaway. Remove findings that would not change or strengthen the recommendation."

4️. The Stakeholder Resistance Map
"My audience includes [ROLES]. Identify each stakeholder’s primary concern or likely objection. Draft a concise, evidence-based response to address each one in advance. Use only insights supported by the analysis."

5️. The Executive Overview Accelerator
"Draft the executive overview. State the recommendation in the first sentence, give the 3 strongest reasons supporting it, and quantify the expected impact where possible. Make the required action clear within 30 seconds."

6️. The Single-Slide Clarity Test
"Condense the entire presentation into one slide: one action-focused headline, one essential chart, and three supporting bullets. If the argument cannot fit, identify the weakest reasoning or information that should be removed."
