🚨 BREAKING: Claude can now help you map out your retirement better than many people charging $2,500.

Give it your age, savings, income, spending, and goals.And it can help you estimate when you could retire and how much you may need.
Here are 10 prompts to build your retirement roadmap 👇

1. Retirement Timeline
"Based on my current age, savings, income, expenses, investment returns, and expected retirement spending, calculate the earliest realistic age I could retire. Show the assumptions and what I need to change to retire earlier: [details]"

2. Monte Carlo Simulation
"Run a Monte Carlo-style retirement analysis using different market return, inflation, and longevity assumptions. Estimate the probability my portfolio lasts until age [X]. Show the strongest and weakest scenarios."

3. Social Security / Pension Strategy
"Compare different claiming strategies for my Social Security/pension. Show how claiming at different ages affects lifetime income, taxes, and portfolio withdrawals. Assume: [details]."

4. Tax Optimization
"Create a tax-efficient retirement withdrawal strategy across my taxable, traditional retirement, and Roth accounts. Show which account I should withdraw from first and why, while considering future tax rates."

5. Healthcare Costs
"Estimate how healthcare and insurance costs could affect my retirement plan from age [X] to [X]. Model several cost-inflation scenarios and show how much additional savings I should target."

6. Inflation Stress Test
"Stress-test my retirement plan against prolonged high inflation. Model 2%, 4%, 6%, and 8% inflation scenarios and show how my purchasing power and portfolio longevity change."

7. Part-Time Retirement
"Model a semi-retirement scenario where I stop full-time work at [age] and earn $[amount] annually from part-time work for [years]. Calculate how this changes my required retirement savings."

8. Asset Allocation
"Analyze my current retirement portfolio allocation: [stocks/bonds/cash/other]. Compare conservative, balanced, and aggressive allocations based on my retirement timeline and risk tolerance. Explain the trade-offs."

9. Retirement Budget
"Build a realistic retirement budget from my current expenses. Separate essential, discretionary, healthcare, travel, housing, and unexpected expenses. Adjust for inflation and show my required annual retirement income."

10. Reverse Retirement Plan
"Work backward from my desired retirement lifestyle. I want to retire at [age], spend $[amount] per year in today's dollars, and have my portfolio last until age [X]. Calculate the savings target, required annual contributions, and approximate investment return needed."
