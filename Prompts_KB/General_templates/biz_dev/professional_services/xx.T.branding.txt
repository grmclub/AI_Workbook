🚨 BREAKING: Claude can now build your ENTIRE BRAND identity in one afternoon.
These 7 prompts take you from just a name to a finished brand kit 👇

1/ The Brand Identity System
"Act as a brand strategist and creative director. Build a complete brand identity for my business. Analyze my offer, my ideal customer, my three main competitors and the feeling I want people to have. Design the positioning statement, the brand personality, the naming direction, the visual direction, the voice and the rollout order for every asset."

2/ The Positioning Blueprint
"Act as a positioning consultant. Find the spot in the market my brand can own. Analyze my competitors' messages, the gaps they leave, my customers' real frustrations and what I do differently. Design a one sentence positioning line, three differentiators, the proof for each, the category I compete in and the words my brand never uses."

3/ The Name and Tagline Lab
"Act as a naming specialist. Generate names and taglines for my brand. Analyze my positioning, my audience, the tone I want and the names my competitors use. Design fifteen name options in three styles, a reason behind each, five tagline options, a shortlist of my top three and quick checks to run on domains and social usernames."

4/ The Logo Concept Builder
"Act as a logo designer. Create logo concepts for my brand that I can preview right here. Analyze my brand name, my personality, my industry and where the logo will appear. Design three distinct concepts as simple vector graphics, with a wordmark, a symbol version, a small icon version and a note on why each one fits the brand."

5/ The Color and Type System
"Act as a visual identity designer. Build the color and typography system for my brand. Analyze my personality, my audience, my competitors' colors and the places my brand will show up. Design a primary palette and an accent palette with hex codes, the font pairing for headlines and body, the usage rules and a sample layout."

6/ The Brand Voice Guide
"Act as a brand copywriter. Define how my brand sounds everywhere it speaks. Analyze my positioning, my audience's language, three writers or brands I admire and the phrases I want to avoid. Design the voice traits, a do and do not list, sample lines for my website, social posts and emails, and a quick checklist for new copy."

7/ The Brand Kit Assembly
"Act as a brand manager. Assemble everything we built into one brand kit document. Analyze the positioning, the name, the logo concepts, the colors, the fonts and the voice. Design a clean brand guide with each part in order, the rules for using every asset, examples of right and wrong use and a launch checklist for my website and social profiles."
