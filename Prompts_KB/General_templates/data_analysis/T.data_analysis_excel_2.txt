🚨 8 Claude prompts to analyze Excel files like a pro. 👇🏻

1. UPLOAD THE EXCEL FILE
• Upload your Excel spreadsheet directly into Claude.
• Works best for sales, finance, marketing, inventory, KPIs, and business data.
• Claude automatically understands the spreadsheet’s structure and data.

2. ASK CLAUDE TO ANALYZE THE DATA
Prompt: “Analyze this spreadsheet like a senior business analyst. Explain what this data represents, identify the most important trends and patterns, detect anomalies or unusual values, point out missing information and possible errors, and provide high-level business insights that could help guide decision-making. Highlight anything that deserves further investigation and explain why it matters.”

3. GENERATE EXCEL FORMULAS AUTOMATICALLY
Prompt: “Generate the exact Excel formulas needed for this dataset. Help me calculate growth rates, profit margins, forecasts, averages, lookups, conditional logic, and other relevant metrics. For every formula, show me where it should be placed, explain what each part does, and provide a simple step-by-step explanation so I can apply it correctly. Use functions such as VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, INDEX MATCH, SUMIFS, IF statements, Pivot Table logic, and advanced calculations whenever appropriate.”

4. CLEAN + FIX THE DATA
Prompt: “Act as a professional data-cleaning specialist and audit this spreadsheet for data-quality problems. Identify duplicate records, inconsistent formatting, missing values, broken or incorrect formulas, unusual outliers, inconsistent entries, and any other issues that could affect the accuracy of the analysis. For each issue you find, explain what is wrong, why it matters, and recommend the best correction or cleanup method.”

5. ASK FOR BUSINESS INSIGHTS
Prompt: “Act as a senior strategy consultant analyzing this spreadsheet. Identify the biggest business opportunities, major risks, hidden trends, customer behavior patterns, key revenue drivers, and areas of underperformance. Use the data to explain what is happening, why it may be happening, and what actions the business should consider taking to improve growth, profitability, efficiency, and overall performance.”

6. GENERATE CHARTS + DASHBOARD IDEAS
Prompt: “Analyze this dataset and recommend the most effective charts and dashboard layouts for presenting the information. For each recommendation, explain why the visualization is useful, what specific insight it should reveal, which chart type is most appropriate, which metrics should be displayed, and what the executive takeaway should be. Organize the recommendations into a clear dashboard structure that makes the most important information easy to understand at a glance.”

7. BUILD AUTOMATION WORKFLOWS
Prompt: “Design an automated reporting workflow based on this spreadsheet. Show me how to structure recurring reports, KPI tracking, automated alerts, performance summaries, dashboards, and other useful reporting processes. Recommend which tasks should be automated, what should trigger each action, what outputs should be generated, and how the workflow could reduce manual work while keeping decision-makers updated with the latest information.”

8. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY MODE
Prompt: “Summarize this spreadsheet as if you were presenting the findings to senior executives. Keep the summary concise, strategic, data-driven, and easy to understand. Focus primarily on the most important decisions, risks, opportunities, performance changes, and recommended actions. Prioritize the insights that executives need to know rather than simply repeating the numbers in the spreadsheet.”

## 🚨 Forget Excel formulas.
Claude can handle your everyday Excel work in seconds.
Here are 10 powerful prompts to automate your most common tasks: 👇

PROMPT 1: Build Any Excel Formula
“Act as a Microsoft Excel expert with 10+ years of experience helping businesses
automate spreadsheets. I will describe what I want my Excel sheet to do in simple
words, and your job is to create the exact formula, function, or calculation
needed to achieve that result.
Explain the formula step by step, including what each part means, where I should
place it, and how I can modify it for different situations. If there is a simpler or
more efficient way to solve the problem, suggest that as well. My Excel task is:
[describe what you need].”

PROMPT 2: Clean and Organize Spreadsheet
“Act as a professional data cleaning specialist. I will upload an Excel spreadsheet
that contains messy, inconsistent, or poorly formatted data. Analyze the entire
spreadsheet and identify problems such as duplicate entries, missing values,
inconsistent formats, spelling mistakes, incorrect data types, unnecessary
columns, and structural issues. Then provide a clear action plan to clean and
organize the spreadsheet into a professional, analysis-ready format. Suggest
Excel formulas, Power Query steps, or automation methods whenever possible.
Here is my spreadsheet: [upload file].”

PROMPT 3: Analyze Excel Data
“Act as a senior data analyst who helps companies make better decisions using
Excel data. I will provide you with an Excel file containing business or personal
data. Analyze the information carefully and identify important trends, patterns,
correlations, unusual changes, opportunities, and potential problems. Explain
your findings in simple language that a non-technical person can understand.
Create a summary of the most important insights, recommend what actions
should be taken based on the data, and suggest additional analysis that could
provide more value. Here is my Excel file: [upload file].”

PROMPT 4: Create an Excel Dashboard
“Act as an expert Excel dashboard designer who creates executive-level reports. I
will provide you with raw Excel data, and your job is to transform it into a
professional dashboard concept. Analyze what information matters most,
identify the key performance indicators, recommend the best charts and
visualizations, and design a dashboard structure that is easy to understand.
Explain which Excel features should be used, including Pivot Tables, charts,
slicers, formulas, or conditional formatting. My goal is to create a dashboard for:
[business purpose]. Here is my data: [upload file].”

PROMPT 5: Find and Fix Excel Errors
“Act as an advanced Excel troubleshooting expert. I have an Excel spreadsheet
with formulas that are not working correctly, showing errors, or producing
unexpected results. Analyze the formulas, identify the exact reason behind the
problem, and explain what went wrong. Then provide the corrected formula and
explain how I can prevent similar issues in the future. Also check if there is a
better formula, function, or spreadsheet structure that would make the file more
reliable. Here is the formula or spreadsheet section: [paste formula/upload file].”

PROMPT 6: Automate Excel Work
“Act as an Excel automation consultant. Analyze my current spreadsheet
workflow and identify repetitive manual tasks that take too much time. Find
opportunities to automate the process using Excel formulas, Power Query, Pivot
Tables, VBA, macros, or other built-in Excel features. Explain exactly how I can
automate each step and create a faster workflow. Focus on reducing manual
work, improving accuracy, and making the spreadsheet easier to maintain. My
current Excel process is: [describe workflow].”

PROMPT 7: Turn Data Into a Report
“Act as a professional business reporting specialist. I will provide you with raw
Excel data, and your job is to transform it into a polished, professional report
that can be presented to managers, clients, or stakeholders. Analyze the data
structure, organize the information logically, create a clear summary section,
recommend important metrics, and suggest charts or visual elements that
improve understanding. Explain how the final report should be formatted and

purpose].”

PROMPT 8: Explain Any Excel Formula
“Act as an Excel instructor who teaches beginners and professionals. I will give
you an Excel formula, and your job is to explain it in a simple but detailed way.
Break down every function, symbol, reference, and calculation inside the
formula. Explain why each part is used, show a real-world example, and provide

better alternative formula, explain that too. The formula is: [paste formula].”

PROMPT 9: Create Excel Templates
“Act as an expert Excel template creator. I need a professional spreadsheet
template designed for a specific purpose. Based on my requirements, create the
ideal spreadsheet structure including column names, formulas, automation
ideas, formatting recommendations, data validation rules, and workflow
improvements. Make the template easy to use for beginners while still powerful
enough for professional needs. Explain how each section should work and how I
can customize it later. The template I need is for: [describe your requirement].”

PROMPT 10: Convert Data Into Insights
“Act as a data visualization and business intelligence expert. I will upload an
Excel spreadsheet containing raw information, and your job is to turn it into a

Claude can give you:
✅ The formula
✅ How it works
✅ Alternative approaches

3/ 📈 Turn your data into a dashboard

• Revenue metrics
