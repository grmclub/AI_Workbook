##Analyse excel files like a senior data analyst

1. UPLOAD THE EXCEL FILE
Start by uploading your spreadsheet directly into Claude.
Works best for:
• Sales data
• Financial reports
• Marketing analytics
• Inventory tracking
• KPI dashboards
• Business operations
Claude will automatically understand the structure of your spreadsheet before you begin the analysis.

2. ASK CLAUDE TO ANALYZE THE DATA
PROMPT:
"Analyze this spreadsheet like a senior business analyst. Explain what this data represents, identify key trends and anomalies, find missing values and possible mistakes, and highlight the most important high-level business insights."

3. GENERATE EXCEL FORMULAS AUTOMATICALLY
PROMPT:
"Generate the exact Excel formulas needed for this dataset. Help me calculate growth rates, profit margins, forecasting, averages, lookups, and conditional logic. Explain each formula clearly and show where it should be used."
Claude can generate VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, INDEX MATCH, SUMIFS, IF statements, Pivot logic, and advanced calculations.

PROMPT:
"Act as a data-cleaning specialist. Identify duplicates, inconsistent formatting, missing values, broken formulas, outliers, and other data-quality issues in this spreadsheet. Then suggest the appropriate corrections."

5. ASK FOR BUSINESS INSIGHTS
PROMPT:
"Act as a senior strategy consultant analyzing this spreadsheet. Identify the top opportunities, biggest risks, hidden trends, customer behavior patterns, revenue drivers, and specific recommendations for growth."

6. GENERATE CHARTS + DASHBOARD IDEAS
PROMPT:
"Suggest the best charts and dashboard layout for this dataset. For each recommendation, explain why it matters, what insight it reveals, the ideal chart type, and the key takeaway an executive should understand."


7. BUILD AUTOMATION WORKFLOWS
PROMPT:
"Design an automated reporting workflow using this spreadsheet. Include recurring reports, KPI tracking, alerts, summaries, dashboard structure, and practical automation recommendations that can turn this spreadsheet into an efficient reporting system."

8. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY MODE

## Analyze Excel Files Like a Data Analyst With These 8 Claude Prompts👇👇👇

1. Understand My Dataset
Prompt:
I’m uploading an Excel file. Act like a professional data analyst and first analyze the structure of this dataset. Identify the columns, data types, number of rows, missing values, duplicates, unusual entries, and any potential data-quality issues. Explain what this dataset appears to contain and suggest 5–10 useful analyses I could perform with it.

2. Clean My Excel Data
Prompt:
Analyze this Excel file and create a data-cleaning plan. Identify missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formatting, spelling variations, incorrect data types, outliers, and other errors. Explain exactly what should be cleaned and why. Do not change any values without first explaining the issue and recommended correction.

3. Find the Key Trends
Prompt:
Analyze this Excel dataset and identify the most important trends and patterns. Compare the relevant categories, dates, products, customers, or other variables. Highlight significant increases, decreases, unusual changes, and recurring patterns. Present the findings in simple language and explain what each trend could mean for the business.

4. Find My Best & Worst Performers
Prompt:
Using the Excel file, identify the top 10 and bottom 10 performers based on the most relevant performance metric. Rank them and explain why they performed well or poorly. Look for patterns that separate the high performers from the low performers and provide actionable recommendations based on your findings.

5. Find Hidden Insights
Prompt:
Act like a senior data analyst and look beyond the obvious numbers in this Excel file. Identify at least 10 interesting or unexpected insights, correlations, anomalies, or relationships in the data. For each insight, explain the evidence behind it, why it matters, and what action I should consider taking.

6. Create an Executive Report
Prompt:
Turn this Excel dataset into a concise executive-level data report. Include:
1. Executive summary
2. Key KPIs
3. Major trends
4. Top and bottom performers
5. Important anomalies
6. Key business insights
7. Recommended actions
8. Questions that require further investigation.
Keep the language clear enough for a non-technical manager to understand.

7. Tell Me Which Charts to Create
Prompt:
Analyze this Excel dataset and recommend the best charts or visualizations for presenting the most important insights. For each recommendation, tell me which columns to use, what type of chart to create, what question it answers, and what insight I should look for. Prioritize charts that would be useful in a professional business presentation or dashboard.

8. Turn the Data Into Action
Prompt:
Based on your analysis of this Excel file, identify the 5 most important actions a business should take. Rank them by potential impact and explain the data supporting each recommendation. Separate the findings into: What the data says → Why it matters → What we should do next. Also identify any assumptions or limitations that could affect your recommendations.
