##summarize before deepdiving into books & content for focusing on important things

1.Core Idea Extraction
Act as a professional knowledge analyst.
Read [Book Title] by [Author] and summarize the single most important idea the author is trying to communicate.
Explain it in plain language, as if you're teaching it to a smart 15-year-old.

2️.Chapter-by-Chapter Breakdown
Break down [Book Title] by [Author] chapter by chapter.
For each chapter, give me:
the key argument,
the main lesson, and
one sentence I can remember it by.

3️.Top Lessons for Real Life
From [Book Title] by [Author], extract the top 5 lessons that are actually useful in daily life.
Focus only on insights that improve thinking, decision-making, or habits—skip anything purely theoretical.

4️.Action Plan Builder
Turn the core concepts of [Book Title] by [Author] into a practical 7-day action plan.
Each day should have one small, specific action I can do to apply the book's ideas in real life.

5️.Memorable Quote Decoder
Pull out 5 of the most powerful quotes from [Book Title] by [Author].
For each quote, explain:
what it means,
why it matters, and
how I can use it today.

6️.Knowledge Compression
Compress [Book Title] by [Author] into the 10 most important concepts, frameworks, and principles.
Format it as a quick-reference cheat sheet I can review in under 3 minutes.

7️.Blind Spot Finder
What are the main criticisms or limitations of the ideas in [Book Title] by [Author]?
What does the author NOT tell you that you should know?
