🚨 BREAKING: Claude can now teach you like a private university tutor.
Copy these 8 prompts to simplify difficult topics, test your knowledge, and make studying feel 10× easier 👇

1. Build a Personal University Course
"Act as a university professor specializing in [subject]. Design a [number]-week course for my current level: [beginner/intermediate/advanced]. Include weekly topics, learning objectives, essential concepts, practical assignments, recommended resources, and a final project. I can study [hours] per week. Begin by giving me a diagnostic quiz, then adjust the course based on my answers."

2. Explain a Difficult Topic Clearly
"Teach me [topic] as a patient private tutor. Start with a simple explanation using an everyday analogy, then explain it at high-school and university levels. Include one practical example, the three most common misunderstandings, and five questions that test genuine understanding. Do not reveal the answers until I respond."

3. Use the Socratic Method
"Help me understand [topic/problem] using the Socratic method. Do not immediately give me the answer. Ask one focused question at a time, analyze my response, identify gaps in my reasoning, and guide me toward the solution with progressively stronger hints. At the end, summarize what I understood and what I should review."

4. Turn My Notes Into a Lesson
"Act as an expert lecturer. Convert the notes below into a structured lesson for a [level] student. Organize them into key ideas, definitions, examples, connections, and likely exam questions. Correct inaccurate information, clearly mark anything uncertain, and finish with a concise revision sheet.
My notes:
[PASTE NOTES]"

5. Create an Adaptive Study Session
"Run a [30/45/60]-minute tutoring session on [topic]. Divide it into explanation, guided practice, independent practice, and review. Ask one question at a time and adapt the difficulty to my answers. If I struggle, simplify the concept and provide a hint. If I succeed, increase the difficulty. Finish with my score, weak areas, and next steps."

6. Prepare Me for an Exam
"Act as an examiner for [subject/exam name]. Create a realistic practice exam covering [topics], with [number] questions in these formats: [multiple choice/short answer/essay/problems]. Match this difficulty: [easy/moderate/hard]. Let me answer first. Then grade each response, explain every mistake, show a model answer, and create a targeted revision plan."

7. Test Whether I Truly Understand
"Interview me about [topic] like a strict university professor conducting an oral examination. Begin with foundational questions, then move to application, comparison, and critical-thinking questions. Ask one at a time and challenge vague answers. After [number] questions, grade my understanding from 1–10 and explain exactly how I can improve."

8. Learn Through the Feynman Technique
"Help me master [topic] using the Feynman Technique. First, ask me to explain it in my own words. Analyze my explanation for missing ideas, incorrect assumptions, and unnecessary jargon. Teach only the parts I misunderstood using simple examples. Then ask me to explain it again until I can give a clear, accurate explanation."

9. Create a Science-Based Revision System
"Turn [subject/material] into a revision system using active recall, spaced repetition, and interleaving. I have [number] days/weeks before [exam/deadline] and can study [minutes] per day. Create a daily schedule, recall questions, flashcards, review intervals, and weekly checkpoints. Prioritize my weakest topics: [list weaknesses]."

10. Give Detailed Feedback on My Work
"Act as a university tutor evaluating my [essay/solution/report/assignment]. Use these criteria: [paste rubric or criteria]. Identify what is strong, what is unclear, factual or logical errors, and the three changes that would improve my grade most. Give specific revision instructions and examples, but do not rewrite the entire work unless I ask.
My work:
[PASTE WORK]"

🚨 STOP MAKING STUDENTS LEARN THROUGH TEXT ALONE.
Turn complex lessons into visual learning with ChatGPT.
Here are 7 prompts to create infographics, concept maps, timelines, diagrams, study guides, and educational comics for any subject 👇

1️. Create an Educational Infographic
Prompt:
"Create a clear, visually engaging, classroom-ready educational infographic about [insert topic] in [insert subject]. Design it for [insert learner type: young learners / teenagers / adults] at [insert grade or learning level]. Identify the most important concepts, facts, terminology, explanations, and examples students need to understand the topic. Organize related information into logical sections and prioritize essential content over unnecessary detail. Use appropriate visual elements, such as illustrations, icons, diagrams, charts, labels, or visual callouts, to clarify key ideas and make complex information easier to understand and remember. Establish a strong visual hierarchy with a clear title, concise sections, highlighted keywords, and easy-to-scan information. Keep the text brief, avoid overcrowding, and ensure the final infographic is accurate, age-appropriate, visually appealing, and suitable for classroom display or digital use."

2️. Create a Concept Map
Prompt:
"Create a clear, visually engaging, classroom-ready concept map about [insert topic] in [insert subject]. Design it for [insert learner type: young learners / teenagers / adults] at [insert grade or learning level]. Place the central concept prominently and organize the most important related ideas, categories, subtopics, and supporting details around it. Show meaningful relationships between concepts using clearly labeled branches, connecting lines, arrows, or other appropriate visual cues. Use concise keywords and short explanations rather than lengthy text, and include relevant icons, symbols, or simple illustrations where they improve understanding. Arrange the information logically so students can easily see how the different ideas connect and how each part relates to the overall topic. Keep the design uncluttered, maintain a strong visual hierarchy, and ensure the final concept map is accurate, age-appropriate, visually appealing, and suitable for classroom display or digital use."

3️. Create an Educational Timeline
Prompt:
"Create a clear, visually engaging, classroom-ready educational timeline about [insert topic] in [insert subject]. Maintain a clear visual hierarchy with a strong title, readable dates, concise descriptions, and clearly defined stages or periods. 🔥 THE SECRET TO STUDYING SMARTER
Don’t use AI only to summarize your notes.
Use it to test what you know, challenge your understanding, and help you correct your mistakes.
Try this simple study routine:
📖 Step 1: Study a topic for 25–40 minutes.
🧠 Step 2: Close your notes and explain what you remember.
❓ Step 3: Use AI to generate practice questions.
✍️ Step 4: Answer without looking at the solutions.
🔍 Step 5: Review your mistakes and revisit weak areas.
🔁 Step 6: Test yourself again later.
This approach encourages active recall and spaced revision instead of relying entirely on passive rereading.
⚠️ IMPORTANT
AI can make mistakes, generate incorrect explanations, or provide inaccurate answers.
Always verify important facts, formulas, and academic claims against your textbooks, lecture notes, and recommended course materials.
Use AI as a study assistant, not as a replacement for thinking.
