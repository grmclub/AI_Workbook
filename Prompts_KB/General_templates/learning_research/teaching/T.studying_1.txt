🔥 THE SECRET TO STUDYING SMARTER
Don’t use AI only to summarize your notes.
Use it to test what you know, challenge your understanding, and help you correct your mistakes.
Try this simple study routine:
📖 Step 1: Study a topic for 25–40 minutes.
🧠 Step 2: Close your notes and explain what you remember.
❓ Step 3: Use AI to generate practice questions.
✍️ Step 4: Answer without looking at the solutions.
🔍 Step 5: Review your mistakes and revisit weak areas.
🔁 Step 6: Test yourself again later.
This approach encourages active recall and spaced revision instead of relying entirely on passive rereading.
⚠️ IMPORTANT
AI can make mistakes, generate incorrect explanations, or provide inaccurate answers.
Always verify important facts, formulas, and academic claims against your textbooks, lecture notes, and recommended course materials.
Use AI as a study assistant, not as a replacement for thinking.
