## 8 ChatGPT Prompts for Creating Comprehensive English Lesson Plans in Word Format 👇👇

1️. Create a Reading Lesson Plan
Prompt:
"Create a comprehensive English reading lesson plan for [age/grade] learners at [CEFR level] on [topic/text]. Use the PWP framework: Pre Reading, While Reading, and Post Reading. Include clear learning objectives, required materials, stage by stage procedures, timing, interaction patterns, teacher instructions, differentiation, anticipated problems and solutions, and assessment. In the Pre Reading stage, activate prior knowledge, introduce the context, and prepare learners for the text. In the While Reading stage, progress from understanding the main idea to identifying key details and deeper comprehension where appropriate. In the Post Reading stage, extend the text through meaningful speaking, writing, or language work. Ensure all activities are scaffolded, engaging, and appropriate to the learners’ CEFR level. Present the complete lesson plan in a professionally structured, editable Word document, using clear headings and tables where appropriate."

5️. Create a Speaking Lesson Plan

8️. Create a Diagnostic and Responsive Lesson Plan

## Here are 7 ChatGPT prompts to help teachers create clear, practical, and customizable resources. 👇

1️. Create a Professional Teaching Portfolio
Prompt:
"Create a professional teaching portfolio based on [subject, grade/age/level, experience, qualifications, roles, and other relevant details] for [evaluation/job application/promotion/accreditation/professional development/other purpose]. Organize it into relevant sections such as professional profile, teaching philosophy, qualifications and experience, curriculum and instructional planning, teaching and assessment practices, evidence of student learning, professional development, collaboration, achievements, and reflective practice. For each section, identify what information and supporting evidence to include, distinguish claims from evidence, and suggest appropriate artifacts such as plans, assessments, anonymized student work, observation feedback, certificates, and reflections. Keep the portfolio clear, concise, professional, and coherent. Do not invent information or evidence, and identify anything important that is missing."

2️. Create an Annual Curriculum Distribution
Prompt:
"Create an annual curriculum distribution for [subject] for [grade/age/level] during [academic year], based on these curriculum requirements: [units, topics, objectives, standards, or curriculum content]. Consider [teaching weeks, terms/semesters, holidays, exams, school events, and other constraints]. Distribute the curriculum realistically and logically, allowing sufficient time for instruction, practice, review, assessment, and consolidation. Give more time to demanding content where needed and explain why. Do not add, omit, or alter curriculum requirements. Present the plan as a clear table or timeline showing the term, week/period, unit/topic, key objectives/content, duration, and assessment/review points. Flag any scheduling conflicts, unrealistic pacing, or content that cannot fit within the available time."

3️. Create a Term or Semester Plan
Prompt:
"Create a detailed [term/semester] plan for [subject] for [grade/age/level] during [term/semester and academic year], based on [annual curriculum distribution and curriculum requirements]. Consider [teaching weeks, lesson frequency and duration, holidays, exams, school events, and other constraints]. Organize the required curriculum into a logical, realistic sequence of units, topics, and objectives, allowing sufficient time for instruction, practice, review, assessment, and consolidation. Align it with the annual curriculum distribution without adding, omitting, or altering required content. Include assessment and review points, with flexibility for interruptions and different learning paces. Present the plan as a clear table showing the week/period, unit/topic, objectives/content, duration, assessment/review points, and notes. Flag any pacing problems, scheduling conflicts, or requirements that may need adjustment."

4️. Create a Unit Plan
Prompt:
"Create a unit plan for [unit/topic] in [subject] for [grade/age/level], based on these curriculum requirements, standards, or objectives: [details]. Plan the unit across [number of lessons/weeks], with each lesson lasting [duration], and organize learning in a logical progression toward the objectives. For each lesson, include learning objectives, key content, teaching and learning activities, resources, practice opportunities, differentiation or support, and formative assessment. Include review, consolidation, and summative assessment where appropriate, ensuring alignment with the objectives. Do not add, omit, or alter required curriculum content. Present the plan as a clear table showing the lesson/sequence, objectives, content, activities, resources, assessment, and differentiation/support. Check the final plan for curriculum alignment, progression, realistic pacing, and coherence."

5️. Create a Lesson Plan
Prompt:
"Create a practical lesson plan for [lesson topic] in [subject] for [grade/age/level], based on [curriculum requirements, learning objectives, unit plan, and prior learning]. Plan the [duration] lesson with a clear beginning, development, and conclusion aligned with the objectives. For each stage, include its purpose, teacher actions, student activities, timing, resources, and checks for understanding. Include explanation, modeling, questioning, guided and independent practice, interaction, feedback, and consolidation where appropriate. Anticipate likely difficulties or misconceptions and provide suitable scaffolding, differentiation, or support. Include a brief formative assessment or exit task. Do not add or alter curriculum requirements. Present the plan as a clear, classroom-ready table and check it for alignment, sequencing, pacing, feasibility, and assessment."

7️. Create a Professional Reflection and Development Record
Prompt:
"Create a professional reflection and development record based on [teaching experience, professional development activity, classroom evidence, observation feedback, or professional goal]. Document what occurred, what I learned, how it relates to my teaching, and my next steps. Go beyond description by identifying key insights, challenges, assumptions, and implications for future practice. Distinguish clearly between what the evidence demonstrates and what can only be inferred. Identify professional learning priorities and realistic actions, including applying new learning, seeking feedback or collaboration, collecting further evidence, and reviewing progress. Do not invent experiences, achievements, evidence, activities, or outcomes. Present the record in a clear, professional format suitable for a teaching portfolio, appraisal record, professional development file, or learning journal."

6️. Create Assessment and Progress-Tracking Materials
Prompt:
"Create assessment and progress-tracking materials for [subject] for [grade/age/level], based on [curriculum requirements, learning objectives, and assessment criteria]. Choose the most appropriate format, such as a rubric, checklist, student progress tracker, or class assessment record. Ensure all criteria are clear, observable, age appropriate, and aligned with the intended learning. Where performance levels are needed, provide meaningful descriptors that clearly distinguish achievement levels. Organize objectives, skills, or criteria so progress can be recorded and reviewed over time. Do not invent student results, curriculum requirements, or assessment evidence. Present the materials in a clear, practical format suitable for regular classroom use."
