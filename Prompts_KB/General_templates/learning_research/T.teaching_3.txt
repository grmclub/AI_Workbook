Prompt:
"Create a clear, visually engaging, classroom-ready educational infographic about [insert topic] in [insert subject]. Design it for [insert learner type: young learners / teenagers / adults] at [insert grade or learning level]. Identify the most important concepts, facts, terminology, explanations, and examples students need to understand the topic. Organize related information into logical sections and prioritize essential content over unnecessary detail. Use appropriate visual elements, such as illustrations, icons, diagrams, charts, labels, or visual callouts, to clarify key ideas and make complex information easier to understand and remember. Establish a strong visual hierarchy with a clear title, concise sections, highlighted keywords, and easy-to-scan information. Keep the text brief, avoid overcrowding, and ensure the final infographic is accurate, age-appropriate, visually appealing, and suitable for classroom display or digital use."

Prompt:

Use concise keywords and short explanations rather than lengthy text, and include relevant icons, symbols, or simple illustrations where they improve understanding. Arrange the information logically so students can easily see how the different ideas connect and how each part relates to the overall topic. Keep the design uncluttered, maintain a strong visual hierarchy, and ensure the final concept map is accurate, age-appropriate, visually appealing, and suitable for classroom display or digital use."
