ChatGPT 6 Astra can do way more than you think.
These 10 prompts turn hours into minutes 👇

1/ TURN ANY IDEA INTO A BUSINESS
"Act as a startup strategist.
Take this idea: [IDEA]
Turn it into a realistic business model.
Give me:
* Target customer
* Painful problem
* Core offer
* Pricing
* Fastest way to validate demand
* 3 ways to acquire the first 10 customers
* A 7-day launch plan
Prioritise ideas I can test without building a full product."

2/ THE 80/20 RESEARCH PROMPT
"Research [TOPIC] and identify the 20% of information that will give me 80% of the understanding.
Separate your answer into:
1. What I absolutely need to know
2. What most people misunderstand
3. What experts pay attention to
4. What is mostly noise
5. The 5 best questions I should ask next."

3/ TURN A GOAL INTO A SYSTEM
"I want to achieve [GOAL].
Don’t just give me advice.
Reverse-engineer the goal into:
* Monthly target
* Weekly milestones
* Daily actions
* Leading indicators
* Biggest bottlenecks
* What I should stop doing
* A simple tracking system
Optimise the plan for consistency, not motivation."

4/ FIND THE MONEY
"Analyse this business:
[BUSINESS / WEBSITE / DESCRIPTION]
Find the 5 biggest opportunities to increase revenue.
Look specifically for:
* Pricing opportunities
* Upsells
* Recurring revenue
* Lead generation
* Conversion improvements
* Automation opportunities
* Products or services we could add
Rank each opportunity by potential impact, difficulty and speed to implement."

5/ LEARN ANYTHING FASTER
"Teach me [TOPIC] like an expert tutor.
Assume I’m intelligent but completely new to it.
Start with the mental model I need first.
Then explain:
* Core concepts
* Simple analogy
* Real-world example
* Common beginner mistakes
* What experts understand differently
Finish by testing me with 5 increasingly difficult questions."

6/ THE DEVIL’S ADVOCATE PROMPT
"I’m considering this decision:
[DECISION]
Don’t agree with me automatically.
Act as a critical strategic adviser.
Identify:
* Assumptions I’m making
* What could go wrong
* What I’m overlooking
* Strongest argument against this
* Opportunity cost
* What evidence would change the decision
Then give me your recommended decision and explain why."

7/ TURN MESSY NOTES INTO ACTION
"Turn these messy notes into an execution plan:
[PASTE NOTES]
Extract:
* Key decisions
* Action items
* Priorities
* Owners
* Deadlines
* Open questions
* Risks
Then create a prioritised ‘What happens next’ section.
Do not summarise things that don’t require action."

8/ CREATE A WEEK OF CONTENT
"Act as my content strategist.
My expertise: [EXPERTISE]
My audience: [AUDIENCE]
My offer: [OFFER]
Create 7 content ideas designed around:
* Contrarian opinions
* Mistakes
* Tutorials
* Case studies
* Personal insights
* Useful frameworks
* Industry changes
For each, give me:
Hook
Core idea
3 talking points
CTA
Avoid generic AI-sounding hooks."

9/ BUILD MY AI WORKFORCE
"Analyse this role or workflow:
[ROLE / WORKFLOW]
Break every task into:
* Human should do it
* AI should assist
* AI can fully automate
For anything AI can handle, recommend the simplest workflow.
Then redesign the entire process to minimise repetitive human work while keeping humans responsible for judgement, relationships and high-impact decisions."

10/ THE CEO PROMPT
"Act as my AI chief of staff.
Here’s everything currently on my plate:
[PASTE TASKS / PROJECTS / PROBLEMS]
Organise it into:
1. Do today
2. Do this week
3. Delegate
4. Automate
5. Delay
6. Delete
Identify the single highest-leverage thing I should work on next.
Explain why.
Then give me the first 3 actions to take."
Most people use ChatGPT like Google with a text box.
Astra gets much more useful when you give it a role, context, constraints and a clear outcome.
