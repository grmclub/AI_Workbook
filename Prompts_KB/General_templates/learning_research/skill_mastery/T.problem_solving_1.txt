# 10 Chain of thought Prompts for complex problems.👇

1. First Principles Thinking Prompt
Prompt:
"I want to solve [Insert Complex Challenge]. Break this problem down into its fundamental truths and basic components. Strip away all assumptions and conventional wisdom. Once you have isolated the basic truths, build a step-by-step solution from scratch." 
Framework Breakdown:
    1. Break down the problem 
    2. Find the fundamental truths 
    3. Remove assumptions 
    4. Build a step-by-step solution 
	
2. The 5 Whys Framework Prompt
Prompt:
"We need to solve [Insert Issue]. Ask and answer 'Why did this happen?' five consecutive times, with each answer driving the next question deeper into the core issue. After the 5th 'Why', present a preventive solution based on the ultimate finding." 	
	
3. Root Cause Analysis Prompt
Prompt:
"I am facing this issue: [Insert Problem]. Do not give me the solution yet. First, list 5 potential root causes. Second, evaluate the probability of each cause based on the symptoms provided. Third, identify the primary root cause. Finally, propose an actionable fix for that primary cause." 
	

4. Multi-Perspective Evaluation Prompt
Prompt:
"I am considering [Insert Decision/Strategy]. Before making a recommendation, analyze this decision from 3 distinct viewpoints: 1) The Financial/Budgetary Lens, 2) The User/Customer Experience Lens, and 3) The Technical/Operational Feasibility Lens. Show your reasoning for each, and then conclude with a balanced verdict." 
Framework Breakdown:
    1. Financial/Budgetary Lens: Cost implications, ROI and value, Budget feasibility, Long-term financial impact 
    2. User/Customer Experience Lens: Needs & pain points, Satisfaction & usability, Adoption potential, Brand perception 
    3. Technical/Operational Feasibility Lens: Technical requirements, Resource availability, Implementation complexity, Scalability & risk 
    4. Balanced Verdict: Weighing all three perspectives for the best outcome. 

5. Pre-Mortem Risk Assessment Prompt
Prompt:
"Imagine it is 6 months from now and our plan to [Insert Project/Goal] has completely failed. Walk me through the step-by-step timeline of how it failed, identifying the top 3 weak points that led to the collapse. Then, provide mitigation steps to fix those 3 vulnerabilities today." 
Framework Breakdown:
    1. The Failure Timeline:
        ◦ 1. Early Signs: What went wrong at the beginning? 
        ◦ 2. Escalation: How did it get worse over time? 
        ◦ 3. Breaking Point: What caused the final collapse? 
        ◦ 4. End Result: How did the plan fully fail? 
    2. Top 3 Weak Points: Identify major vulnerability 1, second critical gap, and third key failure point. 
    3. Mitigation Steps: Actions to take now, processes/resources/controls to add, and safeguards to prevent failure. 


6. Step-by-Step Mathematical & Logic Solver
Problem Example:
"A factory produces 120 units per day. The production cost per unit is $8, and the selling price per unit is $15. If the factory operates for 25 days, what is the total profit?" 
Step-by-Step Solution Breakdown:
    1. Identify Given Values:
        ◦ Units per day = 120
        ◦ Cost per unit = $8
        ◦ Selling price per unit = $15
        ◦ Number of days = 25
    2. Calculate Daily Profit Per Unit:
        ◦ Profit per unit = Selling price - Cost = 15 - 8 = $7
    3. Calculate Daily Total Profit:
        ◦ Daily profit = Units per day * Profit per unit = 120 * 7 = $840
    4. Calculate Total Profit for 25 Days:
        ◦ Total profit = Daily profit * Number of days = 840 * 25 = $21,000
    5. Verify Calculation: All calculations are consistent. 
    6. Final Answer: Total Profit = $21,000
