🚨 BREAKING: Claude can help you MASTER complex topics in 30 minutes — for FREE.
Here are 8 powerful prompts to research smarter, learn faster, and think deeper. 👇

1. Diagnose the Complexity
> Act as a Learning Strategist. I need to understand [complex topic/concept] but have no formal background in [relevant field]. Identify exactly what makes it difficult—terminology, abstract logic, or missing prerequisites. Then list the essential concepts I should learn first.

2. Build the Foundation
> Act as a Patient Subject Matter Tutor. Based on the prerequisite gaps for [complex topic], explain only the essential concepts I’m missing in simple language. Keep each concept to 2–3 sentences, and skip anything not directly needed to understand the topic.

3. Simplify the Core
> Act as an Expert Explainer. Explain [complex topic] in the simplest possible terms using a clear analogy or everyday example. Remove unnecessary jargon while keeping the explanation accurate. Teach it as if I’m smart but completely new to the field.

4. Build Toward Mastery
> Act as a Progressive Complexity Tutor. Using the simplified explanation of [complex topic], gradually add the nuance and complexity needed for real understanding. Introduce one layer at a time, ensuring each builds logically on the previous one.

5. Connect to What You Know
> Act as a Learning Integration Coach. For [complex topic], connect the new ideas to what I already know from [background/field/interests]. Create clear mental bridges that make the concept easier to remember and use—not just memorize.

6. Test Your Understanding
> Act as a Socratic Tutor. Test my understanding of [complex topic] with 3 questions that require real comprehension, not memorization. Start with a basic application, then increase the difficulty to a novel scenario I haven’t seen before.

7. Find Your Blind Spots
> Act as a Knowledge Gap Auditor. Based on my answers about [complex topic], identify what I truly understand versus what I’m only pattern-matching. Be direct about my remaining knowledge gaps and explain exactly what I need to improve.

8. Prove You’ve Mastered It
> Act as a Mastery Verification Coach. Ask me to explain [complex topic] in my own words as if teaching a beginner. Then analyze my explanation for gaps, oversimplifications, and inaccuracies that reveal incomplete understanding.
