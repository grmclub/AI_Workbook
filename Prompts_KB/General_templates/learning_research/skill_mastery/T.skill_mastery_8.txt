##STOP WATCHING TUTORIALS YOU NEVER FINISH. Use these 8 prompts to learn by doing.

THE PROBLEM:
Tutorials = Passive. You watch someone else do it. You forget in 24 hours.
Doing = Active. You do it badly, you fix it, you remember forever.

PROMPT 1: THE DOING PLAN
"I want to learn [Skill: e.g. Etsy, Canva, ChatGPT]. Don't give me a course. Give me a 7-day DOING plan: Day 1: Do X in 30 mins, Day 2: Do Y, etc. Each day = 1 action + 1 output. No watching. No reading. Only doing. If I finish Day 7, what will I have built?"
My 7-day plan for Etsy: Day 1: Make 1 listing, Day 2: Fix title, Day 3: Post it... Day 7: I had a shop. Not a certificate.

PROMPT 2: THE PROJECT BUILDER
"I want to learn [Skill]. Give me 1 project to build that forces me to learn it. Project: [e.g. Build a 5-page website, Design a planner]. Break it into 5 steps. For each step, tell me: What to do, What to Google if I'm stuck, What DONE looks like. No theory. Only project."
You don't learn Canva by watching Canva. You learn by designing a planner in Canva.

PROMPT 3: THE MISTAKE TEACHER
"I tried to [Do X] and got stuck: [Paste error / what happened]. Don't give me the answer. Give me 3 hints to fix it myself. Then ask me: What did you try? What do you think is wrong? Teach me to debug, not to copy."
This is how you actually learn. Not by copying the answer, but by finding it.

PROMPT 4: THE 80/20 SKILL FINDER
"For [Skill], what are the 20% of skills that give 80% of results? Give me only 3 skills to learn. Ignore the other 97%. For each skill, give me 1 exercise to practice it in 20 mins today."
Example for ChatGPT: Prompting, Follow-ups, Projects. Not 100 features. Just 3.

PROMPT 5: THE TEACH-BACK TEST
"I just learned [Concept]. Test me: Ask me 3 questions that force me to explain it in my own words to a 12-year-old. If I can't explain it, I didn't learn it. Correct me only after I try."
If you can't teach it, you don't know it. You just watched it.

PROMPT 6: THE REAL FEEDBACK
"Here is what I built: [Paste / Upload]. Roast it like a $10k coach. Tell me: What is good, What is broken, What is the 1 thing to fix first. Don't be nice. Give me a score /10 and 1 action to improve it today."
Feedback beats tutorials. One roast taught me more than 10 tutorials.

PROMPT 7: THE 30-MINUTE SPRINT
"I have 30 minutes to learn [Skill]. Build me a 30-min sprint: 0-5 mins: Do X, 5-20 mins: Build Y, 20-30 mins: Review and fix. No watching. Timer on. What will I have at the end?"
I do this every morning. 30 mins doing > 3 hours watching.

PROMPT 8: THE NO-MORE-TUTORIALS CONTRACT
"Act as my accountability coach. I keep watching tutorials and never doing. Write me a contract: I will not watch another tutorial until I have built [Project]. If I want to watch one, I must first do [Action] for 30 mins. Make it harsh. Make me sign it by replying 'I agree'."
I signed it. I stopped watching. I started building.
MY NEW RULE:
1 Tutorial = 1 Project
For every 30 mins I watch, I must do 30 mins.
If I can't do it, I don't need to watch it.
You don't have a learning problem.
You have a doing problem
