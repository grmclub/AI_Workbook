TURN ANY BOOK INTO A SKILL YOU CAN ACTUALLY USE USING THESE PROMPTS 👇🏽👇🏽
📚 BOOK → SKILL SYSTEM

## EXTRACT THE USEFUL KNOWLEDGE
"I am reading [BOOK] because I want to improve [SKILL OR OUTCOME].
Identify the ideas that can change what I do—not merely what I know. For each useful idea, explain the decision or behaviour it should change, give one realistic example and mention when the advice may not apply.
Separate the results into: essential principles, useful supporting ideas and interesting information I can safely ignore for now.
Do not summarize every chapter. Prioritize the smallest number of ideas that could create the largest practical improvement."

1. Extract
"Extract the 10 most important ideas from this book. Ignore repetition, stories, and filler. Focus only on ideas that can change how I think or act."
2. Simplify
"Explain each idea in simple language, as if teaching someone with no background knowledge. Give one practical example for each."
3. Convert to skills
"Turn the book’s key ideas into specific skills I can practice. For each skill, tell me exactly what I should be able to do when I have mastered it."
4. Build exercises
"Create practical exercises based directly on the book. Make them progressively harder and designed for real-world application."
5. Create a 7-day challenge
"Turn the book into a 7-day action challenge. Give me one concrete task per day that forces me to apply the book rather than just read it."
6. Apply it to my life
"Analyze how the principles in this book could apply to my current work, finances, business, relationships, leadership, and personal development. Identify specific opportunities."
7. Create decision rules
"Turn the book's most useful principles into 10 simple decision rules I can use when making real-life decisions."
8. Create a checklist
"Create a one-page checklist I can use repeatedly to apply the book’s ideas in real situations."
9. Test me
"Act as a strict coach. Test whether I actually understand and can apply the book. Give me scenarios and grade my responses."
10. Build a mastery system
"Create a 30-day plan to turn this book into a practical skill. Include daily practice, weekly reviews, measurable targets, and a final test."

🔥 The ultimate prompt
"Turn this book from information into capability. Extract its highest-value principles, identify the skills behind those principles, teach each skill simply, create real-world exercises, give me scenarios to solve, challenge my assumptions, identify where the author’s ideas may be weak or outdated, and build a 30-day practice system. By the end, I should be able to demonstrate the ideas through my actions rather than merely explain them."
