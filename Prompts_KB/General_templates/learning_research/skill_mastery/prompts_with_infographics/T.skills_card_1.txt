## AI Study Prompt Cards

1. The Dumb It Down + Trap Detector

	Prompt:
	Act as a top 1% professor in [SUBJECT]. Explain this chapter: [PASTE CHAPTER / UPLOAD PDF].

	Rules:
	1. Explain like I'm 12, no jargon
	2. Give me 3 real-life examples I will actually remember
	3. Tell me 5 traps professors put in exams from this chapter. If I get these wrong, I fail. Be brutal.

2. The Exam Question Predictor

	Prompt:
	This is my chapter: [PASTE CHAPTER]. You are the professor who sets the paper for [UNIVERSITY / Degree]. Based on this, predict:

	1. 5 MCQs with answers
	2. 3 short questions (5 marks)
	3. 2 long questions (15 marks) that WILL come.

	Give me model answers in topper style - to the point, diagram where needed.

3. The Memory Machine

	Prompt:
	I forget everything after reading: [TOPIC].
	Turn this chapter into:

	1. 10 flashcards - Question on front, 1-line answer on back
	2. 1 mnemonic story to remember the whole flow
	3. 1 mind map in text format: Main idea -> Branches -> Keywords.

	Make it so I remember it even during exam panic.

4. The Feynman Executioner

	Prompt:
	I will explain [TOPIC FROM CHAPTER] to you in my own words: [PASTE YOUR EXPLANATION]. Act as a strict examiner.

	1. Roast me where I'm vague/wrong
	   - Point out what's unclear.
	   - Tell me where I'm wrong.
	   - Don't be nice. Be honest.

	2. Ask me 3 follow-up questions that will expose if I really understood
	   - Why is this important?
	   - How does this connect to [another concept]?
	   - What would happen if [condition] changed?

	3. Then give me the perfect 90-second answer I should have given.

	Don't let me fool myself that I know it.

5. The One-Page Topper Notes

	Prompt:
	Take this chapter: [PASTE CHAPTER] and condense it into 1 page that a topper would write the night before exam.

	Include:
	- All formulas / definitions / dates
	- 3 diagrams described in text I can draw
	- What to skip vs what is MUST-LEARN.

	No paragraphs - only bullets, tables, and triggers.
	If I read only this, I should get 80%+.

##20 ChatGPT Prompts to Think Better
Use AI to test your thinking, not just speed up your work.
FASTER OUTPUT → BETTER JUDGMENT

1. The Real Shift
Most people use ChatGPT for speed. Smart operators use it for judgment.
    • Save time rightarrow Think better
    • Watch out for: Hidden bias, Weak assumptions, Missed consequences, Gut-driven decisions

2. See the Full Problem
    • 01. First Principles Thinking
      Strip the problem down to what is actually true.
    • 02. Multi Perspective Analysis
      View the decision through different stakeholders and lenses.
    • 03. Root Cause Analysis
      Trace symptoms back through causes and dependencies.
    • 04. Systems Thinking
      See relationships, feedback loops, and connected consequences.

3. Stress Test Your Thinking
    • 05. Cognitive Bias Detection
      Identify the bias shaping your judgment.
    • 06. Devil's Advocate Stress Test
      Attack the strongest version of your own idea.
    • 07. Assumption Audit
      Surface assumptions and rank the fragile ones.
    • 08. Red Team Analysis
      Expose blind spots your team may be missing.

4. Make Better Choices
    • 09. Decision Matrix Builder
      Compare options against the criteria that matter.
    • 10. Opportunity Cost Evaluation
      Identify what you give up by choosing one path.
    • 11. Strategic Tradeoff
      Clarify what you gain, sacrifice, and prioritize.
    • 12. Second Order Thinking
      Map what happens after the obvious first result.
    • 13. Scenario Planning
      Explore optimistic, realistic, pessimistic, and disruptive futures.

5. Test Logic and Evidence
    • 14. Argument Strength Evaluator
      Check whether the reasoning holds up under scrutiny.
    • 15. Evidence Quality Analyzer
      Separate strong evidence from weak claims.
    • 16. Hypothesis Testing
      Define what evidence would support or reject an idea.

6. Expand How You Think
    • 17. Mental Model Stacking
      Examine the same problem through multiple useful models.
    • 18. Inversion Thinking
      Ask what would guarantee failure, then work backward.
    • 19. Strategic Question Generator
      Surface the questions you did not know you needed to ask.

7. Put It Into Practice
    • 20. Master Critical Thinking Framework
      Combine multiple reasoning tools to examine the problem, challenge assumptions, test evidence, compare choices, and decide clearly.
Workflow Steps:
    1. Pick 2–3 Prompts: Choose the frameworks that match your decision.
    2. Run a Real Decision: Use an actual problem you are working through.
    3. Look for Clarity: Find blind spots before adding another meeting.

##Stop wasting hours on work AI can do.
These 11 tools help you think, learn, build, and execute faster every day.
Here are the 11 AI tools worth trying 👇

1/ Build Your Thinking & Learning System
AI becomes valuable when it improves how you think and learn.
➔ ChatGPT: Generate ideas, draft content, organize thoughts, and solve problems faster.
➔ Gemini: Research topics, brainstorm alternatives, and explore different perspectives.
➔ NotebookLM: Upload PDFs, videos, and URLs to generate summaries, insights, Q&A, and audio discussions.
Instead of spending hours consuming information, use AI to extract the key ideas first.
The goal is not to read more.
The goal is to understand faster.

2/ Eliminate Information & Meeting Overload
A huge amount of productivity is lost collecting information.
These tools reduce that friction.
➔ Otter.ai: Automatically records and transcribes meetings.
➔ Fireflies: Captures conversations and creates searchable meeting notes.
➔ Granola: Helps organize meeting insights without manual note-taking.
➔ Perplexity: Gives direct answers with citations so you spend less time digging through search results.
Focus your attention on decisions and strategy instead of documenting everything yourself.

3/ Build, Create & Communicate Faster
AI is making it possible to go from idea to execution much faster.
➔ Lovable: Turn product ideas into working applications.
➔ Bolt.new: Build and deploy projects directly from prompts.
➔ Replit: Develop and launch applications quickly in the browser.
➔ Napkin: Convert rough ideas into clear diagrams and visual explanations.
➔ ElevenLabs: Create realistic AI voiceovers and audio content.
The bottleneck is no longer building.
The bottleneck is deciding what to build.

4/ Accelerate Execution With AI
Once ideas are clear, execution becomes the priority.
➔ Codex: Helps generate and understand code.
➔ Cursor: AI-powered coding assistance directly inside your workflow.
➔ Claude Code: Assists with coding, debugging, and development tasks.
➔ Gamma: Create presentations and documents from simple prompts.
➔ Beautiful.ai: Design professional slide decks without starting from scratch.
These tools reduce the time between idea and finished output.

5/ Automate Work & Protect Focus
The biggest productivity gains come from removing repetitive work.
➔ Zapier: Connect apps and automate workflows without coding.
➔ n8n: Build advanced automation systems across multiple tools.
➔ Reclaim.ai: Automatically schedules tasks and protects focus time.
➔ Motion: Prioritizes work and organizes your calendar around important tasks.
The real power of AI isn't any single tool.
It's the system you build around them:
→ Think faster
→ Learn faster
→ Execute faster
→ Automate repetitive work
That's the modern productivity loop.
Most people will experiment with a few AI tools.
The people getting the biggest results are building complete systems.
