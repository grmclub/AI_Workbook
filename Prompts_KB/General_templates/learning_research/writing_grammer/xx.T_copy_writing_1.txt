## CHATGPT JUST BECAME YOUR COPY EDITOR
Trim weak lines, fix messy copy, research faster, and find new angles.
It can also upgrade CTAs, study rivals, and help you A/B test smarter.
Here are the exact ways to use it 👇

1/ How to Use ChatGPT to Improve Your Copy with 7 practical copywriting uses
ChatGPT is best as a copy assistant, not a magic writer.
Use it to:
→ tighten what you already wrote
→ find gaps and mistakes
→ generate structured options fast
Rule:
Give ChatGPT a clear goal, a target audience, and a format.
Checkpoint: If you do not provide the audience and offer, the output will sound generic.

2/ Use 1: Trim your copy without losing meaning
Goal:
→ Make copy shorter, clearer, more scannable
Focus on:
→ removing repeats, filler, and long sentences
→ keeping only the strongest claims
Prompt:
Trim this copy by 30%. Keep the meaning, keep the tone, keep all key benefits. Use short sentences and 1 to 2 line paragraphs:
[paste]
Checkpoint:
→ Compare before vs after and verify no benefit or proof was deleted.

3/ Use 2: Fix errors and improve clarity
Goal:
→ Clean grammar, simplify words, strengthen flow
Focus on:
→ active voice
→ simple words
→ logical order
Prompt:
Edit this copy for clarity and correctness. Use active voice, simple words, and short sentences. Keep the original message and tone:
[paste]
Checkpoint:
→ Read it out loud. If you stumble, shorten the sentence.

4/ Use 3: Conduct research for better hooks and claims
Goal:
→ Get buyer language, objections, and angles
Focus on:
→ pains, desired outcomes, fears, alternatives
Prompt:
I sell [offer] to [audience]. List:
top 10 pains in their words
top 10 desired outcomes
top 10 objections
Then write 10 hook ideas using those phrases.
Checkpoint:
→ Replace any vague phrase with a real example or number.

5/ Use 4: Generate ideas for angles, headlines, and structure
Goal:
→ Create more creative options without starting over
Focus on:
→ different angles: speed, simplicity, risk removal, proof, contrarian
Prompt:
Generate 15 angles for this offer. For each:
headline
core promise
3 bullets
Offer: [paste]
Audience: [paste]
Checkpoint:
→ Pick 3 angles and test them before writing 15 more.

6/ Use 5: Improve calls-to-action that people actually click
Goal:
→ Make the next step obvious and low friction
Focus on:
→ one action, one benefit, one risk reducer
Prompt:
Write 20 CTAs for this page/post.
Constraints:
max 8 words each
mix soft and direct CTAs
include 5 with a risk reducer (free, cancel anytime, no spam)
Context: [audience + offer + goal]
Checkpoint:
→ Your CTA should match intent. Do not push “buy now” to cold traffic.

7/ Use 6: Study competitors without copying
Goal:
→ Find positioning gaps and messaging opportunities
Focus on:
→ what they claim, what they avoid, what proof they use
Prompt:
Analyse these competitor homepages. Output:
their main promise
their USP
their proof style
what is missing
Then propose 5 positioning angles I can own.
Text:
[competitor copy pasted]
Checkpoint:
→ Only use insights. Never copy phrases.

8/ Use 7: A/B test variations for hooks, bullets, and offers
Goal:
→ Produce many on-brand variations quickly
Focus on:
→ changing one variable at a time
Variables to test:
→ headline
→ first 2 lines
→ bullet order
→ CTA wording
Prompt:
Create 10 variations of this headline.
Rules:
keep the same meaning
change the wording and structure
write 5 punchy and 5 curiosity-driven
Headline: [paste]
Checkpoint:
→ Test with real data. Pick a metric (CTR, reply rate, conversion) and run the winner.

9/ A simple workflow to use every time
Do this for any copy:
→ Draft it yourself in rough form
→ Use ChatGPT to trim and clean
→ Ask for 10 hooks and 10 CTAs
→ Generate 5 variations for A/B testing
→ Keep a swipe file of winners in Notion
Final checkpoint:
If the copy does not clearly say who it is for, what they get, and what to do next, rewrite those lines first.
