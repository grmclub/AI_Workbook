## 8 ChatGPT Prompts for Creating Comprehensive English Lesson Plans in Word Format 👇👇

1️. Create a Reading Lesson Plan
Prompt:
"Create a comprehensive English reading lesson plan for [age/grade] learners at [CEFR level] on [topic/text]. Use the PWP framework: Pre Reading, While Reading, and Post Reading. Include clear learning objectives, required materials, stage by stage procedures, timing, interaction patterns, teacher instructions, differentiation, anticipated problems and solutions, and assessment. In the Pre Reading stage, activate prior knowledge, introduce the context, and prepare learners for the text. In the While Reading stage, progress from understanding the main idea to identifying key details and deeper comprehension where appropriate. In the Post Reading stage, extend the text through meaningful speaking, writing, or language work. Ensure all activities are scaffolded, engaging, and appropriate to the learners’ CEFR level. Present the complete lesson plan in a professionally structured, editable Word document, using clear headings and tables where appropriate."

5️. Create a Speaking Lesson Plan
Prompt:
"Create a comprehensive English speaking lesson plan for [age/grade] learners at [CEFR level] on [topic/communicative goal]. Use the TBL framework: Pre Task, Task Cycle, and Language Focus. Include clear learning objectives, materials, stage by stage procedures, timing, interaction patterns, teacher instructions, differentiation, anticipated problems and solutions, and assessment. Prepare learners for the speaking task with useful language and context, then engage them in a meaningful pair or group task that requires genuine communication. Ensure activities maximize learner speaking time and are appropriate to the learners’ CEFR level. Present the complete lesson plan in a professionally structured, editable Word document, using clear headings and tables where appropriate."

8️. Create a Diagnostic and Responsive Lesson Plan
Prompt:
"Create a comprehensive English lesson plan for [age/grade] learners at [CEFR level] to diagnose and address their understanding of [target language/skill]. Use the TTT framework: Test, Teach, Test. Begin with a short diagnostic task to identify what learners can already do and where they need support. Use the results to provide focused teaching and practice, then repeat a similar communicative task so learners can demonstrate improvement. Include clear learning objectives, materials, stage by stage procedures, timing, interaction patterns, teacher instructions, differentiation, anticipated problems and solutions, and assessment. Ensure the teaching stage responds directly to the needs revealed by the initial task and that all activities are appropriate to the learners’ CEFR level. Present the complete lesson plan in a professionally structured, editable Word document, using clear headings and tables where appropriate."

7️. Create an Integrated Skills Lesson Plan
Prompt:
"Create a comprehensive English lesson plan for [age/grade] learners at [CEFR level] on [topic/theme], integrating listening, speaking, reading, and writing where appropriate. Use the ESA framework: Engage, Study, and Activate. Include clear learning objectives, materials, stage by stage procedures, timing, interaction patterns, teacher instructions, differentiation, anticipated problems and solutions, and assessment. Begin with an engaging activity that activates learners’ interest and prior knowledge, provide focused work on relevant language and skills, and then move to a meaningful communicative task where learners apply what they have learned. Ensure the skills are logically connected rather than taught as separate activities, and adapt all tasks to the learners’ CEFR level. Present the complete lesson plan in a professionally structured, editable Word document, using clear headings and tables where appropriate."
