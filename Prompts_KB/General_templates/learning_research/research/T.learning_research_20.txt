##Found a great paper? Don’t just save it.
Use it to find 10–20 more papers you probably wouldn’t have discovered through a normal keyword search. 👇

📌 Try this citation chaining workflow:
1. Start with one strong paper.

2. Check its references.
These are the studies that came before it.

3. Check “cited by.”
Find newer research that built on the paper.

4. Look for related papers.
Find studies with similar methods, populations, or research questions.

5. Follow the key authors.
Researchers publishing repeatedly in the same niche can lead you to important work.

6. Repeat with your strongest new papers.
You can also create three buckets:
📌 Foundational papers
📌 Recent developments
📌 Contradictory/alternative findings
This is often much more powerful than repeatedly searching the same keywords.
