## 7 powerful prompts to start learning smarter: 👇
Your brain gets sharper when you train it daily. 🧠⚡

1. Learn ANY Topic From Scratch
Stop guessing what to learn next. Let AI build your learning path.
Prompt:
"I want to master [TOPIC] from beginner to advanced. Build a step-by-step roadmap with clear milestones, daily practice, and the best free resources. Tell me what to learn first—and why."

2. Turn AI Into Your Personal Tutor
Don’t just ask AI for answers. Make it teach you.
Prompt:
"Act as my personal tutor for [TOPIC]. Teach me one lesson at a time using simple explanations, real examples, and quick quizzes. Only move on when I’ve mastered the current lesson."

3. Practice Until It Sticks
Learning happens when you practice—not just when you read.
Prompt:
"Give me 10 practice exercises for [TOPIC], starting easy and getting harder. After each answer, explain what I got right, what I got wrong, and how to improve."

4. Test Yourself Like an Exam
The best way to measure learning? Test yourself.
Prompt:
"Create a quiz on [TOPIC] with multiple-choice, true/false, and real-world questions. Hide the answers until I finish, then explain every answer."

5. Learn From Your Mistakes
Mistakes aren’t setbacks—they’re your fastest path to improvement.
Prompt:
"Review my work like a teacher. Don’t fix it for me. Identify my mistakes, explain why they’re wrong, and guide me toward the correct answer."

6. Build a Daily Learning Habit
15 minutes a day beats hours of inconsistent studying.
Prompt:
"Create a 15-minute daily learning plan for [TOPIC] for the next 30 days. Make each lesson build on the previous one and include one practical exercise."

7. Master Any Skill Faster
Don’t just memorize. Understand it. Apply it. Teach it.
Prompt:
"Help me master [TOPIC] using the Feynman Technique. Teach me the concept, have me explain it in my own words, identify gaps, and repeat until I can explain it simply."
