5 prompts to make it your personal professor

1. THE PROFESSOR PROMPT
Turns your source into a full lecture from scratch.
Prompt: 'You are my professor teaching [SUBJECT/TOPIC]. Using only the sources I've uploaded, teach me this material as if this is our first class. Structure your lecture in 3 parts:
1. Foundation: the 5 core ideas I MUST understand
2. Deep dive: examples, analogies, and how these ideas connect
3. Check: 5 questions to test if I actually learned it
Explain like I'm smart but new to this topic. Cite the source page or section for every claim.'

2. THE STUDY GUIDE GENERATOR
Compresses everything into a study sheet you'd actually use.
Prompt: 'Build me a complete study guide from these sources.
Include:
- Key concepts (with 1-line definitions)
- Formulas / frameworks / models
- 5 examples I can memorize
- 10 flashcard-style Q&A pairs for spaced repetition
- 3 common mistakes students make on this topic
Format it so I can print it and study from one page.'

3. THE EXAM SIMULATOR
Turns your notes into a full practice exam that grades you.
Prompt: 'Using only my uploaded sources, create a 15-question test that covers the most important material:
- 8 multiple choice questions
- 5 short-answer questions
- 2 long-form questions
After I answer, grade each response, explain what I got wrong, and rank my weakest areas so I know where to focus.'

4. THE FEYNMAN EXPLAINER
Explains a hard concept 3 different ways so it actually sticks.
Prompt: 'Take [SPECIFIC CONCEPT] from my sources and explain it three times:
1. Like I'm 12 years old (use everyday analogies)
2. Like I'm a college student in this field
3. Like I'm about to teach this concept to someone else
Then tell me: which explanation matches how deeply I actually understand it?'

5. THE REAL WORLD BRIDGE
Connects the theory in your notes to actual application.
Prompt: 'Give me 5 real-world scenarios where I would use [CONCEPT/TOPIC] from these sources.
For each one:
- Describe the scenario in 2 sentences
- Show me exactly how the concept applies
- Walk me through the step-by-step reasoning
- End with the practical decision or outcome
Make me feel like I could use this tomorrow.'
