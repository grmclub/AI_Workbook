## 🚨 You can turn your documents, research, and notes into educational videos with NotebookLM.
Here’s a practical workflow for creating short video explainers from your own sources: 🧵👇

1. Add your sources
Create a new notebook in NotebookLM and add the material you want to use:
• PDFs, ebooks, or research papers.
• Notes, scripts, or text files.
• Web sources using NotebookLM’s search feature when you don’t have a document ready.

2. Set the language first
Open the settings and choose your preferred output language before generating the video.
This helps ensure the narration and generated content match the language you want.

3. Pick your video format
Open Video Overview and choose the format that fits your content.
• Short Video — ideal for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.
• Cinematic Explainer — better suited for longer, landscape-style educational content.

4. Use these custom prompt templates
Choose Custom Topic instead of relying entirely on the default options. Give NotebookLM a clear idea of what you want emphasized.
▪︎ Educational explainer:
Prompt > "Explain the main ideas from this source in a fast, engaging video. Use simple visual animations and motion graphics to make [TOPIC] easy to understand. Prioritize clarity and visually simplify complex information.”
▪︎ Story-driven:
Prompt > “Turn this source into a compelling narrative about [TOPIC]. Start with a strong hook, build curiosity throughout the video, and use cinematic visuals to highlight the most important discoveries and conclusions.”
▪︎ Step-by-step tutorial:
Prompt > "Create a practical instructional video based on this source. Present [PROCESS] as clear visual steps and focus on helping the viewer achieve [OUTCOME]. Keep each stage simple and easy to follow.”

5. Review before publishing
Always preview the generated video.
AI-generated visuals can sometimes misrepresent diagrams, data, or technical details, so verify the important sections before publishing.
For content in languages other than English, also check the captions carefully. If they need correction, you can add accurate subtitles afterward using a video editor.
