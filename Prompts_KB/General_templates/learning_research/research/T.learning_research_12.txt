## 🚨 You can turn your documents, research, and notes into educational videos with NotebookLM.
Here’s a practical workflow for creating short video explainers from your own sources: 🧵👇

1. Add your sources
Create a new notebook in NotebookLM and add the material you want to use:
• PDFs, ebooks, or research papers.
• Notes, scripts, or text files.
• Web sources using NotebookLM’s search feature when you don’t have a document ready.

2. Set the language first
Open the settings and choose your preferred output language before generating the video.
This helps ensure the narration and generated content match the language you want.

3. Pick your video format
Open Video Overview and choose the format that fits your content.
• Short Video — ideal for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.
• Cinematic Explainer — better suited for longer, landscape-style educational content.

4. Use these custom prompt templates
Choose Custom Topic instead of relying entirely on the default options. Give NotebookLM a clear idea of what you want emphasized.
▪︎ Educational explainer:
Prompt > "Explain the main ideas from this source in a fast, engaging video. Use simple visual animations and motion graphics to make [TOPIC] easy to understand. Prioritize clarity and visually simplify complex information."
▪︎ Story-driven:
Prompt > "Turn this source into a compelling narrative about [TOPIC]. Start with a strong hook, build curiosity throughout the video, and use cinematic visuals to highlight the most important discoveries and conclusions."
▪︎ Step-by-step tutorial:
Prompt > "Create a practical instructional video based on this source. Present [PROCESS] as clear visual steps and focus on helping the viewer achieve [OUTCOME]. Keep each stage simple and easy to follow."

5. Review before publishing
Always preview the generated video.
AI-generated visuals can sometimes misrepresent diagrams, data, or technical details, so verify the important sections before publishing.
For content in languages other than English, also check the captions carefully. If they need correction, you can add accurate subtitles afterward using a video editor.

## The most useful AI skill is building AI workflows that research, analyze, create, verify, and improve. 8 prompts to practice it 👇

1. TURN ONE GOAL INTO AN AI WORKFLOW
Prompt:
"Act as my AI Systems Architect.
My goal: [GOAL]
Current situation: [CURRENT STATE]
Desired result: [RESULT]
Tools available: [TOOLS]
Resources: [RESOURCES]
Obstacles: [OBSTACLES]
Timeframe: [TIME]
Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]
Success metrics: [METRICS]
Design an AI-assisted workflow from input to final result.
Show what should be researched, analyzed, created, verified, executed, monitored, and improved.
Explain each stage and remove unnecessary steps."


2. FIND WHAT AI CAN AUTOMATE
Prompt:
"Audit this workflow: [WORKFLOW].
My role: [ROLE]
Frequency: [FREQUENCY]
Time spent: [TIME]
Current tools: [TOOLS]
Recurring problems: [PROBLEMS]
Quality requirements: [STANDARD]
Human-only tasks: [TASKS]
Classify each step as:
• Automate
• AI-assisted
• Delegate
• Keep human-controlled
Rank the opportunities by potential time saved, difficulty, reliability, risk, and impact.
Then design the highest-value automation."


3. BUILD AN AI RESEARCH WORKFLOW
Prompt:
"Design a research workflow for [GOAL].
Topic: [TOPIC]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Region: [REGION]
Preferred sources: [SOURCES]
Freshness: [TIMEFRAME]
Depth: [DEPTH]
Final output: [FORMAT]
Create a process that:
• Identifies key questions
• Searches relevant sources
• Compares evidence
• Finds contradictions
• Separates facts from assumptions
• Flags uncertainty
• Produces actionable conclusions
Add verification checkpoints before the final answer."


4. BUILD A SMARTER CONTENT WORKFLOW
Prompt:
"Build an AI-assisted content workflow for [GOAL].
Niche: [NICHE]
Audience: [AUDIENCE]
Platform: [PLATFORM]
Voice: [VOICE]
Expertise: [EXPERTISE]
Formats: [FORMATS]
Frequency: [FREQUENCY]
Examples I like: [EXAMPLES]
Create a process to research ideas, find original angles, draft content, verify important claims, improve hooks, adapt it to the platform, and review performance data.
Prioritize useful, original content over generic viral formulas."


5. GIVE AI A DECISION FRAMEWORK
Prompt:
"Help me evaluate this decision: [DECISION].
Objective: [OBJECTIVE]
Options: [OPTIONS]
Priorities: [PRIORITIES]
Evidence: [EVIDENCE]
Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]
Risk tolerance: [RISK LEVEL]
Deadline: [DEADLINE]
Identify missing information, compare the options, quantify trade-offs where possible, and stress-test the strongest choices.
Clearly separate:
• Facts
• Assumptions
• Predictions
• Recommendations
Explain the reasoning behind the final recommendation."


6. MAKE AI CHECK ITS WORK
Prompt:
"Create a quality-control process for [WORKFLOW].
Expected output: [OUTPUT]
Quality standard: [STANDARD]
Common failures: [FAILURES]
Critical information: [DATA]
Unacceptable errors: [ERRORS]
Human approval points: [APPROVALS]
Before delivery, check:
• Accuracy
• Completeness
• Consistency
• Relevance
• Source quality
• Formatting
• Compliance
If a check fails, identify the problem, revise the output, and verify it again."


7. DESIGN A MULTI-AGENT WORKFLOW
Prompt:
"Design a multi-agent workflow for [GOAL].
Desired result: [OUTCOME]
Available tools: [TOOLS]
Data sources: [SOURCES]
Constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]
Quality standard: [STANDARD]
Create only the specialized roles needed for research, analysis, writing, coding, verification, or execution.
For each agent, define:
• Input
• Task
• Output
• Tools
• Decision authority
• Human approval
Explain how agents exchange information, resolve conflicts, and verify the final result.
Minimize unnecessary handoffs."


8. BUILD A CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT LOOP
Prompt:
"Create an improvement loop for [AI WORKFLOW].
Current performance: [CURRENT]
Target: [TARGET]
Metrics: [METRICS]
Feedback data: [FEEDBACK]
Common failures: [FAILURES]
Review frequency: [FREQUENCY]
Design a process that:
• Records outcomes
• Analyzes failures
• Finds patterns
• Tests changes
• Compares results
• Keeps changes only when evidence supports them
Include safeguards against degradation, unnecessary complexity, overfitting, and silent failures."

## Elevate Skills

1. THE KNOWLEDGE GAP FINDER
"Ask me 15 questions about [subject] without giving me the answers. Analyze my responses and identify the exact concepts I misunderstand or haven't mastered."

2. THE FEYNMAN TEST
"Ask me to explain [concept] as if I'm teaching it to a 12-year-old. Identify every vague or incorrect part of my explanation, then help me rebuild it clearly."

3. THE EXPERT VS BEGINNER
"Show me how a beginner, intermediate learner, and expert would approach [problem]. Explain the differences in their reasoning and decision-making."

4. THE PRESSURE TEST
"Give me increasingly difficult real-world problems involving [skill]. Don't give hints unless I ask. After each attempt, evaluate my reasoning and raise the difficulty."

5. THE COUNTEREXAMPLE MACHINE
"Challenge my understanding of [idea]. Give me edge cases, counterexamples, and scenarios where my current assumptions could fail."

6. THE FEEDBACK LOOP
"Review my work: [paste work]. Score it from 1–100 across accuracy, clarity, depth, originality, and execution. Give me the three improvements that would make the biggest difference."

7. THE MASTERY CHECKPOINT
"Based on everything I've learned about [subject], create a final mastery test combining recall, application, problem-solving, and explanation. Afterward, create my next-level learning plan."

Pro Tip:
The goal isn't to make AI think for you.
It's to make AI challenge your thinking.
Learn.
Test.
Fail.
Correct.
Repeat.
That's how skills compound.
