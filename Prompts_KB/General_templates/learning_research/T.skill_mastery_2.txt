##7 Claude Prompts to Learn Anything 10x Faster

1. The Feynman Technique
​Use this when a concept feels too academic, abstract, or overwhelming. It forces the AI to strip away the jargon.
​The Prompt: > "Teach me [insert topic] in simple language, as if you are explaining it to a beginner. Break it into clear, easy-to-grasp steps and provide simple examples. Afterward, ask me to explain it back to you in my own words. Evaluate my response to identify any gaps, misconceptions, or missing pieces in my understanding."

2. First Principles Breakdown
​Use this when you need to understand the underlying logic of a subject, rather than just memorizing surface-level facts.
​The Prompt: > "Act as an expert instructor. Break down [insert topic] into its most fundamental building blocks using first principles thinking. Challenge standard assumptions, explain the ‘why’ behind each concept, and rebuild the idea from the ground up so I understand the core mechanics."

3. The Active Recall Coach
​Reading creates the illusion of competence. Recalling information builds actual memory. Use this to test yourself.
​The Prompt: > "After teaching me the basics of [insert topic], quiz me with progressively challenging questions that force me to actively recall information without looking at my notes. Mix conceptual, applied, and scenario-based questions. Evaluate my answers, clearly explain any corrections, and reinforce my weak areas with targeted follow-up questions."

​4. Spaced Repetition System
​Use this to ensure you do not forget what you learned today by the time next week rolls around.
​The Prompt: > "Create a spaced repetition revision schedule for mastering [insert topic]. Outline exactly what I need to review daily, weekly, and monthly based on memory retention principles. Specify review activities—like recall, practice, and testing—and tell me how to adjust the intervals based on my performance to move this information into my long-term memory."

5. Smart Knowledge Summary
​Use this when you need a cheat sheet or a high-level overview of a broad topic.
​The Prompt: > "Summarize [insert topic] using clear frameworks, mental models, and simplified structures. Highlight the key principles, patterns, and relationships within the subject. Present this information in a highly structured way that makes it easy to remember and apply to real-world situations."

6. Learning Acceleration Roadmap
​Never wonder what you should study next. Use this to create a personalized syllabus.
​The Prompt: > "Design a structured, end-to-end learning roadmap for mastering [insert skill]. Break the journey down into progressive stages, milestones, and checkpoints. Specify the key resources to study and practice tasks for each stage, and define measurable criteria so I can efficiently track my progress and always know exactly what to learn next."

7. Targeted Weakness Assessment
​Use this to find out what you do not know before it costs you in an exam or a real-world scenario.
​The Prompt: > "Ask me a series of diagnostic questions about [insert topic] to identify my weakest areas, blind spots, and misunderstandings. Adapt the difficulty of the questions based on my answers. Once we finish, explain where I went wrong and recommend specific exercises or focus areas to strengthen my overall comprehension."

9 prompts that turn hours of work into minutes

1 | Core Meaning Extractor (Deep Understanding Mode)
Best for: textbooks, PDFs, lecture notes, and dense research materials.
Prompt:
"Analyze all uploaded materials and extract the true meaning behind the content. Generate 5 essential, high-signal questions that go beyond surface recall and test real understanding of the material. Focus on core concepts and precise definitions, ideas that are repeated or emphasized, and how different concepts connect through relationships like cause and effect, hierarchy, or dependencies. Also include real-world applications or implications mentioned in the content. For each question, briefly explain why it matters and what deeper understanding it reveals."

2 | Structural Thinking Builder (Beyond Surface Summaries)
Use when summaries feel shallow, fragmented, or fail to reveal how the material fits together.
Prompt:
"Review all uploaded documents and generate 5 essential, high-signal questions that, when answered, would fully explain the core ideas, structure, and purpose of the material. Focus on uncovering how the content is organized, how each section connects to the next, the underlying framework or logic driving the ideas, and the key principles that hold everything together. Ensure the questions reveal the “why” behind the material, not just the surface-level details or isolated facts."

3 | Insight Discovery Engine (What Actually Matters?)
Best for: researchers, writers, and analysts looking to uncover meaningful insights beyond surface-level information.
Prompt:
"From the uploaded sources, identify the most surprising, unusual, or high-value insights that stand out as truly important. Focus on ideas that challenge assumptions, reveal hidden patterns, or provide deeper understanding that is not immediately obvious. Include short supporting quotes where they strengthen the insight, and clearly explain why each insight is important, unexpected, or worth paying attention to, emphasizing its implications, impact, or potential use."

4 | Guided Insight Deep-Dive (For Focused Research)
Use when you need focused, distraction-free insights without information overload.
Prompt:
"I’m researching [INSERT TOPIC]. Review all uploaded materials and extract the most compelling, high-signal insights that are directly relevant to this topic. Focus specifically on [INSERT SPECIFIC ANGLE] and deliberately filter out anything that is unrelated or outside the scope of this focus, especially [WHAT TO EXCLUDE]. Prioritize depth, clarity, and meaningful connections, and include direct quotes where they add credibility, precision, or stronger evidence to the insights."

5 | Interactive Quiz Generator (Memory That Sticks)
Best for: classrooms, self-study, revision, and active recall practice.
Prompt:
"Create an interactive quiz hosted by two speakers discussing [INSERT TOPIC]. Include 10 questions in total, mixing multiple-choice and true/false formats to test both recall and understanding. Let one speaker occasionally give incorrect answers to simulate common misconceptions, while the other corrects them clearly and explains the reasoning behind the correct answer. Keep the dialogue engaging and conversational, and end with a short performance summary that highlights strengths, weaknesses, and areas that need improvement."

6 | Multilingual Podcast Creator
Best when turning documents into globally accessible, audio-style content for different audiences.
Prompt:
"Create a full discussion or podcast episode based on the uploaded materials entirely in [INSERT LANGUAGE]. Ensure the entire response is written in [LANGUAGE], avoiding English unless a technical term cannot be translated. Maintain a natural, conversational tone that feels like a real discussion between speakers, with clear flow, engaging explanations, and smooth transitions between ideas while preserving the original meaning and depth of the content."

7 | Product Manager Lens (Decision-Ready Insights)
Best for: turning dense documents into clear, actionable decisions and strategic direction.
Prompt:
"Act as a senior product manager reviewing internal documentation and synthesize the key insights into a decision-ready memo. Ignore filler and surface-level explanations, and focus only on what drives decisions. Include user evidence by extracting direct quotes that highlight real problems or needs, add feasibility notes outlining technical, operational, or resource constraints, and identify blind spots where information is missing, unclear, or contradictory. Present everything in clear, concise bullet points, and ask for clarification if any part of the request is too vague or incomplete to produce a meaningful output."

8 | Scientific Analysis Mode (Method Over Opinion)
Best for: academic work, technical research, and evidence-based evaluation.
Prompt:
"Act as a research assistant to a senior scientist in [INSERT FIELD], assuming advanced domain knowledge and avoiding explanations of basic terminology. Focus strictly on analyzing the material through a scientific lens by evaluating the research methodology, assessing data quality and integrity, and identifying any contradictions, gaps, or weak evidence within the content. Present your analysis in clearly structured sections using bold headings for Key Findings, Method Strengths & Weaknesses, and Conflicting Evidence, ensuring the output is precise, critical, and grounded in evidence rather than opinion."

9 | High-School Translator Mode (Clarity for Any Audience)
Best for: simplifying complex content for beginners, students, or non-experts.
Prompt:
"Act as an engaging high-school teacher and rewrite the content so that a 12-year-old can easily understand it without losing the original meaning. For each section, include a clear TL;DR in one simple sentence, a relatable analogy using real-world comparisons to make the idea easier to grasp, and a vocabulary breakdown where 3 difficult words are explained in plain, simple language. For the more complex or dense parts, convert the key ideas into true/false questions to reinforce understanding and make the content easier to learn and retain."

1. ​Structured Curriculum Designer
​
Prompt:
“Act as an elite instructional designer. Review my uploaded documents and build an 8-week curriculum to master this subject. For each week, outline:
→ The core concept to master.
→ Which specific uploaded sources to read.
→ A daily practice exercise based on the text.
→ A weekly milestone project.”

2. Strict Questioning Tutor
​
Prompt:
“Act as a strict tutor for the material in these uploads. Do not give me direct answers. Instead, ask me one thought-provoking question at a time to test my comprehension of the sources. Wait for my response. If my logic contradicts the documents, point out the citation I missed and ask another question to guide me.”

3. Core Truths Builder
​Prompt:
“Synthesize all my uploaded materials. Strip away the complex terms. Break the arguments down into their fundamental, undeniable truths. Once we have those basics, show me step-by-step how the authors build their complex theories back up from them.”

4. Feynman Simplifier
​
Prompt:
“Explain the hardest concepts in these sources using the Feynman Technique.
→ Step 1: Explain it to a 12-year-old using an everyday analogy.
→ Step 2: Introduce the technical terms from the text, linking them back to the analogy.
→ Step 3: Ask me to explain it back to you, and score my accuracy based strictly on the source material.”

5. Expert Mindset Decoder
​
Prompt:
“Scan the uploaded case studies and books. Identify the top 3 decision-making frameworks used by the experts mentioned. For each framework, provide:
→ A brief definition.
→ A scenario where a beginner fails by ignoring this mindset.
→ How the expert succeeds in that exact scenario using the text's principles.”

6. Skill Transfer Bridge
​
Prompt:
“I understand [Concept A from Upload 1]. I am trying to grasp [Concept B from Upload 2]. Act as a learning strategist and map the overlapping concepts between these specific documents. Explain the mechanics of [Concept B] using direct metaphors and frameworks from [Concept A] to accelerate my understanding.”

7. Real-World Simulator
​
Prompt:
“Act as a real-world simulator based entirely on the rules and data in my uploaded documents. Feed me difficult scenarios one at a time. I will tell you my action. Then:
→ Grade my decision out of 10 based on the sources.
→ Explain the consequences using evidence from the text.
→ Tell me what the authors would suggest doing differently. Give me the first scenario to begin.”

8. Ruthless Critic
​Prompt:
“I am going to explain my understanding of the uploaded research below. Act as a ruthless academic critic. Tear apart my explanation, point out edge cases from the documents I completely missed, expose my logical flaws, and challenge my assumptions. Do not hold back. My explanation: [Insert Explanation]”

9. Long-Term Memory Builder
​
Prompt:
“I need to permanently memorize the core tenets of these uploads. Generate a 30-card flashcard deck drawn directly from the text. Format it as a table with 'Front' and 'Back' columns for export. Focus heavily on counter-intuitive facts, common mistakes mentioned by the authors, and scenario-based application.”

10. Final Exam Grader
​
Prompt:
“Based entirely on the material I have uploaded, generate a comprehensive 3-part final exam.
→ Part 1: 3 conceptual short-answer questions.
→ Part 2: 2 troubleshooting scenarios based on the text.
→ Part 3: 1 complex synthesis question combining multiple sources.
Wait for my answers, grade me strictly using citations, and list my remaining blind spots.”

## 10 ChatGPT prompts to boost critical thinking and productivity

1. Set Clear Goals 🎯
"I want to achieve [SPECIFIC GOAL]. Help me turn this goal into a realistic step-by-step action plan. Break it down into clear milestones, daily or weekly actions, deadlines, potential obstacles, and measurable ways to track my progress. Keep the plan practical and achievable."

2. Simplify Complex Ideas 🧠
"Explain [TOPIC] in simple terms that a 12-year-old could understand. Break the concept into the most important ideas, use relatable examples and analogies, avoid unnecessary jargon, and finish with a short summary and 3 questions to test my understanding."

3. Write Better Tutorials ✍️
"Create a beginner-friendly guide to using ChatGPT. Explain the key concepts, show practical examples, share best practices, common mistakes to avoid, and provide actionable tips. Keep it clear, engaging, and easy to follow in under 500 words."

4. Generate Your Own Prompts ⚡
"You are an expert AI coach for [YOUR PROFESSION]. Identify the tasks where ChatGPT could save me the most time or improve my results. Then create 10 powerful, practical prompts I can use in my daily work. Make each prompt specific, detailed, and ready to copy and paste."

5. Identify Knowledge Gaps 🔍
"I’m studying [SUBJECT]. Act as my personal tutor and assess my current knowledge. Ask me questions one at a time, starting with basic concepts and gradually increasing the difficulty. After my answers, identify my knowledge gaps, explain what I misunderstood, and recommend what I should study next."

6. Master a New Skill ⚡
"I want to learn [DESIRED SKILL] from beginner to a practical level in 30 days. Create a structured daily learning plan with lessons, exercises, practice tasks, useful resources, and weekly milestones. Focus on hands-on learning and include a way to measure my progress each week."

7. Become an Expert Researcher 📚
"I’m creating a report about [TOPIC]. Help me research this subject thoroughly and organize the information into a clear, logical structure that non-experts can understand. Identify the most important questions, key concepts, opposing viewpoints, supporting evidence, and areas that require further research."

8. Make Better Decisions 💡
"I need to make a decision about [DECISION]. Act as an objective decision-making advisor. Ask me the most important questions first, then analyze my options based on benefits, risks, costs, long-term consequences, and opportunity costs. Give me a clear comparison and recommend the strongest option with your reasoning."

9. Think Like an Expert 🧩
"I want to understand [TOPIC] at an expert level. Teach me how experts think about this subject, including the fundamental principles, mental models, common misconceptions, and important patterns. Start with the basics, then gradually increase the complexity and give me real-world examples and exercises."

10. Solve Problems Like an Expert 🔥
"I’m facing this problem: [DESCRIBE PROBLEM]. Analyze it step by step and identify the root cause rather than just the symptoms. Give me several possible solutions, explain the pros and cons of each, rank them by effectiveness and practicality, and create a clear action plan for implementing the best solution."
