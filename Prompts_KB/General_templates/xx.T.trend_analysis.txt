1/ Build a 5-Minute AI Trend Alert System
Most founders and professionals consume huge amounts of AI content every week.
The problem is not access to information.
The problem is retention.
You scroll through headlines, save articles, open tabs, and then forget most of what you saw.
Miss one important update from OpenAI, Anthropic, HAI, or another major source and your strategy can quickly become outdated.
Instead of manually checking dozens of websites every day, build a simple system that automatically collects, summarizes, and delivers the most important AI updates to you.
The goal is simple:
→ Spend less time searching.
→ Spend more time learning.
→ Catch important shifts before everyone else.
-----------------------------------
2/ Collect and Prioritize High-Signal Sources
Start by creating a database in Notion.
Use it as your central hub for AI news and updates.
Add RSS feeds from trusted sources such as:
→ OpenAI
→ Anthropic
→ HAI
→ Stratechery
Next, create a Weight column using a score from 1 to 5.
This helps separate high-value sources from lower-priority ones.
For example:
→ 5 = Must-read sources.
→ 4 = Strong industry sources.
→ 3 = Useful but occasional.
→ 2 = Low-priority monitoring.
→ 1 = Rarely important.
This simple scoring system helps focus attention on information that matters most.
---------------------------------
3/ Let Zapier and Perplexity Do the Heavy Lifting
Once updates enter Notion, automate the processing step.
Use Zapier to trigger actions whenever a new article appears.
Connect Perplexity to generate:
→ A short recap.
→ Key insights.
→ Why the update matters.
Store the results alongside the original article.
A useful summary structure is:
→ Title
→ Two-sentence summary
→ Why it matters
→ Key bullet point
This turns long articles into fast, actionable insights that can be reviewed in seconds.

4/ Create a Weekly Digest and Share It
Information becomes valuable when it is easy to review.
Create a weekly digest that automatically runs every Monday at 8 AM.
The digest should include:
→ Top AI updates.
→ Key industry developments.
→ Important product launches.
→ Strategic insights.
Send the digest directly to your Slack channel such as #ai-trends.
Use Slack blocks and collapsible sections to make scanning faster.
This gives your team one place to stay informed without interrupting their workflow.
---------------------------------
5/ Improve the System Every Friday
The best monitoring systems evolve over time.
Schedule a short Friday review session.
Use it to:
→ Remove low-value sources.
→ Adjust feed weights.
→ Improve summary quality.
→ Shorten summaries that feel too long.
→ Keep only the signals that create value.
One extra tip:
Create a Notion page called "AI Trend Alerts."
Store:
→ Zapier workflow links.
→ Perplexity prompts.
→ Setup instructions.
→ System documentation.
When you need to make a change later, everything is in one place.
A well-built AI trend alert system can save hours every week while helping you stay ahead of important industry changes.

1/ The Rise Of AI Agents In Enterprise Operations
For years, businesses have used software to automate repetitive tasks.
AI agents take this a step further.
Instead of following only fixed rules, AI agents can:
→ Understand instructions
→ Analyze information
→ Make recommendations
→ Execute tasks across tools
→ Learn from context
This shift is moving AI from a simple productivity tool to an active participant in business operations.
As a result, organizations are beginning to deploy AI agents in real-world workflows where speed, consistency, and scalability matter.
----------------------------
2/ How Companies Are Using AI Agents Today
Many enterprise teams are already integrating AI agents into daily operations.
Common use cases include:
→ Automating routine workflows
→ Managing customer inquiries
→ Summarizing meetings and reports
→ Conducting research and analysis
→ Monitoring business metrics
→ Supporting internal knowledge retrieval
Instead of employees spending hours gathering information or performing repetitive actions, AI agents can handle much of the groundwork.
This allows teams to focus on higher-value activities that require judgment, creativity, and strategic thinking.

## AI agents for DSS

3/ AI Agents Are Changing Decision Support
One of the most valuable capabilities of AI agents is their ability to process large amounts of information quickly.
They can:
→ Review documents
→ Analyze trends
→ Compare data sources
→ Generate insights
→ Surface potential risks
This doesn't mean AI replaces decision-makers.
Rather, it provides leaders with faster access to relevant information, helping them make more informed decisions.
The goal is not to remove humans from the process.
The goal is to help humans make better decisions with less effort.
