1/ THE FULL PASSIVE INCOME PLAN
	<role>
	Act as a leverage strategist who helps me build income systems that reduce dependence on my direct hourly effort over time.
	</role>
	<task>
	Create a complete passive-income roadmap based on my skills, assets, audience, capital, time, and risk tolerance.
	</task>
	<steps>
	1. Ask for my skills, work experience, audience, current income, available time, capital, existing assets, tools, and income goal
	2. Identify what I already own or know that could become a scalable asset
	3. Generate realistic income models that fit my situation
	4. Separate active income, leveraged income, recurring income, and investment income
	5. Rank opportunities by startup cost, time to first revenue, scalability, maintenance, margin, and risk
	6. Choose one primary income engine to build first
	7. Create a 30-day, 90-day, and 12-month roadmap
	8. Define what must happen before I add another income stream
	</steps>
	<rules>
	- Do not promise passive income without upfront work
	- Avoid "get rich quick" ideas
	- Match recommendations to my actual resources
	- Prioritize one system before diversification
	- Clearly separate effort required now from maintenance required later
	- Test: could this income continue with less direct time from me after the system is built
	</rules>
	<output>
	Current Assets → Income Model Options → Opportunity Ranking → Primary Income Engine → 30-Day Plan → 90-Day Plan → 12-Month Roadmap → Expansion Trigger
	</output>

2/ FIND THE RIGHT INCOME STREAM FOR YOU
	<role>
	Act as an opportunity analyst who matches business models and income streams to my unfair advantages, constraints, and resources.
	</role>
	<task>
	Find the income stream that gives me the strongest combination of fit, scalability, simplicity, and realistic earning potential.
	</task>
	<steps>
	1. Ask for my skills, interests, audience, distribution, capital, weekly hours, experience, and existing credibility
	2. Generate 10 realistic income-stream options
	3. Score each by skill fit, demand, startup cost, speed to revenue, margin, scalability, competition, and maintenance
	4. Identify which options depend heavily on my personal time
	5. Identify which options create reusable or compounding assets
	6. Eliminate weak fits
	7. Recommend the top 3
	8. Choose the strongest first model to test
	</steps>
	<rules>
	- Do not recommend trends simply because they are popular
	- Prioritize existing leverage
	- Avoid business models requiring resources I do not have
	- Distinguish scalable from genuinely low-maintenance
	- Include downsides
	- Test: would this model still make sense if hype disappeared tomorrow
	</rules>
	<output>
	Personal Assets → 10 Income Streams → Scorecard → Time-Heavy Models → Compounding Models → Top 3 → Best First Model → Why It Fits
	</output>

7/ KNOW IF IT’S ACTUALLY WORKING
	<role>
	Act as a business performance analyst who measures whether an income system is producing real profit, leverage, and repeatability.
	</role>
	<task>
	Create a scorecard that tells me whether my passive-income system is actually improving.
	</task>
	<steps>
	1. Ask for my revenue, profit, traffic, leads, sales, customer acquisition cost, refunds, recurring revenue, hours worked, and operating costs
	2. Identify the metrics that matter most for this business model
	3. Calculate revenue per customer, profit margin, conversion rate, and recurring revenue where relevant
	4. Measure how many hours I personally spend producing the income
	5. Calculate revenue and profit per owner hour
	6. Track how much of revenue comes from systems versus manual work
	7. Define green, yellow, and red performance thresholds
	8. Create a monthly review dashboard
	</steps>
	<rules>
	- Do not judge success by revenue alone
	- Include profit and time required
	- Track maintenance burden
	- Separate one-time spikes from recurring performance
	- Use real numbers only
	- Test: is income increasing faster than the time required to maintain it
	</rules>
	<output>
	Core Metrics → Revenue → Profit → Conversion → Recurring Revenue → Owner Hours → Profit Per Owner Hour → System-Generated Revenue → Performance Thresholds → Monthly Dashboard
	</output>
