7 ChatGPT prompts for creating professional presentations in 2 minutes
Titles are generated by AI from Meta
🚨 STOP TELLING CHATGPT "MAKE ME A PRESENTATION."
That’s the fastest way to get a boring, generic deck.
Use these 7 prompts instead to create:
→ Professional slide structures
→ Better visuals
→ Stronger storytelling
→ Presentation-ready content

1. Complete Presentation Builder
"Act as an expert presentation strategist. Create a complete slide-by-slide presentation about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Give every slide a clear purpose, compelling headline, concise key points, relevant examples, visual ideas, and a logical narrative. Start with a strong hook, build the argument progressively, and finish with a memorable conclusion and clear call to action."

2. Research-Driven Presentation
"Create a research-backed presentation about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Organize the information into a logical slide sequence and support important claims with current statistics, credible sources, relevant case studies, and practical examples. Keep each slide focused and easy to scan, and include a source/reference section at the end."

3. Beginner-Friendly Presentation
"Create a beginner-friendly presentation explaining [TOPIC] to someone with no prior knowledge. Start with the basics, then gradually introduce more advanced ideas using simple language, relatable analogies, examples, and step-by-step explanations. Avoid unnecessary jargon and make each slide answer one clear question."

4. Investor Pitch Deck
"Build an investor-ready pitch deck for [BUSINESS/IDEA]. Structure it around the problem, target customer, solution, product, market opportunity, business model, traction, competitive landscape, advantage, go-to-market strategy, financial outlook, team, funding request, and closing. Make the story persuasive without exaggerating claims, and suggest what evidence each slide should show."


5. Story-Driven Presentation
"Turn [TOPIC] into a compelling story-driven presentation using this flow: Hook → Problem → Tension → Discovery → Insight → Transformation → Action. Give every slide a strong headline and minimal supporting text. Include examples, emotional moments, transitions, and speaker notes so the presentation feels like a story rather than a collection of facts."

6. Executive/Corporate Presentation
"Create an executive-level presentation about [TOPIC] for [DECISION-MAKERS]. Use a concise, professional structure covering the current situation, key findings, opportunities, risks, trends, financial or operational impact, recommendations, and next steps. Suggest appropriate charts or tables where they add clarity, and make every slide decision-oriented."

7. Presentation + Speaker Script
"Create a complete presentation about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE], then write a matching speaker script for every slide. Keep the on-slide content concise while using the script for context, explanations, examples, and storytelling. Add delivery cues for pauses, emphasis, transitions, and audience engagement. Make the spoken narrative naturally complement—not repeat—the slide content."
