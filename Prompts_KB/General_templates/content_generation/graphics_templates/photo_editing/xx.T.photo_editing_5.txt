##Chatgpt image prompts

1. The Style Stacker:
"in the style of [Pixar + Studio Ghibli + ultra realistic], 8K, cinematic lighting, highly detailed"
Stacking 2-3 styles makes it look 10x more expensive.

2. The Camera Command:
"Shot on Sony A7R IV, 85mm lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field, bokeh background"
This is how you get that professional DSLR look.

3. The No-AI Look Command:
"-- No cartoon, no plastic skin, no fake smile, no extra fingers, keep it natural and human"
This removes the AI weirdness instantly.

4. The Lighting Hack:
"Golden hour lighting, soft shadows, volumetric light, ultra photorealistic"
Lighting = 80% of a good image. This command alone fixes bad images.

5. The Aspect Ratio Trick:
"Vertical portrait 9:16, full body, centered subject, blank space at top for text"
Perfect for Reels covers, Pinterest, and thumbnails.

6. The Consistency Command:
"Same character, same face, same clothes, same style across all images -- seed 4242"
Use this if you want the same person in 30 different images.

7. The Viral Poster Command:
"Minimalist poster style, bold typography, negative space, award-winning design, trending on Behance"
Makes any quote or idea look like a $500 design.
Copy one of these and paste it at the END of your normal prompt.
