🚨 CLAUDE CAN DESIGN 30 DAYS OF SOCIAL MEDIA GRAPHICS IN MINUTES.
These 8 prompts create scroll-stopping designs that look like a $/3,000/month designer made them. (Sav3 th!s.) 👇

Start with the grid concept. Everything follows it.
1/ The Feed Aesthetic System
"Act as a social media art director. Build a complete visual system for [brand]'s Instagram feed. Analyze my brand colors, my content mix, and the mood I want visitors to feel scrolling my grid. Design a color rule, a font pairing, a photo treatment style, and a template structure for quote posts, ready to reuse every week."

2/ The Quote Graphic Template
"Design a text layout for a quote graphic featuring this line: [paste quote]. Analyze the mood of the quote, my brand fonts, and how much white space keeps it readable at thumbnail size. Design the headline placement, the supporting attribution line, and a background treatment that matches my existing feed."

3/ The Carousel Cover Designer
"Design the cover slide layout for a carousel about [topic]. Analyze what makes someone stop and tap through versus scroll past, my brand style, and the single word or number that should dominate the slide. Design the headline size, the supporting subhead, and where my logo mark sits without crowding it."

4/ The Story Highlight Covers
"Design five Instagram story highlight cover concepts for categories like [about, offers, reviews]. Analyze my brand colors and icon style. Design a consistent icon treatment, a background color rule, and sizing guidance so all five look like a matched set instead of five random images pulled together."

5/ The Testimonial Graphic Template
"Design a testimonial graphic template using this customer quote: [paste]. Analyze what makes a testimonial feel credible at a glance, my brand style, and where a star rating or customer name adds trust without cluttering the layout. Design the quote placement, the attribution treatment, and a subtle background pattern."

6/ The Product Showcase Layout
"Design a product showcase graphic for [product]. Analyze the single feature I want to highlight, my brand colors, and the amount of text a scroller will actually read. Design the image placement, one short headline, and a small supporting detail line, keeping the whole layout readable in under three seconds."

7/ The Countdown Announcement Template
"Design a countdown-style announcement graphic for [event/launch]. Analyze the urgency I genuinely have, my brand style, and the single number or date that matters most. Design a bold number treatment, a short supporting line, and a call to action placement that works even if someone only sees it for a second."

8/ The Monthly Content Grid Planner
"Plan a nine-post grid layout for next month using these template types. Analyze visual balance across the grid, my posting frequency, and which template should appear most often based on past engagement. Design the exact order of templates across nine grid squares so the feed reads as one cohesive set. Templates: [list]."
