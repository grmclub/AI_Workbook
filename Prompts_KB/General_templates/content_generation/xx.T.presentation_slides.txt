## 🚨 Breaking: I asked Claude to fix my presentation.
It didn't just polish the slides. It made the pitch investor-ready.
Here are the 7 prompts I used:

1. Presentation Audit
Review my presentation about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Score every slide from 1–10 for clarity, storytelling, structure, design, credibility, and persuasiveness. Explain every weakness and provide specific recommendations to improve it.

2. Storyline Rebuild
Rebuild this presentation into a compelling narrative. My goal is to [DESIRED OUTCOME]. Rearrange the slides if necessary, strengthen the flow between ideas, and explain why your structure is more persuasive.

3. Slide Rewrite
Rewrite every slide using concise, high-impact language. Replace weak headlines, remove fluff, simplify complex ideas, and ensure every slide communicates one memorable takeaway. Keep the presentation under [X] minutes.

4. Visual Upgrade
Redesign every slide with a premium, modern look. Recommend the best layouts, charts, diagrams, typography, spacing, icons, and visual hierarchy. Describe exactly what each slide should look like and why.

5. Tough Question Simulator
Generate the 25 toughest questions that [AUDIENCE] is likely to ask after this presentation. Explain why each question matters and help me prepare confident, evidence-backed answers.

6. Credibility Check
Analyze every claim in this presentation. Identify weak arguments, unsupported statements, and missing proof. Recommend statistics, benchmarks, case studies, examples, or testimonials that would strengthen each point.

7. Delivery Coach
Create speaker notes for every slide. Improve my opening, transitions, pacing, emphasis, body language, and closing. Then identify likely objections from [AUDIENCE] and write persuasive responses to each.

##5 Claude prompts that will help you create your next presentation in minutes.👇👇

1. Turn Any PDF into a Presentation
Prompt:
Analyze the attached PDF or pasted document: [ATTACH PDF OR PASTE CONTENT]. Create a polished 10-slide presentation that captures the document's core message. Extract only the most important insights, remove repetition and unnecessary details, organize the content into a logical narrative, give each slide a clear title with concise bullet points, and ensure the presentation is easy to understand for someone who has never read the original document.

2. Generate a Complete Presentation Outline
Prompt:
Create a professional presentation on [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Build an 8–10 slide outline with a logical flow from introduction to conclusion. For each slide, include: a compelling title, 3–5 key talking points, the objective of the slide, speaker notes, and the most effective visual or graphic to support the content.

3. Turn Raw Data into a Storytelling Presentation
Prompt:
Analyze the following data: [PASTE DATA OR DESCRIBE SPREADSHEET]. Create a presentation that tells a compelling story using the data. Identify the most significant trends, patterns, and insights, organize them into presentation slides, recommend the most appropriate chart or graph for each insight, and write concise explanations that make the findings easy to understand and actionable.

4. Turn Meeting Notes into an Executive Recap Deck
Prompt:
Review the following meeting transcript or notes: [PASTE TRANSCRIPT OR NOTES]. Create a clean, executive-style presentation summarizing the meeting. Include the meeting objective, key discussion points, decisions made, action items with owners and deadlines (if available), unresolved questions, risks, and recommended next steps. Keep every slide concise, organized, and easy to skim.

5. Turn Numbers into Powerful Presentation Visuals
Prompt:
Review these statistics and data points: [PASTE DATA OR METRICS]. For each data point, recommend the most effective visualization (such as a bar chart, line graph, pie chart, waterfall chart, comparison table, heat map, icon grid, or infographic). Explain why that visual is the best choice, provide a clear chart title, and write a compelling one-line insight or caption that highlights the significance of the data rather than simply repeating the numbers.
