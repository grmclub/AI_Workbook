1. The Complete Presentation Architect
Prompt:
Act as an expert presentation strategist and visual storyteller. Create a [NUMBER OF SLIDES] slide presentation about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE] with the goal of [GOAL].
For every slide include:
▪︎ A compelling slide title.
▪︎ The core message.
▪︎ 3–5 concise bullet points
▪︎ Detailed speaker notes.
▪︎ Suggested visuals (charts, diagrams, icons, images, or screenshots).
▪︎ Smooth transition to the next slide.
▪︎ Audience takeaway.
Finish with a memorable conclusion, a strong call to action, and a final Q&A slide.

2. Document to Presentation Converter
Prompt:
Convert the following [DOCUMENT/REPORT/ARTICLE/NOTES] into a professional [NUMBER OF SLIDES] slide presentation for [AUDIENCE]. Organize the content into logical sections, simplify complex ideas, create compelling slide titles, recommend visuals for every slide, generate detailed speaker notes, and remove unnecessary information while preserving the most valuable insights.

3. Executive Presentation Builder
Prompt:
Create an executive presentation about [TOPIC] for [COMPANY/AUDIENCE]. Include the business problem, supporting evidence, market trends, risks, opportunities, financial impact, recommendations, implementation roadmap, expected outcomes, KPIs, and next steps. Use a professional tone suitable for executives, investors, or board members.

4. Data Storytelling Master
Prompt:
Analyze the following [DATASET/REPORT/SPREADSHEET] and transform it into a compelling presentation for [AUDIENCE]. Identify the most important insights, explain why they matter, highlight trends and anomalies, recommend the best chart for each dataset, anticipate audience questions, and conclude with actionable recommendations supported by the data.

5. Presentation Design Director
Prompt:
Redesign my presentation about [TOPIC] using modern presentation design principles. For every slide, recommend the best layout, typography, spacing, color palette, icons, images, charts, animations, and visual hierarchy. Improve readability, reduce clutter, maintain brand consistency using [BRAND COLORS/STYLE], and make the presentation look premium and presentation-ready.

6. Audience Personalization Engine
Prompt:
Rewrite my presentation about [TOPIC] specifically for [AUDIENCE]. Adjust the language, tone, technical depth, examples, terminology, objections, motivations, and call to action to match their experience level and interests. The objective is to [GOAL] while keeping the presentation engaging and persuasive.

7. Presentation Auditor & Improvement System
Prompt:
Review my presentation about [TOPIC] as an expert presentation coach. Evaluate storytelling, structure, clarity, flow, slide design, readability, audience engagement, persuasion, and visual effectiveness. Score each category out of 10, explain every weakness, identify missing sections, and rewrite the presentation with stronger messaging, smoother transitions, improved visuals, and more impactful speaker notes.

## 🚨 Breaking: I asked Claude to fix my presentation.
It didn't just polish the slides. It made the pitch investor-ready.
Here are the 7 prompts I used:

1. Presentation Audit
Review my presentation about [TOPIC] for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Score every slide from 1–10 for clarity, storytelling, structure, design, credibility, and persuasiveness. Explain every weakness and provide specific recommendations to improve it.

2. Storyline Rebuild
Rebuild this presentation into a compelling narrative. My goal is to [DESIRED OUTCOME]. Rearrange the slides if necessary, strengthen the flow between ideas, and explain why your structure is more persuasive.

3. Slide Rewrite
Rewrite every slide using concise, high-impact language. Replace weak headlines, remove fluff, simplify complex ideas, and ensure every slide communicates one memorable takeaway. Keep the presentation under [X] minutes.

4. Visual Upgrade
Redesign every slide with a premium, modern look. Recommend the best layouts, charts, diagrams, typography, spacing, icons, and visual hierarchy. Describe exactly what each slide should look like and why.

5. Tough Question Simulator
Generate the 25 toughest questions that [AUDIENCE] is likely to ask after this presentation. Explain why each question matters and help me prepare confident, evidence-backed answers.

6. Credibility Check
Analyze every claim in this presentation. Identify weak arguments, unsupported statements, and missing proof. Recommend statistics, benchmarks, case studies, examples, or testimonials that would strengthen each point.

7. Delivery Coach
Create speaker notes for every slide. Improve my opening, transitions, pacing, emphasis, body language, and closing. Then identify likely objections from [AUDIENCE] and write persuasive responses to each.
