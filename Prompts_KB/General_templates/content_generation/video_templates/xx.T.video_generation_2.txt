##You do not need a film crew to create cinematic videos.
With Seedance, one strong prompt can become a polished video with professional camera movement, atmosphere, storytelling, and visual effects.
Here are the 5 steps I use to create cinematic AI videos 👇

1/ Add Natural Breathing and Micro-Movements
Prompt:
"Use the attached image as the exact visual reference and animate the main character with subtle" realistic breathing and natural micro-movements. Avoid exaggerated gestures" facial distortion" identity drift" body warping" or sudden movement. The final result should feel like a real person standing naturally in front of a camera."
