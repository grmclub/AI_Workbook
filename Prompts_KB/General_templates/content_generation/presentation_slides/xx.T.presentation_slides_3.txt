## Delete Canva! Delete PowerPoint! ChatGPT can now turn your notes into a presentation decks. TRY 10 prompts that do it all 👇🏽

1. 📝 TURN NOTES INTO SLIDES
Turn the notes I provide into a professional [10]-slide presentation. Create a clear narrative, organize the information logically, give every slide one main idea, and keep the text concise. Include a title and conclusion slide.

2. 🧠 TURN ANY TOPIC INTO A PRESENTATION
Create a presentation about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Use [NUMBER] slides. Start with the problem or context, explain the key ideas, provide examples, and finish with practical takeaways.

3. 📄 TURN A DOCUMENT INTO A DECK
Read the attached document and convert the most important information into a presentation. Do not simply copy paragraphs. Extract the key insights, organize them into slides, and preserve important facts, numbers, and context.

4. 🎯 MAKE IT PRESENTATION-READY
Transform this content into a presentation for [AUDIENCE]. Simplify complicated explanations, remove unnecessary text, create strong slide titles, and make each slide easy to understand in under 10 seconds.

6. 📊 TURN DATA INTO A STORY
Analyze the data I provide and create a presentation that tells the story behind the numbers. Identify the most important trends, comparisons, and insights. Recommend the best chart for each important finding and clearly state the source.

7. 🎤 ADD SPEAKER NOTES
Create speaker notes for every slide. Expand on the slide content without simply reading it aloud. Make the notes conversational, informative, and easy to present.

8. 🔥 MAKE A BORING DECK BETTER
Review this presentation like an expert presentation editor. Identify confusing slides, excessive text, weak headlines, missing information, repetition, and poor flow. Then rewrite the deck with a stronger narrative while preserving the original facts.

9. 🧑🏽‍💼 ADAPT IT FOR ANY AUDIENCE
Rewrite this presentation for [AUDIENCE]. Adjust the language, examples, level of detail, tone, and slide structure so the audience can understand the message quickly. Keep the underlying facts unchanged.

10. 🚀 BUILD THE FINAL DECK
Using the source material and instructions provided, create a complete [NUMBER]-slide presentation. Include slide titles, concise body content, visuals, charts where appropriate, speaker notes, and a logical narrative. Do not invent facts. Clearly flag anything that requires verification. Review the entire deck for consistency before finalizing it.

##🚨MAKE BETTER PRESENTATIONS WITH CHATGPT
Turn ideas into polished slides in minutes. 7 prompts to try👇

1. The Full Presentation Builder
"Act as a world-class presentation creator. Create a complete, slide-by-slide presentation on the topic: [insert topic]. Include title slides, key points, examples, data, metaphors, visuals suggestions, and a closing call-to-action. Structure it like a TED-level presentation with storytelling flow."

2. The Deep Research Presentation
"Create a full presentation on [insert topic] using deep research. Include real statistics, case studies, expert quotes, practical insights, and examples. Make each slide educational, engaging, and backed by credible sources. Provide references at the end."

3. The Simplified Explanation Presentation
"Build a presentation on [insert topic] designed for beginners. Break down complex concepts into simple explanations, analogies, and step-by-step logic. Make the content so clear that even a 10-year-old can understand the full topic."

4. The Business Pitch Presentation Creator
"Create a complete investor-style pitch deck for my business idea: [insert idea]. Include problem, solution, market size, product features, business model, competitive advantage, financial projections, team slide, and closing pitch script."

5. The Storytelling Master Presentation
"Create a complete presentation on [insert topic] using storytelling structure: Hook → Conflict → Journey → Insight → Transformation → Call to Action. Make slides emotional, memorable, and persuasive. Add speaker notes with delivery guidance."

6. The Corporate Presentation
"Create a formal, corporate-style presentation on [insert topic]. Include professional tone, structured bullet points, charts, graphs, SWOT analysis, trends, forecasts, and data-backed recommendations. Make it suitable for boardrooms."

7. The Complete Presentation + Script Combo
"Create a fully ready presentation on [insert topic], AND write a complete spoken script I can read while presenting. Ensure the script matches the tone, pacing, and emotional flow of the slides. Include pauses, emphasis, storytelling cues, and delivery instructions."
