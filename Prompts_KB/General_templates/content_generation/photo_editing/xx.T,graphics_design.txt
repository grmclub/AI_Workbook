Here You Have 6 Prompts That Turn Lost Hours Into A Strategy

1. Background Removal (Clean and Commercial Cutout)
‎
‎Prompt:
‎‎"Remove the background from the attached image.
‎Preserve the original subject exactly as it was photographed, including edges, shadows, and surface texture.
‎Generate a clean transparent background, without halos, without blur, and without artificial smoothing.
‎Maintain the original resolution and proportions.
‎This image will be used for professional product listings."

2. Product Mockup Based on an Existing Photo
‎
‎Prompt:
‎‎"Using the attached product photo, place the product into a realistic studio mockup. Use a neutral setting with soft lighting, suitable for an e-commerce site. Match the light direction, shadows, and reflections to the original product photo. Do not alter the product's shape, color, or branding. The result should be indistinguishable from a professional product photoshoot."

‎3. High-End Branding Visual (Minimalist)
‎
‎Prompt:
‎‎"Create a high-end branding image featuring the attached product. Use a minimalist composition with controlled lighting and a restrained color palette. Highlight the material quality, surface details, and form. Avoid text, slogans, or decorative elements. The final image should be suitable for a luxury brand’s website or a pitch deck."

4. Color and Finish Variations Based on a Single Product
‎
‎Prompt:
‎‎"Generate multiple variations of the attached product in different colors and finishes. Keep the geometry, lighting, and perspective identical across all versions. Change only the color and surface finish. Each variation must look like a real manufactured product, not just a simple digital recolor."

‎5. Resource for Social Media without 'Ad Energy'
‎
‎Prompt:
‎‎"Create a clean and eye-catching image for social media using the attached product.
‎The composition should feel organic and understated, not sales-oriented.
‎Use a clear visual hierarchy, natural lighting, and modern framing.
‎Do not add text, badges, or exaggerated effects.
‎The image should convey quality and clarity at a glance."

‎6. Before-and-After Visual Comparison
‎
‎Prompt:
‎‎"Create a before-and-after visual comparison demonstrating the effect of using the attached product. Divide the image into two balanced sections. Ensure consistent lighting, perspective, and setting across both states. The transformation should be evident without exaggeration."
