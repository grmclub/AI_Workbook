ChatGPT keep changing your face when you change your outfit or background? 😡 Try this "Identity Lock" prompting technique 👇

1/ Professional Headshot
Turn a casual photo into a headshot
Upload a clear original photo, then prompt:
"Transform this into a professional headshot.
Preserve the subject’s facial identity and natural features, including face shape, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, skin texture, hairline, and apparent age.
Only change the background, lighting, framing, color grading, and clothing.
Do not intentionally beautify, reshape, or smooth the face."

2/ Cinematic Portrait
Change the style, not the face
"Transform this into a cinematic portrait with an 85mm lens look, shallow depth of field, and dramatic studio lighting.
Use the original photo as the primary identity reference.
Preserve the facial features, expression, apparent age, hairstyle, and natural skin details.
Only change the lighting, background, composition, and color grading."

3/ Change the Outfit
Keep everything else consistent
"Change only the subject’s clothing to [OUTFIT].
Preserve the face, expression, body proportions, pose, hands, hair, camera angle, and background.
Make the new clothing match the existing body position, lighting, shadows, and perspective.
Avoid unnecessary changes to the person."

 ·
4/ Swap the Background
Put yourself somewhere new
"Replace only the background with [LOCATION].
Keep the subject’s face, body, clothing, pose, expression, and hair as consistent with the original as possible.
Match the new environment’s perspective, depth, lighting, and color temperature to the subject.
Avoid unnecessary facial changes."

5/ Fix a Changed Face
Already lost your likeness? Try this
Upload:
Image 1: Original photo
Image 2: Edited photo
Then prompt:
"Use Image 1 as the primary reference for the subject’s facial identity.
Use Image 2 as the reference for the outfit, lighting, style, and environment.
Recreate the edited image while making the face more consistent with Image 1.
Preserve the other requested changes from Image 2."

6/ Run an Identity Check
One final prompt before you're done
"Compare the edited image with the original reference.
Check the face shape, apparent age, eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, skin details, and hairline for unintended changes.
Correct noticeable differences that were not requested while preserving the intended edit."

NOTE: One Important Thing
No prompt can perfectly lock a face every time.
For more consistent results:
• Start with a sharp reference photo
• Make one major edit at a time
• Avoid changing everything at once
• Keep your original photo as the reference
• Compare the final result with the original
