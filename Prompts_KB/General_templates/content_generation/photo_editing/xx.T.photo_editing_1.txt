That’s why your AI edits look fake.

1. 🎬 Cinematic Portrait Treatment
Give my photo a premium cinematic treatment. Preserve my exact face, identity, proportions, expression, and clothing. Enhance the lighting, contrast, color grading, and atmosphere with a realistic, camera-shot finish. Keep the result natural and avoid any artificial or overly stylized look.

2. 📸 DSLR Look Upgrade
Make my photo look like it was captured with a professional full-frame DSLR camera. Keep my face, body, expression, clothing, and identity exactly unchanged. Add realistic depth of field, natural skin texture, subtle film grain, and balanced exposure. Do not beautify, reshape, smooth, or alter my features.

3. 🖤 Luxury Editorial Style
Transform my photo into a high-end luxury editorial portrait. Preserve my exact facial structure, body proportions, identity, and natural skin texture. Refine the lighting, composition, contrast, and background separation to create a sophisticated magazine-quality result without making it look artificial.

5. 👔 Clean Professional Headshot
Turn my photo into a polished professional headshot. Keep my face, hair, expression, identity, and natural skin texture unchanged. Use soft, realistic lighting, balanced exposure, and subtle background blur for a clean studio-quality finish. No beautification, face reshaping, excessive smoothing, or artificial retouching.

4. 🌅 Golden Hour Outdoor Edit
Transform my photo into a realistic golden-hour portrait. Keep my identity, face, body, clothing, and natural features exactly the same. Add warm natural sunlight, soft realistic shadows, balanced skin tones, and subtle depth of field. Avoid artificial glow, excessive warmth, oversaturation, or an obvious AI look.

6. 🏙️ Urban Street Photography
Transform my photo into a realistic professional urban street portrait. Preserve my exact face, identity, body proportions, expression, and clothing. Enhance the natural lighting, shadows, colors, and background while adding realistic depth of field and subtle camera grain. Keep the scene authentic, cinematic, and natural—no face reshaping, artificial skin, or exaggerated effects.
