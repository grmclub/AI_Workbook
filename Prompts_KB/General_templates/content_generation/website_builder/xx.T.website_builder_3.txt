🚨 Gemini can build your entire website in one afternoon.
No coding. No designer. No developer.
10 powerful prompts to make your site look $5,000+ 👇

1. The Ultimate Website Blueprint
“Act as a senior web strategist. Plan a high-converting website for my [business] in [industry/location]. Define the essential pages, navigation, content hierarchy, conversion strategy, and ideal visitor journey. Keep it strategic, simple, and beginner-friendly.”

2. The High-Converting Homepage
“Act as a conversion-focused web designer. Build the ideal homepage structure for my [business]. Include the hero, trust signals, services, benefits, testimonials, FAQs, and CTAs. Explain the purpose of each section and make the layout clean, persuasive, and easy to navigate.”

3. The Persuasive Homepage Copy
“Act as a direct-response copywriter. Write high-converting homepage copy for my [business] targeting [audience]. Create a powerful headline, subheadline, CTA, three customer-focused benefits, and a trust-building closing section. Keep it clear, concise, and persuasive.”

4. The Professional Website Design System
“Act as a senior UI/UX designer. Create a premium design system for my [business] website. Define the color palette with HEX codes, font pairings, buttons, spacing, visual hierarchy, and overall style. Make it modern, cohesive, and easy for a beginner to implement.”

5. The Ready-to-Launch Landing Page
“Act as a senior front-end developer. Build a fully responsive landing page for my [business] using clean HTML and CSS. Include a compelling hero, services/features, testimonials, and CTA. Make the code semantic, accessible, mobile-friendly, easy to edit, and production-ready.”

6. The High-Converting Services Page
“Act as a conversion copywriter. Write a high-converting services page for my [business] offering [services]. For each service, create a compelling title, concise description, ideal customer, and clear outcome. Focus on results, clarity, and customer value—not generic features.”


7. The Trust-Building About Page
“Act as a brand storyteller. Write an authentic About page for my [brand/business]. Include the origin story, mission, values, differentiators, and reasons customers should trust us. Make it human, confident, relatable, and memorable without sounding corporate.”

8. The SEO Content Engine
“Act as an expert SEO strategist. Create search-optimized content for my [page] targeting [keyword/topic]. Write a compelling title, logical headings, concise paragraphs, and natural keyword placement. Prioritize search intent, readability, usefulness, and strong organic visibility—without keyword stuffing.”

9. The Website UX Upgrade
“Act as a senior UX auditor. Analyze my [website/website idea] and identify the biggest opportunities to improve navigation, layout, hierarchy, CTAs, readability, and mobile usability. Give me practical, prioritized fixes I can implement immediately.”

10. The Code Upgrade System
“Act as a senior front-end engineer. Review my website code and improve its structure, responsiveness, accessibility, performance, and maintainability. Fix errors, remove unnecessary code, and return the improved version with a simple explanation of every major upgrade.”

## Claude can map out your entire brand identity, visual direction, and logo concepts without hiring anyone. Steal these 7 prompts 👇👇

1. The Brand Foundation
Act as my brand strategist. My business is [what you do], my audience is [who they are], and I want to feel [3 words: e.g. bold, warm, premium]. Give me my brand's core vibe, personality, and the feeling someone should get in 3 seconds.

2. The Color Palette
Based on my brand [paste vibe], give me a color palette: 2 main colors, 2 accents, and a neutral. Give me the hex codes and explain what each color says about my brand so it's intentional, not random.

3. The Font Direction
Recommend 3 font pairings for my brand [paste vibe]. One heading font, one body font per pairing. Tell me where to find each one free and what mood each pairing gives off.

4. The Logo Concepts
Give me 5 logo concepts for [business name], niche [niche], vibe [paste]. For each, describe the style, the icon or symbol idea, and why it fits. Written so I can hand it to a designer or drop it into an image tool.

5. The Tagline
Write me 10 tagline options for [business name]. Short, memorable, says who I help or the transformation I create. Mix punchy and emotional. No corporate jargon.

6. The Visual Direction
Build me a simple brand style guide: my colors, fonts, tagline, and the overall look and feel of my content. Include 5 rules to keep everything consistent across Instagram, so my page looks like one brand.

7. The Canva Brief
Turn all of this into a step-by-step brief I can follow in Canva to build my logo and brand templates myself. List exactly what to create and in what order, assuming I'm a total beginner.
