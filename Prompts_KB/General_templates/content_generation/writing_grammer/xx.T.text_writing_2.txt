1/ Turn a Rough Draft Into Strong Copy
Prompt: Take the draft below and rebuild it into clear, confident, compelling copy. Replace vague language with specific wording, eliminate repetition, tighten weak sentences, and use active voice. Preserve the original ideas while making the writing sound polished and credible.

2/ Give It a Professional Editorial Pass
Prompt: Edit the following piece as an experienced professional editor. Correct grammar and punctuation, improve awkward phrasing, strengthen transitions, and fix structural problems. Prioritize clarity and readability without making the writing sound overly formal. After editing, list the 5 changes that made the biggest difference.
Draft: [Paste your writing]

7/ Make the Argument More Convincing
Prompt: Strengthen this piece without resorting to hype or manipulation. Clarify the main argument, emphasize the most meaningful benefits, address obvious objections where useful, and improve the order of ideas. End with a natural next step for the reader.
Draft: [Paste your writing]
 
3/ Make a Complex Idea Easy to Understand
Prompt: Rewrite the following content for an intelligent reader who is unfamiliar with the subject. Replace unnecessary jargon, simplify complicated sentences, and explain difficult concepts in plain language. Do not remove important nuance or meaning.
Draft: [Paste your writing]
 
4/ Make the Writing Feel More Human
Prompt: Rewrite this piece so it feels natural, relatable, and written by a real person. Add personality, concrete details, and subtle emotional elements where they genuinely improve the message. Avoid clichés, exaggerated claims, and artificial-sounding language.
Draft: [Paste your writing]
 
5/ Create 3 Distinct Versions
Prompt: Rewrite the following piece three different ways without changing its core message:
▪︎Clear and professional
▪︎Casual and conversational
▪︎Bold and energetic
Make each version genuinely different in rhythm, vocabulary, and sentence structure rather than simply swapping a few words.
Draft: [Paste your writing]
 
6/ Cut the Fluff
Prompt: Edit this piece aggressively for concision. Remove repetition, filler, unnecessary qualifiers, weak openings, and sentences that don't move the message forward. Reduce the length by roughly one-third while preserving the key information and original intent.
Draft: [Paste your writing]
 
7/ Make the Argument More Convincing
Prompt: Strengthen this piece without resorting to hype or manipulation. Clarify the main argument, emphasize the most meaningful benefits, address obvious objections where useful, and improve the order of ideas. End with a natural next step for the reader.
Draft: [Paste your writing]

8/ Diagnose the Writing Before Rewriting It
Prompt: Analyze the following piece like a demanding writing coach. Rate it from 1–10 for clarity, structure, originality, readability, and persuasiveness. Identify the 3 biggest problems holding it back, explain how to fix each one, then provide a revised version incorporating your recommendations.
Draft: [Paste your writing]
 
9/ Fix the Flow
Prompt: Rework this piece so each idea leads naturally into the next. Improve transitions, vary sentence length, remove abrupt jumps, and rearrange paragraphs if necessary. The finished version should feel effortless to read from the opening sentence to the final line.
Draft: [Paste your writing]

10/ Optimize It for People Who Skim
Prompt: Rewrite this content for someone reading quickly on a phone. Put the strongest information first, shorten dense sentences, break up large paragraphs, and make key points immediately visible. Keep the substance intact while making the piece easy to scan and understand quickly.
Draft: [Paste your writing]

## 6 prompts for better writing

1. "Rewrite this in a way that is clearer, more persuasive, and more engaging without changing the core message: [text]."

2. "Act as a professional editor. Critique this piece and suggest specific improvements for clarity, flow, and impact: [text]."

3. "Write this in my voice. Here are examples of my writing style: [examples]. Maintain my tone, vocabulary, and sentence structure."

4. "Create 10 different hooks for this topic optimized for curiosity, attention, and engagement: [topic]."

5. "Analyze this content and explain where readers might lose interest, get confused, or stop reading: [content]."

6. "Turn these ideas into a compelling piece with a strong opening, clear structure, and memorable conclusion: [ideas]."

Most people ask AI to write.Great writers use AI to:
• sharpen ideas
• strengthen structure
• improve clarity
