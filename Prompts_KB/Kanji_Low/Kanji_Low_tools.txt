## claude levels 

Level 1 — Chat Mode Using Claude like Google. Ask a question, get an answer. Copy, paste, done. 90% of people never leave this level.

Level 2 — Prompted Claude Writing proper prompts with role, context, format, and constraints. Getting consistent, usable outputs instead of generic replies.

Level 3 — Workflow Claude Using Claude inside repeatable business processes — content creation, client emails, proposals, SOPs. Claude becomes a system, not a search bar.

Level 4 — Connected Claude Claude with MCP tools, browser access, file systems, and integrations. It's not just answering — it's doing. Reading, writing, searching, acting.

Level 5 — Agentic Claude (Claude Code) Claude builds and runs its own systems. Full apps, automations, and agents deployed from a single conversation. Most people don't know this exists.
