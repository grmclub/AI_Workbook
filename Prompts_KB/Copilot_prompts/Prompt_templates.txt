--Prompt templates

For xx team and yy application. Generate 10 FAQs: 1) Mix basic and advanced, 2) Cover benefits/costs/how-to/concerns, 3) Draft clear answers (2-3 sentences each), 4) Flag need for detail

--Mail-1
Analyze all emails I received in the last 30 days. Create a full intelligence briefing including:
1) Key actors and recurring senders
2) Communication tone by sender (supportive, neutral, demanding)
3) Topic clusters and themes
4) Unresolved threads requiring action
5) Projects that appear across multiple emails
6) Status score for each project (on track, at risk, blocked)
7) People I need to engage with more frequently

Format as an executive briefing.

--Mail-2
Predict which email topics are likely to become future problems based on patterns in the last 30 days. For each potential issue: 1) Issue description, 2) Warning indicators observed, 3) Likelihood score (1-10), 4) Potential impact if not addressed, 5) Recommended mitigation strategies, 6) Who should be involved in prevention, 7) Timeline for preventive action. Prioritize by risk score (likelihood × impact).

--Data Analysis-1
Analyze this dataset and tell the data story:
1) Key trends over time
2) Notable outliers or anomalies with potential explanations
3) Correlations or relationships between variables
4) Segments or clusters in the data
5) Unexpected findings
6) Business implications of each insight
7) Recommended actions based on analysis

Create visualizations for top 3 insights and provide narrative explanations suitable for presentation.

--Troubleshooting Guide

--Workload Forecast Generator
Based on the last 60 days of emails, predict my workload for next month including: 1) Expected email volume by week, 2) Likely deadlines and time commitments, 3) Task clusters (related work items), 4) High-pressure weeks (crunch times), 5) Meetings and collaborative work load, 6) Personal bandwidth vs. expected demand. Suggest pacing strategy and workflow optimization recommendations to balance load.

--Email Battle Plan Generator
Based on the last 7 days of emails, generate a personal battle plan with: 1) Top 5 priorities ranked by urgency and impact, 2) Emerging threats or issues to watch, 3) Overdue tasks and their consequences, 4) Tasks with unclear ownership, 5) Tasks likely to escalate if not addressed, 6) Recommended action sequence with time estimates, 7) Delegation opportunities. Format as a tactical execution plan with timelines.
